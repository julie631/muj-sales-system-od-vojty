# Tone Control + The "Why Behind the Behavior"

## Context
Voss + Troy Smith Fireside coaching call. Topic: how to stay regulated during attacks AND how to de-escalate someone who is themselves under attack.

## Core thesis
1. **Tone moments = rep opportunities** for self-improvement.
2. **The attack is never about you** — find the why behind the behavior.

## Tool 1: WAIT + Stay Curious [00:00]
- WAIT = **W**hy **A**m **I** **T**alking?
- Combined with: **stay curious.**
- "I will literally, as the person might be saying [something inflammatory], and I feel my tone is going to change — 'wait and remain curious.'"
- "Has worked extremely well for me every time I am under some sort of attack."

## Tool 2: Find the Why [01:01]
- "Normally the attack is not against you. It's something else in their world that hasn't been resolved."
- "It's an issue that hasn't been resolved or pressure that they're under. You just happen to be the sounding board."
- "Why am I getting upset?" — also turn it on yourself.

## Case Study 1: Jet Blue Baggage [04:02]
- Baggage department was being attacked constantly when bags were lost.
- Training takeaway: it's not about the bag.
- Could be wedding + special equipment + time-critical pressure.
- "What is that event? What is that stress point?"
- Once they listened for the underlying stress, they could solve THAT (e.g., $X stipend to buy needed clothes immediately).

## Case Study 2: Troy's Airport Story [06:30]
- Late flight cancellation. LaGuardia shut down. Crowd attacking the gate agent.
- "Sir, I'm going to have to stay overnight, give me a hotel room." She: "We don't have any more passes." Passenger: "It figures."
- Troy waits his turn. Approaches with smile.
- Label: **"Man, seems like you're having the worst possible day ever."**
- She: "These people act like it's my fault."
- Troy: "Seems like they put you on an island out here." (low voice, calm)
- He could see her shoulders drop.
- Result: she finds a flight (Baltimore, 3 seats). Offers him a hotel voucher unprompted (which earlier she had said she didn't have).
- Why she helped: "Because of his tone and the way he was treating her."

## Tonality Matters at Every Interaction [05:03]
- "When someone is attacking us because of a bag... what is that event, that stress point? If we can help the person resolve that stressing point... they will concentrate more on the listening part to understand what's the reason behind the attack and then provide solutions directly to that reason."

## CZ adaptation notes
- WAIT CZ: pojmenovat jako **"POČKEJ"** (Proč Otevírám Co Kdy Eskaluješ Já?) — nebo prostě používat WAIT v angličtině jako vnitřní heuristiku.
- "Seems like you're having the worst day" CZ: **"Vypadá to, že jste dnes zažil úplně nejhorší den."** (calm, low voice, dynamic silence).
- Pro tebe: critical for sales calls where prospect arrives stressed/grumpy (např. fyzioterapeutka mezi dvěma pacienty). Don't take the brusqueness personally — label it: "Vypadá to, že máte teď fakt narváno."
- Underlying need framing: owner on phone snaps "Já teď nemám čas." → not real refusal, real issue is "you're a sales call interrupting my flow." Label it: "Vidim, že vás teď otravuju ve špatnou chvíli."
