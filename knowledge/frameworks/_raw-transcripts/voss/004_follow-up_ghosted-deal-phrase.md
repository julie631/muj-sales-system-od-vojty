# The One Phrase That Revives Ghosted Deals

## Context
Voss reveals the highest-leverage follow-up phrase he teaches. Tested at the 2025 Negotiation Mastery Summit — only 4 attendees did the homework and all 4 reported the SAME outcome: dead 7-figure deals closed.

## Core thesis
Stop chasing. Stop "just checking in." Send ONE phrase and shut up.

## THE Phrase

**"Have you given up on this deal?"**

That's it. Nothing before. Nothing after.

### What NOT to do [02:03]
- ❌ "Hi, how are you, just checking in, wanted to see if you had any updates."
- ❌ Sweetening the pot, discounts.
- ❌ "Have you given up on this deal? Because I haven't, so just in case, get back to me when you get a chance."
- ✅ Just the phrase. Nothing else. In person or text.

## Why it works [03:04]
**Two possible responses:**
1. They respond within 3-5 min: "No, I haven't given up. Let's find a solution."
2. They respond: "Sorry, we've moved on."

**Either way = win.**
- Either you close → make money.
- Or you finally move on from worrying about a dead deal.

## The psychology [04:05]
- **Loss aversion** is at least 2x as powerful as gain.
- "More decisions are made to avoid a loss or to avoid being fired than it is to accomplish a gain or get promoted. It's basically a fear-driven world."
- The phrase doesn't push them to "yes." It gives them **ownership, safety, security, control** — the conditions for clear decisions.
- "When people feel ownership, they fight to keep it."
- It also flips the dynamic: "You're not chasing. You're calmly signaling it's okay to walk away. But are you okay with walking away? That's power. That's real influence. That's tactical empathy."

## Why "chasing" kills you [02:33]
- "Chasing is not negotiating. It smells like desperation. And desperation kills deals."
- "Do you date somebody that was desperate? Why? Because you're trying to sell instead of triggering a decision."

## Pro tebe — aplikace
- Máš-li follow-up kadenci o několika krocích, tahle věta = ideální pro **FU #4 nebo #5**, když běžné re-engagement nezabralo.
- Použij ji na leady, které ti v pipeline uvízly — buď to urychlí closing, nebo vyčistí backlog.

## CZ adaptation notes
- "Have you given up on this deal?" → CZ: **"Hodili jste to za hlavu?"** or **"Nechali jste to být?"** or **"Zabalili jste to?"**
- ⚠️ KRITICKÉ: **nepřidávat NIC** před ani za. Žádné "ahoj", žádné "promiňte". Jen ta věta.
- Forma: SMS/WhatsApp/hlasovka. Hlasovka silnější (osobnější), text rychlejší.
- Tone: NE výčitka, NE smutek. **Klidný, neutrální, autonomy-respecting.**
