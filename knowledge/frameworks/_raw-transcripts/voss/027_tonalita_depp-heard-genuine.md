# Camille Vasquez — Strategies from the Depp Trial

## Context
Voss interviews Camille Vasquez (attorney from Depp v. Heard). Several Q&A topics covered with practical takeaways for team alignment, provocation, and getting reticent people to open up.

## Q1: Team Alignment on Communication Style [00:00]
- "It takes a lot of dispute resolution. You have to test and try every single person's communication style on a particular question."
- Show why your approach is better — don't tell.
- Depp-Heard case: "We tested every single question with different styles, different tones, and different potential answers."
- "Show and tell. You see live what could go wrong when you push too hard on a particular topic vs what could go really right when you push just enough."

## Q2: Provocation Response [01:17]
- "If the counterpart uses a rude, disrespectful, provocative or aggressive tone..."
- "I think you should always try to ignore the provocation, because it's not going to further your goal."
- "Don't respond in kind."
- Give them a bit of GRACE. Voss: "Amen."

## Q3: Adapting Style to Female Counterpart [02:01]
- Doesn't matter who's on the other side.
- "Be genuine and authentic to your style irrespective of who's on the other side."
- After doing the research. "Gender doesn't necessarily play a role."

## Q4: Being Authentic Without Sounding Funny [03:01]
- "It's about taking that extra step. Give them the platform to speak — even if it's 3-5 minutes."
- Even if you'll be late to your next meeting, it's worth it.
- "It's about making them feel heard. Doesn't mean you're going to agree."

### The 5-Minute Signal Technique [04:01]
- **"I'm going to be 5 minutes late to my meeting, but it's worth it because I want to hear you out."**
- "That will make such an impact."
- "They know they can come to you. You're going to take 5 minutes out of your schedule as busy as you are to listen."
- "It's that signaling that makes a difference."

## Q5: Getting Introverts to Open Up [05:25]
- "Finding out what they care about."
- "It could be something really simple — like they really like to go fishing."
- "Once you find that out, you're going to get trust."
- "Trust is what builds communication. Then they're going to really love to talk to you about fishing. Then you sneak in the questions that really matter to you."
- "Even the most introverted person cares about something — and would love to chat with you about that."

## CZ adaptation notes
- Test & try CZ for you + your team: po každém ztraceném sales callu udělat retro — co mělo fungovat? Co kdyby tón byl jiný? Show & tell uvnitř týmu.
- Provocation ignore CZ: na cold outreach kdy někdo "Nezajímá mě to, dej mi pokoj" → IGNORE, label místo toho ("Vidím, že jsem zavolala v nevhodnou chvíli."). Don't react.
- 5-min signal CZ: **"Mám další meeting za 5 minut, ale stojí to za to — pojďme to dotáhnout."** = signal důležitosti.
- Introvert opener CZ: find what the owner loves (atmosféra jeho provozovny? Old-school traditions řemesla? Specific clientela?) → ask about THAT → trust → real business Q's.
- "Be authentic, don't change for them" → critical. Don't adapt persona based on prospect. Same you, every call.
