# Verbal Attacks — Stay Curious + I-Message

## Context
George Mumford (Black Swan team) coaching call. Topic: how to stay regulated during attacks AND when to confront persistent manipulators.

## Rule #1: The 45-Second Rule [00:01]
- "Stay curious during the attack."
- "Unless you're dealing with a sociopath... 45 seconds to a minute of dynamic silence, let the air out of the balloon."
- "Unless you're dealing with someone suffering from mental illness, they're not going to be able to sustain an attack if you're not fighting back."
- During silence, ask yourself: "What would cause them to say that or do that?"

## The 3 Sources of Attack

### Source 1: You're Not Listening [01:01]
- From their perspective, you've missed something.
- Like a 3-4 year old tugging at your pants while you ignore them → eventually melts down.
- Ask yourself: **"What am I missing?"**

### Source 2: They're Under Pressure You Haven't Acknowledged [02:03]
- Labels that clear log jams:
- **"It sounds like the people on your side are making your life extremely difficult."**
- **"It feels like they expect you to bring this in well under budget."**
- **"It seems like you're in a fishbowl right now and everybody is scrutinizing your performance."**

### Source 3: Manipulation [03:03]
- They KNOW attacks make you uncomfortable.
- Track record: making people uncomfortable → they acquiesce, concede.
- You want comfort restored, so you start giving away stuff.
- Stay in the moment. Determine which one it is.

## When to Confront (Persistent Manipulators) [04:06]
- If you label pressure / fall on your sword, that usually defuses sources 1 & 2.
- Manipulators DON'T back off. You may need to confront them.

## The I-Message (Black Swan Version) [05:07]
- Old formula: "I feel ___, when you ___, because ___."
- "We wanted to focus on the OTHER side. Make it unequivocal this is coming from YOU."
- Inverted: **"When you ___, I feel ___, because ___."**

### The Crucial "Because" Upgrade [06:09]
- Old "because" = your emotion: "because I feel insulted."
- People didn't care. "He said 'so I don't care' because he didn't have anything to lose."
- **New "because" = WHAT THEY STAND TO LOSE if they don't stop.**

### Example
- "When you continue to throw out ultimatums, I feel backed into a corner because you're limiting the options we have in moving forward."
- Reframes their behavior as costing them options.

## When to Deploy [07:09]
- "Persistent unproductive behavior" — not first time someone yells.
- "If it happens multiple times where it's disrupting the flow of productive dialogue, then you have to say something."
- "Failure to address counterproductive behavior when it rears its head impedes your ability to influence."
- "Used sparingly. This is a very assertive move. Great for threats, demands, ultimatums."

## CZ adaptation notes
- I-Message CZ: **"Když [opakovaně dělají X], cítím [Y], protože [co tím ztrácí]."**
- Example CZ: "Když pořád stupňujete tlak na rychlou odpověď, cítím se zatlačená do kouta, protože tím omezujete varianty, jak to vyřešit ve váš prospěch."
- 45-sec rule CZ: silně užitečné na zoom callech kdy prospect vybuchne — counter "jeden tisíc, dva tisíc..." až do 45.
- Sources of attack CZ labels:
  - "Vypadá to, že vás kolem tlačí, abyste to dotáhli."
  - "Vidím, že jste teď pod docela velkým tlakem."
  - "Vypadá to, že to vedení chce pod každou cenu vyladit."
- Pro tebe: critical when negotiating with frustrated existing client whose delivery broke. Don't fight back — 45-sec silence, then label source 2 (their pressure).
