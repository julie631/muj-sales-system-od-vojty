# Negotiation Myths That Kill Your Sales

## Context
Short hype-style YouTube video. Voss frames negotiation around 5 myths that "tank your deals, stall your career, and ruin relationships." Distilled, fast-paced version of his core methodology.

## Core thesis
"You stop negotiating like a needy amateur and start negotiating like a Black Swan" when you flip 5 myths.

## The 5 Myths

**[00:00]** **Myth 1: Get them to say YES.**
- The myth: goal = yes ASAP.
- Voss: "Yes is a trap. People say yes to get rid of you."
- Red flags of fast yes: no authority, placating you, setting you up to fail with their team.
- Fix: **No-oriented questions** that make people feel protected and in control.
  - "Is now a bad time to talk?"
  - "Would it be ridiculous for us to explore a better way forward?"

**[01:00]** **Myth 2: Start with common ground.**
- The myth: build rapport via shared hobbies.
- Voss: "When someone's skeptical, nervous, or even hostile, they don't care if you both like skiing in Vail."
- Fix: Build trust by **showing you understand their pain** — tactical empathy.
- Lead with **Accusation Audit**: "You're probably thinking this is just another sales call. It might feel like I'm pushing an agenda here."

**[02:01]** **Myth 3: State your value proposition (elevator pitch).**
- The myth: lead with value, tell them why they need you.
- Voss: "You think you're helping, they think you're selling. In high-stakes negotiations, the more you talk, the more they distrust you."
- Fix: Let them **discover the value** via calibrated questions.
  - "How does this line up with what you're trying to solve?"
  - "What's the biggest obstacle you're facing right now?"

**[03:02]** **Myth 4: Control the conversation.**
- The myth: whoever controls = wins. Talk talk talk.
- Voss: that puts you in the "agreement graveyard."
- Fix: Give them the **illusion of control** via strategic silence + listening.
- "Get a PhD in shut up. Silence not just forces their thinking, but creates a space for them to think... they can't resist filling that silence. That's where the gold is."

**[05:04]** **Myth 5: Fairness wins the day.**
- "Fair is the most weaponized word in negotiation."
- When someone says "I just want what's fair" = "I want what benefits me and I want you to feel bad if you disagree."
- Fix: Anchor in **vision and loss** instead.
  - "What happens if we don't solve this?"
  - "How will this affect your team if it stays like this?"
- Loss aversion: "People act to avoid pain more than to gain pleasure."

## Recap [06:04]
- Don't chase yes → chase truth via no.
- Don't pitch value → discover problems.
- Don't lead with logic → lead with empathy.
- Don't control → influence.
- Don't play the fairness card → ask better questions.
