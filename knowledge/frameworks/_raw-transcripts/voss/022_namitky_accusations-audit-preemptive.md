# Accusations Audit — Pre-empt the Negatives

## Context
Black Swan team coaching session. Topic: preemptive labeling, AKA the Accusations Audit. Why labeling NEGATIVES upfront is the most underrated skill.

## Reframe Negotiation [01:02]
- Take "negotiation" out of the Lexicon. Replace with "difficult conversation."
- "Do difficult conversations make you uncomfortable?"
- "When you're uncomfortable, what do you want to do more than anything? Get comfortable again."
- "What's the fastest way to get comfortable? Solve the problem as quickly as possible."
- **"The sooner I cut my price, the sooner I get out of the room."**
- This is why negotiations feel bad — and why people cave too fast.

## Negative Labels = 3x Power [02:03]
- Black Swan initially thought labels were the easiest skill = not most important. Wrong.
- **"Labeling the negative is three times as powerful as labeling a positive."**
- Going after negatives = faster results.

## The Accusations Audit [03:04]
- "A list of opinions, assumptions, and impressions that the other side has about you, your circumstance, the company you represent, the environment."
- Could be professional OR personal.
- Could be fair OR unfair, sane OR insane.
- "If they were to stand on the street corner and yell at the top of their lungs about you, what would you want to deny?"

## Prep Questions Before a Hard Conversation [04:06]
- How is what you're going to ask going to create issues for them?
- How have they viewed others in your space?
- How do they view your company?
- How do they view you as a man? As a woman?
- **What would you want to deny? How would you deny it? → By LABELING it.**

## Why Do It at the Start [05:08]
- "We do it at the beginning so they don't throw it back in your face later."
- "We snatch permission and authority away from the other side for using the negative against us in the course of the conversation."

## Real-World Testimonial [06:10]
- Travis (audience): used Accusations Audit when team was questioning a project.
- He opened with: "You guys might think I don't know what I'm doing here. You might think I'm not experienced enough. You might think I'm taking this in the wrong direction."
- After the meeting, each team member individually came back: "I didn't think any of those things. I actually think you're doing a really good job."

## The "Matrix Moment" Principle [07:11]
- "You don't know how deep their negatives go when it pertains to you."
- "You got to follow them down the rabbit hole."
- Plan for deeper negatives than surface assumes.

## CZ adaptation notes
- Accusations Audit CZ prep template:
  - "Pravděpodobně si říkáte, že [negative #1]."
  - "Možná vám příjde, že [negative #2]."
  - "Asi si myslíte, že [negative #3]."
- For cold outreach CZ AA:
  - "Pravděpodobně si říkáš, že tohle je další firma, která ti chce něco prodat."
  - "Asi máš pocit, že už jsi podobný systém viděl 100x."
  - "Možná si říkáš, jestli to vůbec funguje na CZ trhu."
- For sales call opening: 30-60s AA shortcuts past resistance. Script & practice this.
- "Difficult conversation" reframe CZ: prospects often say "moc se mi nechce volat" → reframe pro sebe jako "není to call, je to obtížná konverzace; uvolním si do ní mentálně".
