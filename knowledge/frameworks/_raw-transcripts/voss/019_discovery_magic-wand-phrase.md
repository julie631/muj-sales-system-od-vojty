# Magic Wand Phrase #1 — "It Seems Like You Have a Reason"

## Context
First in a 10-part series on Black Swan's "magic wand phrases." This one: "It seems like you have a reason for saying that."

## The Phrase
**"It seems like you have a reason for saying that."**

## What It Does [01:01]
- Invites elaboration on their position.
- Whether you agree or disagree, you need to know the UNDERLYING motivations.
- "It tells them: 'I'm interested in understanding you.' Which accomplishes the objective of: interesting people are interested — even charming."

## The Critical Followup: Dynamic Silence [01:23]
- After you say the phrase, **BE SILENT**.
- "Look at them thoughtfully, expectantly, like you know they have great thoughts to share."
- "Count thousands to yourself if you have to. Just resist the urge to fill the silence."

## The Awkwardness Principle [02:02]
- "If you feel awkward doing this, that's a good thing."
- "Awkwardness is an indicator of accelerated learning."
- **"Embrace it. Hug the cactus. Find out that you can liberate yourself from your amygdala."**
- "By embracing awkwardness, you'll become more comfortable and confident."

## Why This Matters [02:35]
- "No one will collaborate with you willingly until they feel heard and respected."
- "You need their collaboration to get what you want."
- "Life is a team sport. You're building rapport and creating trust."

## The 4 + 64 Rule [02:54]
- **Try this 4 times today** — to get the feel of it. After 4 reps you have enough data for gut feel.
- **Then do it 64 times** — to wire it in. Becomes automatic.

## Followup [03:20]
- After dynamic silence, **summarize** them.
- Keep at it until you get a **"That's right."**
- Then **wait** for good things to happen.
- **Don't share common ground.** "It's not about you. Make a day of this tactical empathy day."

## The Output [03:52]
- "What you will find is a day of people opening up to you in ways you haven't seen before."

## Bruce Lee Quote [04:03]
- "I fear not the man who has practiced 10,000 kicks once, but I fear the man who has practiced one kick 10,000 times."

## CZ adaptation notes
- Magic wand CZ: **"Vypadá to, že pro to máte/máš nějaký důvod."**
- After saying it: **TICHO**. Žádné "že jo?" nebo "souhlasíš?" — to ruší kouzlo.
- Daily practice: 4x ráno (s ranní kávou v týmu / s partnerem), pak v rámci toho dne dalších 60. Do týdne = wiring complete.
- "Hug the cactus" = CZ "kaktus mi přijde milý" — Voss's reframe of awkwardness as data, not failure.
- Don't share common ground = NEpřidávat "to chápu, mně se to taky děje" → krade focus z nich.
