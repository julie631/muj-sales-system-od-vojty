# 5 Secret Deal Killers (and How to Stop Them)

## Context
Direct YouTube short-form video. Voss as "CEO of Black Swan Group" pulling back the curtain on hidden traps. Each killer = 1 problem + 1 phrase + 1 action.

## Core thesis
"Most deals don't die because of what's said. They die because of what's not said."

## The 5 Killers

### 1. The Hidden Decision Maker [00:55]
- You think you're talking to DM, but someone in the shadows can kill the deal last-minute.
- **Fix**: Asking label. "It seems like someone else needs to feel comfortable with this before we move forward."
- "Simple, non-threatening. Powerful."

### 2. The Unspoken Budget Constraint [01:25]
- "They say price looks fine. You think you're good. You're not."
- "Most buyers won't admit they're overbudget until it's too late."
- **Fix**: Calibrated question + silence.
  - "How does this align with your budget goal for the quarter?"
  - Then **shut the front door**. Listen for hesitation.
- Label what you see: "It seems like something just crossed your mind." Label body language, facial affect.

### 3. The Unvoiced Fear [02:02]
- "People won't tell you they're scared. They'll ghost you instead."
- Fears: looking stupid, buying the wrong thing, getting fired, explaining your offer to their boss.
- **Fix**: Label it. "It seems like you might have some concerns about this working for you."
- **Six-second pause** (count "one-thousand, two-thousand..." up to six).
- If they don't speak: "What worries you most?" — gentle, not interrogating.

### 4. The Silent Competitor [03:25]
- "You're not the only one in the race, but you might be the only one not talking about it."
- "If you don't know who the fool in the game is, probably you."
- **Fix**: "It seems like you're still shopping around."
- "That's a fair label. Ask it nicely. Not like an accusation. Gently, with no judgment, no defensiveness. It's just the intel you need to win."

### 5. The Counterfeit Yes [04:04]
- 3 types of yes: **commitment, confirmation, counterfeit**.
- "The vast majority of yeses are counterfeit. They say yes just to get you off the phone."
- "Polite buyers are dangerous buyers."
- **Fix**: Don't go for yes — go for **"That's right."**
- How: summarize their situation. "Sounds like you're trying to achieve X but struggle with Y." Then **go silent**.
- When you get "That's right" → "you know you spoke the truth to them. And since they see you as a truth-teller, they're bought in with you."

## Closing principle
- "Your best weapon is tactical empathy."
- "Don't out-talk your customer. Out-listen."
- "Stay curious, find the black swan, go close your next deal."

## CZ adaptation notes
- Asking label CZ: "Vypadá to, jako by to ještě musel s někým probrat, než to pošlete dál."
- Budget CZ: "Jak tohle sedí s tím, co jste si pro tenhle kvartál naplánovali?" (then SHUT UP)
- Fear label CZ: "Vypadá to, že máte určité obavy, jestli vám to bude fungovat" → 6-second pause.
- Competitor CZ: "Mám pocit, že se ještě rozhlížíte." (gently, no judgment).
- "That's right" CZ: "Přesně tak."
- Hot for small-business sales: 5 of 5 killers happen in calls with owner-operators. Especially #1 (často je v rozhodování i partner/manželka/parťák) a #2 (rozpočet malých firem je často citlivá záležitost).
