# Power Dynamics Part 2 — Personality-Type Specific Plays

## Context
Continuation of voss-029 panel (Sandy, Derek, Troy on Black Swan team). Deeper dive into personality types under a power dynamic.

## Better Phrasing for Telling Them It's a Bad Idea [00:00]
- Don't say "this is going to fail miserably."
- Tools:
  - **Mirror** the end of their ask. They restate it. New ideas may surface.
  - **Label pointing toward loss**: "Seems like you've had some success with these techniques in the past..." or summary ending with "as a result, you feel some [risk] with this being successful long-term."
  - **Calibrated question**: "What happens if that option doesn't work?"

## The Universal Need Under Power [02:01]
- "When you get down to brass tacks, the powerful person's needs are no different than anyone else's. Just heightened."
- "They need to be HEARD, UNDERSTOOD, and RESPECTED. Just more elevated than with assertive person or analyst or accommodator."

## Assertive Type [03:01]
- "Will blow a deal up as long as you leave the room knowing and respecting their position, power, authority, intellect."
- "The deal can go to hell in a handbasket. As long as they've gotten that across to you, they're good with it."
- "These are the EASIEST to deal with because there's less I have to say."
- "Hit them with a decent label and they'll vomit up all of this information."
- "Mostly irrelevant, mostly telling you how great they are. But there will be nuggets within."

## CRITICAL: Closing the Assertive [05:03]
- Assertive comfortable with **yes without a how.**
- Don't disengage until you've hammered out the HOW.
- **"What and how them to death about implementation, next steps, what it's going to look like in the future."**
- Doing this raises negatives on their side → set it up with Accusations Audit + soften delivery.
- Ask what/how questions "as plaintively as possible" — encouraging them to help you because you don't get it.

## Analyst Type [09:06]
- "An analyst with power — they've got data and info on their side. You can't make them see anything else even if their data is wrong."
- "Like fighting an uphill battle with two flat tires."

## Accommodator with Power [09:06]
- A third variant — accommodator playing a powerful role.
- "A whole different animal."

## Diagnostic Reminder [09:06]
- Don't assume power = assertive. Verify before applying assertive playbook.

## Tool Recap [05:03]
- For assertive power: **label + ego label + mirror + calibrated Q**.
- Mirrors especially powerful — "we're not quiet assertive people, we just keep talking. When we breathe, you use that mirror and you send us where you want us to go."
- "You don't have to have control when dealing with an assertive — they'll run everything."
- "All you have to do is just guide them where you want them to go."

## Why Assertive is "Easy" [08:06]
- "If you're giving them the impression that 'I just don't know how this is going to work, I need you to really explain it to me,' they'll go right on into it."
- "They feel like you want to hear them talk more → they'll talk more."
- "They don't realize you're doing that on purpose to get the information you need."

## CZ adaptation notes
- Personality diagnosis CZ: u majitele v prvních 60 sek diagnostikuj typ:
  - **Asertivní** — mluví hodně, sebevědomý, projevuje sílu.
  - **Analytický** (data-driven owner) — ptá se na čísla, ROI, technické detaily.
  - **Akomodující** (lidi-pleaser owner) — "to zní hezky, ale...".
- Pro assertive close CZ: po "Jo, beru to" → KRITICKY: "Pojďme rozkrokovat, jak to udělame krok za krokem — máte preferenci, kdy se sejdeme na onboarding?" (what/how implementation questions).
- Pro analyst CZ: data first ("Tady je report — {tvoje case study s čísly}"), pak labels.
- Pro accommodator s power CZ: cuká se → label "Mám pocit, že chcete být zdvořilý, ale nejste si jistý."
