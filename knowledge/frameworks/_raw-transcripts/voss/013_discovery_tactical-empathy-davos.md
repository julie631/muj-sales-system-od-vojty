# Tactical Empathy at the World Economic Forum (Davos 2025)

## Context
Voss's 34-minute keynote at WEF Davos 2025. Most comprehensive single talk on tactical empathy in his YouTube catalog. Audience = global elite (CEOs, government officials, ambassadors).

## Opening Hook [00:48]
- Late-night FM DJ voice = downward inflicting voice slows the brain (neuroscience).
- Smile = involuntary reciprocation.
- **"31% smarter in a positive frame of mind"** (citing Shawn Achor's TED Talk).

## Indecent Redefinition of Empathy [02:02]
- "Empathy is the transmission of information. Only."
- NOT agreement. NOT disagreement. NOT sympathy. NOT compassion.
- The REACTION to empathy is often compassion: "You feel compassion from me. I don't have to have compassion for you, but you feel it from me."
- "No human being on Earth can resist compassion. Even sociopaths react to compassion."

## Cross-Cultural Universality [04:04]
- "Everybody just wants to know that you know where they're coming from." (Definition of empathy.)
- "How do you know that I know where you're coming from? I've got to say it out loud."
- Core emotions (Paul Ekman): anger, fear, disgust, happiness... 5-7 depending on academic.
- Born original equipment — universal. Feelings = core emotions interacting with life.
- "Empathy is so powerful, it's even effective with vegans."

## Magic Phrase #1: "What do you love about [X]?" [11:11]
- Powerful version of "small talk."
- Story: at Forest Whitaker fundraiser, Voss meets a "stereotypical entertainment LA male" he doesn't like.
- Voss: "What do you love about Los Angeles?" → man transformed, energized, opened up.
- WHY: opens "deep thinking portal" (Kahneman). Even when tired, deep thinking about love energizes them.
- **Story: water company CEO** → asked what he loves about being CEO. Answer: leading teams + giving shareholders return. Mission was 4th. Voss learned in seconds: "I could never work for him."
- "The test of your core values are: will you lose money over them?"

### What if they don't love it? [18:19]
- Lyft driver in Vegas. Hostile, doesn't help with bags. Voss feels rage spiral coming.
- Voss: "What do you love about driving for Lyft?"
- Driver opens up: working to support a grandson whose father abandoned them.
- "No matter what you ask anybody, they may not love where they are, but they will love WHY they're there."

### Dubai exercise [22:21]
- Disengaged participant. Voss: "What do you love about being in Dubai?"
- Reply: "Dubai is a polished piece of garbage." (Different word.)
- Conversation unfolded → Palestinian ethnicity, working to send money home. Voss learns his core values: loyalty, family, hard work, opportunity, freedom.

## Magic Phrase #2: "Seems like you have a reason for saying that." [23:22]
- Universal follow-up.
- "Find anyone today and ask 'What do you love about Donald Trump?' / 'I love him.' / 'Seems like you have a reason for saying that.'"
- Works regardless of opinion polarity.
- "Find the things the two of you can potentially agree on. Even if you don't agree, the act of being made to feel heard triggers compassion."

## The Filipino Al-Qaeda Hostage Story [27:24]
- Voss coached a negotiator in a kidnapping. American held by Al-Qaeda terrorist.
- Demand: $10M "ransom" reframed as "war damages for 500 years of oppression."
- Argument: Filipinos were really "Moros from a separate Moro Islamic homeland."
- Initial approach: debate, argue, counterpoint → nothing.
- Voss tells negotiator: **"You will summarize his facts, not ours. His."**
- Negotiator summarizes 500-years-of-colonial-oppression argument.
- Terrorist: **"That's right."**
- Ransom went from $10M to $0 in that moment.
- Subsequent kidnap twists/turns over coming months. NO RANSOM EVER MENTIONED AGAIN.
- Maundy Thursday (anniversary of Gethsemane), hostage simply walked away.
- 3 weeks later, terrorist called the negotiator: "Have you been promoted? I don't know what you said. I was going to kill the American. You stopped me. You should be promoted."

## The Closing Mandate [31:29]
- "Always leave people willing to speak to you again."
- "You will make a difference that will echo across the world."

## CZ adaptation notes
- **"What do you love about [X]?" CZ:** **"Co vás baví na...?"** / **"Co máte rád na...?"**
- For a sales call opening: **"Co tě baví nejvíc na tvé firmě?"** — instant rapport, deep portal opened, learns core values.
- **"Seems like you have a reason" CZ:** **"Vypadá to, že pro to máš nějaký důvod."**
- "Transmission of information" framing → critical to disabuse the "empathy = soft" misunderstanding. Empathy is a power tool, not weakness.
- Voss's empathy works "with vegans" too → translates to: works with skeptical CZ business owners regardless of how cold they start.
