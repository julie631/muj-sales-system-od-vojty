# Why Forcing a Yes is Killing Your Sales

## Context
Tight YouTube explainer on the dangers of yes-seeking and the no-oriented question replacement. Voss's most condensed version of this core principle.

## Core thesis
**"Yes is the most dangerous word in business and your sales are suffering for it."**

## Why Yes Kills

### 1. The Cornered Yes [00:48]
- "Yes feels like being cornered, a trap door, a loss of autonomy."
- "The moment they feel cornered, their survival brain kicks in. And now you're the threat. And people don't make deals with threats."

### 2. The Easy Yes [01:10]
- "The friendly yes. The 'Let's circle back' yes. The 'Sure, this looks good' yes."
- "That one feels better, but it's even more lethal. Because it's counterfeit. A false yes."
- "They're not saying yes to commit. They're saying yes to escape."
- "Now your follow-ups, your emails, your calls feel like chasing a ghost."

## The Fix: No-Oriented Questions [01:43]

### Example questions
- "Is now a bad time to talk?"
- "Would it be ridiculous to consider another option?"
- "Is this a bad idea?"
- "Would it be a horrible idea to go in this direction?"
- "Are you against talking about pricing?"

### Effects
- Make YOU a non-threat.
- Buy clarity, honesty, real engagement.
- "Let them feel protected. Let them feel in control."

## Why This Works (Psychology) [02:22]
- "Humans are twice as motivated to avoid a loss than to gain something of equal value."
- "Every yes feels risky. Every yes feels like potential loss."
- "But saying no, that's safe. It's autonomy. It's protection."

## The Reframe [03:02]
- "When someone says no, they're actually opening the door to real conversation."
- "No is not rejection. It's the beginning of trust-based influence."

## The Action [03:24]
- Forget about getting them to say yes.
- Instead: "Would it be a horrible idea to go in this direction?" Or "Are you against talking about pricing?"
- **Then shut up. Let them lean in. Let them engage. Let them tell you the truth.**
- "Chasing yes is costing you trust. Getting to no is how you build it."

## CZ adaptation notes
- "Is now a bad time?" → CZ: **"Volám v nevhodnou chvíli?"** (top-tier opener for cold calls)
- "Would it be ridiculous?" → CZ: **"Bylo by úplně mimo, kdybychom..."**
- "Are you against...?" → CZ: **"Vadilo by vám, kdybychom..."**
- "Would it be a horrible idea?" → CZ: **"Bylo by hrozné, kdybychom...?"**
- Pro tebe kritické: v cold callech i hlasovkách use "Volám v nevhodnou chvíli?" místo "Máš teď chvilku?". Open with NOH, not yes-seeking.
- During sales call, when introducing pricing: "Vadilo by ti, kdybych se dotkla investice?" instead of "Můžeme se bavit o ceně?"
