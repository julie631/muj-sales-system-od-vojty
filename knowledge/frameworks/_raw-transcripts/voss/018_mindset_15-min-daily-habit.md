# 15-Min Daily Habit — Build Tactical Empathy as Muscle Memory

## Context
3rd-most-viewed Voss video in top 30 (57k views). Tactical playbook for daily skill-building. Critical for going from "I know about labels" → "I instinctively use labels under pressure."

## Core Thesis
"Most people go into big conversations cold. Like walking into a ring without warming up. They read the book once, maybe watched a masterclass. But then they expect their brain to perform under pressure?"

"Negotiation, just like jiu-jitsu or jazz piano, is neural. You don't know it, you become it."

## The 15-Minute Practice (5 x 3-min reps)

### Rep 1: Label the Headlines [01:00]
- Grab a news story or trending topic.
- Practice 3 labels based on emotional dynamics. **Say them OUT LOUD.**
- Examples:
  - "It sounds like the public's frustrated."
  - "It looks like the company's scrambling."
  - "It seems like people feel betrayed."

### Rep 2: Calibrated Questions [01:45]
- Choose a tough situation. Ask:
  - "What makes that difficult?"
  - "How would you like it to look instead?"
  - "What's the biggest risk if this doesn't change?"
- **Out loud, again.**

### Rep 3: Mirror Out Loud [02:02]
- Take a conversation you had that day. Play it back in your head.
- Now mirror their last 3-5 words **out loud** with curiosity tone.
- Body language helps: **tilt head to the side, lift it up slightly.**

### Rep 4: Accusations Audit Warm-Up [02:24]
- Think of a FUTURE conversation. What are you afraid they'll say?
- Say it BEFORE they do.
- "This is probably going to sound selfish." (Anticipating their accusation.)
- "You might think I'm just trying to dodge responsibility."
- "You might think this is going to be harsh."
- Practice anticipation aloud.

### Rep 5: Journal a Real Conversation [03:10]
- Replay a real conversation from your day.
- What did I miss? What could I have labeled? What didn't I hear?

## The Compound Effect

### Timeline [03:54]
- **Day 1**: feels awkward.
- **Day 5**: start noticing things in conversations others miss.
- **Day 15**: tone changes. Stop reacting. Listen more.
- **Day 30**: fluency.

### Day 30 outcomes [04:23]
- **Fluency** in tactical empathy. Labeling becomes instinct.
- **Emotional control**. You hear attack, stay calm.
- **Priming**. Start conversations with right tone and framing.
- **Confidence**. You're training, not performing.

## The Neuroscience [05:04]
- "Every time you build a new pathway, it feels awkward. It feels like a struggle. It feels like you're doing something wrong. Instead, you're building that new pathway."
- "It takes 63 to 64 repetitions to lay down that new neural pathway, that new behavioral groove."

## The Output [05:24]
- Instead of panicking under pressure → you pause.
- Instead of blurting → you calibrate.
- Instead of pitching → you mirror.
- Instead of pushing → you label.
- "The other person feels heard. They'll tell you everything you need to close the deal."

## CZ adaptation notes
- Practice CZ phrasing aloud daily — labels feel awkward in CZ initially ("zní to, jako..." nezní přirozeně). Repetition = naturalization.
- Mirror practice: CZ tone shift on last word is HARDER than EN — needs more practice.
- Accusations audit prep (ukázka): "Pravděpodobně si říkáte, že jste podobnou službu už viděl..." / "Asi vám příjde drahé..." / "Možná si říkáte, že to nebude fungovat pro malou firmu..."
- 15-min daily = realistic. Schedule: ráno před coffee, while walking. (NE jako separátní task — sloučit s existující rutinou.)
