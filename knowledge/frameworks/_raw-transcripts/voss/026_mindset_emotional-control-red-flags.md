# Emotional Control — Red Flags + Step Back

## Context
Jocko Willink + Voss live audience Q&A. Topic: how to keep emotions out of the driver's seat during tough conversations. Highly practical body-based techniques.

## Step 1: Identify YOUR Red Flags [00:48]
- "We all have a different set of red flags — warnings that you're starting to get emotional."
- Examples:
  - Talking louder
  - Talking faster
  - Face flush
  - Clenching fists
  - Breathing harder
  - Some go OPPOSITE — going silent because angry
- **"Be detached enough that you realize 'oh, I'm starting to lose my temper right now.'"**

## Practice Partner: Your Kids [01:33]
- "Listen, are you married? Do you have kids?"
- "Our kids actually... I don't know who trains them, but they get trained to get under our skin."
- "The greatest training partners for controlling your emotions."
- Different friction at different ages.

## After-Action Review [02:00]
- After you get mad, go: "Hold on a second. What happened? How did it break down? What was being said to me?"
- "If you really start to get good, you can pre-identify certain statements/attitudes that make you mad."

## Step 2: Control Techniques

### A. Step Back [02:47]
- Literally. 4-5-6 inch step back.
- "It broadens your field of view."
- "Physiological reactions happen when you broaden field of view — it calms you down."
- "That's why when we look at the ocean it makes us feel calm. We look at the mountains — calm. As opposed to getting in someone's face."
- "Take a step back, look around, take a breath."

### B. The "Slow Radio Voice" [03:27]
- "When we go on the radio, we want to be relaxed."
- "If I get on the radio and say 'EVERYONE GET TO BUILDING 34 NOW!' — first, I cause everyone to panic. Worse — everyone makes fun of me afterwards."
- So Jocko learned to slow down ANY time he keyed the radio.
- "It's Jocko, everyone get to building 34, now." (Calm tempo.)
- "Brings that calm, gets emotions in check."

### C. Nodding [03:55]
- Let the other person know you're listening.
- "Affirm. Don't open mouth and start trying to verbally spar."

### D. Exposed Stance [04:14]
- **Hands down at your side. Open them up. Lift chin up.**
- "Basically exposing yourself."
- "Opposite mindset of a fight" (which is hands up + chin down).
- Calms YOU + signals safety to other person.

## Don't Void Emotions [05:00]
- "I'm not void of emotions. You have to put emotions into the CALCULUS of decision-making."
- Example: working with someone who's mad about the last project — bring emotion INTO the decision math.
- "But I can't let them be the driving force behind my decisions."
