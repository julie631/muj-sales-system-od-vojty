# Dealing with Manipulative People — Red Flags + Stronger Labels

## Context
Black Swan team coaching panel (Voss + George + Travis). Topic: manipulative counterparts, with focus on a hypothetical $10M deal where the prospect has been verbally attacking the rep.

## Core thesis
For every manipulative person, there are 2-3x more collaborative people who want to do business with you. Don't waste your time. But if you proceed, fall back on training.

## The "Happy Ears" Trap [00:00]
- "You get happy ears and you start to hope that you're reading the situation wrong."
- You start hoping they'll get better after the contract is signed.
- "And these same people come back to me and say 'I can't believe he's acting like this... I thought it would get better.'"
- "Who do you want to throw rocks at? Who's to blame for this? Certainly not me. The person showed you who they were and you chose to ignore it."

## Tool 1: Slow It Down [02:05]
- Don't respond emotionally to big deals.
- "Would you be opposed to me taking a few days to get back to you on this?"
- Go to your people (team, advocates, friends). Walk through the red flags.

## Tool 2: Fall Back on Training [03:08]
- "Quick 2+1": labels, mirrors, dynamic silence — in the middle of attacks.
- You may have to say no.
- You may have to make an ask → use **Accusations Audit** if delivering bad news during an attack.
- Easy to forget skills in middle of attacks and just hit back. Don't.

## Tool 3: Stronger Labels [04:10]
- After multiple attacks (e.g., 4 in 20 minutes), hit them with a stronger label:
- **"It sounds like you meant for that to sound as threatening as it did."**
- **"It seems like you're deliberately trying to back us into a corner."**
- Two effects:
  - **Unconscious manipulators** = it shakes them out of scripted behavior, makes them introspect. They didn't know they were doing it.
  - **Conscious manipulators** = they'll confirm. "Yeah, I meant it." That's your signal: decision time.

## Tool 4: Faces of No [06:13]
- Conscious manipulator confirmed → walk away politely.
- "I'm sorry, the way that you are engaging me just does not work for us."
- "I've laid it out that this conversation is about to end if you don't change."
- "I've said no to the behavior without actually saying no. I'm letting them feel it before I actually lower the boom."
- "I'm convey willingness to walk away."
- One last invite to collaborate. If they persist → they don't care → find someone who does.

## Tactical Empathy ≠ Doormat [04:10]
- "We're not telling you to lay down and roll over. We're not telling you to be victimized by anybody."
- Tactical empathy = subordinating yourself, deferring — but not being a victim.

## CZ adaptation notes
- Stronger label CZ: **"Vypadá to, že jste to chtěl říct tak hrozivě, jak to znělo."** or **"Vypadá to, že nás záměrně tlačíte do kouta."**
- Slow it down CZ: "Vadilo by vám, kdybych si pár dní rozmyslela?" (note: použít NOH formu).
- Face of No CZ: "To, jakým způsobem se mnou jednáte, mi nesedí."
- Pro tebe: applicable when prospects on a sales call start playing power games (často vlastníci větších firem). Don't get happy ears because the deal is bigger.
