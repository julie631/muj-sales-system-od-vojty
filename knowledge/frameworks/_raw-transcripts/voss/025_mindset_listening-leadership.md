# Listening — The Most Underrated Leadership Skill

## Context
Voss + Jocko Willink (Navy SEAL) discussion. Topic: communication = listening. Short and dense (4:34).

## The Voss-Jocko Question Pattern [00:00]
- Voss notes Jocko uses "earnest questions" — including no-oriented questions.
- Example from Jocko's book: instead of saying "your CEO doesn't want to drive your salespeople out," Jocko ASKED them: "Do you think your CEO is trying to drive your salespeople out?"
- "Instead of seeking yes, there's something about asking people a question where the answer is no — that makes them confront reality."

## Earnest Questions vs Accusatory [01:00]
- "Sometimes the earnest question reveals the truth to people."
- Not "Why did you do that wrong?" (accusatory) → "Can you explain why you did X, just so I understand?"

## Humility Foundation [01:25]
- "Humility is the most important characteristic of a leader."
- "My assumption isn't that you're wrong and I'm right. My assumption is that you're right and I'm wrong."
- Asks via that frame.
- Example: "Chris, you moved left there instead of right — can you explain why?"
- Chris: "Yeah, I saw enemy to the right, so left was obvious." Jocko: "Oh great, I couldn't see from my position. Thanks."

## Communication = Listening [02:00]
- "When I talk about communication, everyone thinks I'm talking about talking. But I'm actually talking about listening."
- "The most underrated skill in leadership is to shut your mouth and listen."

## Voss's Mishearing Diagnostic [02:48]
- "An executive talking about deals falling apart due to miscommunication. I said: 'Is it miscommunication or is it mishearing?' He said: 'Yeah, it's absolutely mishearing.'"

## The 90/10 Talking Story [03:00]
- Jocko's client: "I had a great conversation with him."
- Jocko: "What percentage of the conversation were YOU talking?"
- Client went quiet. (Tells = 90%+.)
- "The individual he's having the conversation with isn't making progress, isn't getting his thoughts out."
- Counterpart's later perspective: "Joe doesn't listen. Ego fills the whole conversation."

## Intent Has a Smell [03:35]
- "Intent has a smell. If the intention behind your question is that you think you were probably wrong instead of 'I was right and you were wrong' — that completely changes the way it lands."

## CZ adaptation notes
- "Is it miscommunication or mishearing?" CZ: **"Je to nedorozumění, nebo špatný poslech?"**
- 90/10 rule: on calls aim 80% listening / 20% talking. Use mirror as a tool to keep them talking (no need for you to talk).
- Earnest questions CZ assumption-flip: NE "proč to tak děláš?" → ALE "Pomoz mi pochopit, proč jste to udělali takhle." (humility first).
- "Intent has a smell" CZ: **"Záměr je cítit."** — když hlasovka smrdí prodejem, prospect to cítí. Když smrdí péčí, taky.
- Pro tebe: critical for sales call energy. 90/10 talk-listen is the gold-standard. If you're talking >30% of discovery, you're losing.
