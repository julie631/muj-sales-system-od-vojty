# How to UNFK Your Sales — 4-Step Framework

## Context
Tactical YouTube video. Voss's most condensed framework: 4 steps + 1 bonus to flip "dead-end conversations into closed deals." Each step has scripts.

## Core thesis
"Most sales fail because you're talking AT your customer, not WITH them. If you're explaining, you're losing. Pitching features instead of finding the hidden information."

## Step 1: Lead with Tactical Empathy [01:02]
- Before talking about your offer, talk about THEM.
- Label their likely reality:
  - "It seems like there's a reason we're talking."
  - "It seems like hitting your targets this quarter has probably been tough."
  - "You're probably under a lot of pressure to cut cost."
- "When you label emotions, you calm them down."
- Demonstrate understanding: "I see you. I hear you."
- **Even if you miss, they correct you — solid gold.**

## Step 2: Calibrated Questions [02:02]
- Start with **How** or **What**.
- ❌ AVOID: "How can I help you?"
  - "If you don't know how you might help me, why should I waste my time with you?"
- ✅ Good examples:
  - "How have things failed in the past?" (taps memory, easy to access)
  - "What might make this a good fit for you?" (collaborative problem-solving)
  - "How does this align with what you're trying to achieve?"
- 2 effects: (1) shifts problem-solving to customer (ownership), (2) reveals black swans.

## Step 3: Mirror Like a Pro [03:30]
- Repeat the last 1-3 words.
- Example: "We just don't have the budget right now" → "The budget right now."
- **Tone is critical** — comprehension or curiosity. Without it, you sound like a robot.
- Mirror buys time, makes them feel heard, unlocks detail.
- They'll often spill what's actually behind the objection (budget next quarter, right ROI, etc.).

## Step 4: Get to "That's Right" [04:23]
- "When you hear 'That's right,' you know they feel understood — not manipulated, not pressured. Understood."
- Summarize their situation better than they could (2-3 sentences).
- If you get it right → they nod, say "That's right."
- "People don't argue with their own conclusions."
- If you DON'T get it right → stay silent, they'll fill in. Repeat what they added until you DO get a "That's right."

## Bonus: Permission to Pitch [05:50]
- Get permission with a **no-warranted question**:
  - "Would it offend you if I shared some ideas with you?"
  - "Would it offend you if I shared some solutions with you?"
- This triggers permission to move forward without making them feel cornered.

## Bonus 2: Find Silent Deal Killers [06:23]
- Every deal has hidden info that changes everything.
- Mix labels + mirrors + calibrated questions until you get a "That's right."

## CZ adaptation notes
- Label likely reality CZ: "Vypadá to, že tenhle kvartál je dost náročný" / "Pravděpodobně máte tlak, abyste neztratili klienty."
- Calibrated CZ: "Jak by tohle muselo vypadat, aby vám to dávalo smysl?" / "Co je teď ten největší problém?"
- Mirror CZ: tone matters HUGELY in češtině — must rise on the end. "Nemáme teď rozpočet?" (curious, rising intonation).
- "That's right" CZ: **"Přesně tak"** or **"Přesně to."**
- No-warranted permission CZ: **"Naštvalo by vás, kdybych vám ukázala pár nápadů?"** or **"Bylo by úplně mimo, kdybych ti řekla, jak by se to dalo vyřešit?"**
- This is the EXACT framework for discovery: empathy → calibrated → mirror → that's right → permission to pitch the offer.
