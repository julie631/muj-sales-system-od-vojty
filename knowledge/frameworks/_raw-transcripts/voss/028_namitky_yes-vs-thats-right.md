# Yes vs "That's Right" — Why You're Chasing the Wrong Thing

## Context
Updated framework video. Voss revises his older "Yes, how" guidance to "That's right, how." Tight 5-min explainer.

## Hope is Hopium [00:38]
- "Yes at its best is an aspiration, a hope."
- "Hope is not a strategy. Hope is a drug. Hopium. Hope clouds observation."

## The 3 Kinds of Yes [00:55]
1. **Commitment** — genuine, actionable yes.
2. **Confirmation** — yes to clarify.
3. **Counterfeit** — a lie. Vast majority of yeses.

## Aim for "That's Right" Instead [01:11]
- Phrase signals **genuine agreement and understanding**.
- "Every time someone says 'that's right,' they're moving in your direction. They're acknowledging that you get them."

## The Ultimate "That's Right" — Via Epiphany [01:30]
- Best version: a moment of insight they gained because of something YOU said.
- "It triggers oxytocin, the love drug, the bonding neurochemical."
- "They bonded with you and you've established trust."

## "That's Right" Still Needs How [01:50]
- The how isn't optional.
- BUT: after "That's right," IF you stay silent, "they just might outline the how FOR you."
- Powerful + autonomy-respecting.

## Why Yes Falls Short [02:11]
- "People are conditioned to expect hidden hooks and micro-commitments in a yes."
- Micro-traps. Tie downs. They've been trapped by it before.
- "Can you expect good implementation of a deal where a counterpart has been cornered?"

## The No-Oriented Question Pivot [02:40]
- Replace yes-Q's with no-Q's:
  - "Do you agree" → **"Do you disagree?"**
  - "Would you like to" → **"Are you against?"** or **"Is this a ridiculous idea?"**

## The Yes Business is Broken [03:14]
- "You're playing at the low win table. You compromise, concede, cut your price, and still have implementation problems."
- "Your deal pipeline is poor, your referral pipeline is anemic, and you live in quarter-to-quarter anxiety. Yes is killing you."

## The Reframe [03:54]
- "Get out of the yes business altogether and get into the agreement and long-term trusted relationship business."
- Focus on understanding, empathy, mutually beneficial solutions, even Black Swans (things you didn't even think of).
- "Strive for that's right and watch your deals close more smoothly and your relationships flourish."

## Closing Note [04:33]
- "Negotiation is a perishable skill."
- "When the sun rises tomorrow, your choices: same, better, or worse. Two of three require involvement from you."

## CZ adaptation notes
- "That's right" CZ: **"Přesně tak"** / **"Přesně to."** — both work.
- Epiphany version CZ: target moment when prospect goes "ach jo, to mě nenapadlo..." — that's the oxytocin moment.
- Yes-Q to No-Q flip CZ:
  - "Souhlasíte?" → **"Nesouhlasíte?"** (zní divně, ale funguje)
  - "Chtěl byste...?" → **"Vadilo by vám...?"**
- AUDIT your last 5 sales calls for "yes" chasing patterns — likely 50%+ tie-down questions. Replace with NOH structure.
- "Yes is killing you" CZ message → kritický framing pro každou pipeline aktivních leadů — kolik z nich řekli "ano" jen aby se odlepili?
