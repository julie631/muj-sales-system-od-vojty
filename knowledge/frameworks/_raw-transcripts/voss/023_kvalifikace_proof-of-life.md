# Proof of Life — Don't Be the Rabbit

## Context
Voss expanding on a concept he calls "Proof of Life" — borrowed from hostage negotiation. Question: is there a real deal AND is it with YOU? Audience: anyone with a long pipeline of deals that don't close.

## The Core Principle [00:01]
**"It's not a sin to not get the deal. It's a sin to take a long time to not get the deal."**

## The "Rabbit" Analogy [02:03]
- Source: Voss reading *Bare Knuckle Bargaining* recommendation.
- Dog race: the rabbit never gets caught. Dogs chase forever.
- "When you're the fool in the game, you're never going to get the deal."
- Vince Vaughn movie reference: rabbit used to drive down another's price.
- Voss thought: "That's a Hollywood figment. No one would ever do that in real life."
- Then he sees it in business books.

## How Common Is It? [03:34]
- Challenger Sale research: 1,000 executives polled.
- "How often do you engage in negotiations where you're never going to give the other side the deal?"
- **20%** by their own admission.
- Voss's read: executives MINIMIZE in self-reports. Reality is closer to **80%**.

## The Math [04:05]
- If 20% of your time is on dead deals, eliminating them = same money in 80% time.
- Higher hourly rate, better quality of life.
- "If this hypothesis is true... you could just eliminate these. You're automatically making the same amount of money only working 80%."

## Two Components of Proof of Life [02:03]
1. **Is there a real deal at all?** (Could be vapor.)
2. **Is it with YOU?** (Could be a price-drive on someone else.)

## How Tactical Empathy Helps [05:06]
- Why questions WERE avoided ("creates defensiveness, damages rapport").
- **"Why" today = tactical empathy emotional intelligence surgical strike."** When you know how to use it.
- Tactical empathy works universally — regardless of culture, gender, ethnicity.
- The lies don't change human nature; tactical empathy uncovers them.

## "Ignore Human Nature at Your Peril" [04:46]
- "How often do you lie in a negotiation? You're going to slow at the time and maybe only 20%."
- Reality: up to 80%.
- "Ignore human nature at your peril."

## CZ adaptation notes
- Proof of Life CZ: pojmenovat jako **"důkaz života"** nebo **"existuje deal vůbec?"**.
- Pro tebe: projdi aktivní leady v pipeline → kolik z nich je rabbit? Použij tactical empathy + Voss-004 phrase "Hodili jste to za hlavu?" → buď close, nebo pohřbi.
- "If you don't know who the fool is, it's you" → CZ "Pokud nevíš, kdo je v té hře blázen, jsi to ty."
- Kvalifikace ve tvém funnelu MUSÍ obsahovat early proof-of-life check: "Co vás vedlo k tomu, že jste si na call udělal čas?" / "Jak to dnes pro vás vypadá s rozhodováním?" / "S kým ještě tohle řešíte?"
- The 80% lying rate is a brutal reframe but matches reality of CZ small businesses — owners often "říkají, co chceme slyšet, aby z toho callu utekli."
