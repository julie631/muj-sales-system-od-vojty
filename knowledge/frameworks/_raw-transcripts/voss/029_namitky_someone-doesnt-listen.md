# Negotiating with Someone Who Doesn't Listen

## Context
Black Swan team panel (Sandy, Derek, Troy) on the power dynamic problem. What do you do when someone considers themselves the powerful one and dominates the conversation? Insight: don't fight power directly — give illusion of control.

## The LinkedIn Quote Inspiration [00:00]
- Sandy saw a LinkedIn post about handling power dynamics.
- "Don't try to knock them down a peg. Let them feel like they're in control."
- "Give them the illusion of control. Let them hold on to that power."
- "As long as you know in your mind that's what's happening, you can still make the conversation work for you."

## Tools That Work [01:00]

### A. Labels + Mirrors [02:01]
- "When you can really listen to them and respectfully do labels and mirrors where they have to defend without being questioned what they want — it works."
- "They have some self-discovery. They listen to themselves while they're answering the question. They realize how stupid it sounds out loud when they have to explain it. They walk that back."

### B. Ego Labels [03:01]
- "Don't underestimate the power of an ego label."
- "They need to be (we say HEARD AND RESPECTED) — other people say STROKED."
- "By not catering to that, you're allowing them to stay in an agitated and negative state, which impedes their ability to process."

### C. Mirrors for Information Extraction [04:02]
- Powerful types get fatigued — they don't realize how much energy they spend talking.
- "When it's your turn to talk, you're going to be fresher."
- "You have uncovered a lot of information."
- "Black Swans coming out early in the conversation."

### D. Their Fear: Losing Power [05:04]
- "When you're dealing with somebody who feels they're in power, their biggest fear is that you're going to take that power away from them."
- "If you head in that direction, you're going to spin them up."
- **Use Accusations Audit:**
  - "This may feel like a power play."
  - Acknowledge the fear they have that they may lose power and control to you.

### E. Calibrated Questions [07:30 + part 2]
- Instead of telling them they're wrong → ask a calibrated question.
- "Make them formulate the thought in their mind."
- "They come to the realization on their own without you having to point it out."
- "They maintain their power and control. Now it's THEIR idea to switch it up."

### F. "What happens if..." Question [01:31 part 2]
- "Seems like that's the only option" → let them sit.
- Then: "What happens if that option doesn't work?"
- Triggers reality-check thinking on their side.

## Assertive Type Specific Insights [03:01 part 2]
- Assertives blow up deals if they don't feel HEARD, even at expense of money.
- "The deal can go to hell. As long as they've gotten that across to you, they're good with it."
- Counterintuitive: "Many people feel these are the most difficult to deal with. Reality: they're the EASIEST."
- "Less I have to say. Hit them with a decent label and they'll vomit up all this information."
- Nuggets within. You let them go.

## Critical Followup for Assertive Closes [05:03 part 2]
- Assertive comfortable with **yes without a how.**
- "You're doing yourself a disservice if you don't what-and-how them to death about implementation, next steps, what it's going to look like in the future."
- "Don't let them go until you have concrete steps."

## Analyst Type Notes [08:06 part 2]
- "If they're an analyst with power, they've got the data and information on their side. You can't make them see anything else."
- "Like fighting an uphill battle with two flat tires."
- Don't assume power = assertive. Could be analyst or accommodator.

## CZ adaptation notes
- Illusion of control CZ → critical na sales call s větší firmou (např. stavební firma s několika partami), kde owner je často "I'm the boss" type.
- Ego label CZ for assertive: **"Vidím, že jste si tenhle business postavil sám. Spoustu věcí už jste musel rozseknout."**
- Accusations audit pro power-dynamic CZ: **"Tohle možná zní jako pokus o nátlak — to není záměr."**
- "What happens if that doesn't work?" CZ: **"Co se stane, když by to takhle nefungovalo?"**
- Pro tebe: assertive owners → let them talk, use mirrors, ego labels. Don't try to push back. Let them feel powerful, then nail down implementation with what/how questions at the close.
- DON'T assume power dynamic = assertive. Watch for analyst (data-driven) and accommodator (people-pleaser w/ power).
