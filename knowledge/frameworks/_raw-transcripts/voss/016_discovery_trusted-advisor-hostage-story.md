# Trusted Advisor — Troy's Most Difficult Hostage Negotiation

## Context
Troy Smith (former police negotiator, Black Swan instructor) speaking to real estate audience. Tells his hardest hostage negotiation as a parable for how to become a "trusted advisor" instead of just another sales rep.

## The Setup [01:01]
- Troy just moved into a new house with his wife. Pager goes off: 911.
- Radio: old man holding little girl hostage at gunpoint.
- Troy's reaction: "How dare he. He's a punk. Want to mess with somebody who can't defend themselves."
- "I became very angry."
- Wife pissed because moving day. Troy driving over angry. "Hoping the SWAT team blow his brains out."

## Critical Mindset Reset [02:00]
- Arriving on scene, sergeant: "Troy, you're going to be the negotiator."
- Troy: "Great. If I get a chance I'mma blow his brains out."
- Sergeant: "Whoa whoa whoa. What's going on with you? What is your job? To save lives. EVERYBODY'S life. What's going on with you?"
- Sergeant made Troy **sit down for 10 minutes and dump his bucket** about everything in his world.
- "I had to dump my bucket first before I could become a negotiator and listen to the other side."
- "75 to 80% of the conversation is about who the other side is."
- "I couldn't do that because half of my brain was offline because I was thinking about what was going on in MY world."

## On Scene [02:55]
- Old man sitting on picnic chair, rocking, eyes closed, hugging girl, gun to back of her head.
- Girl is calm — not scared. Doesn't match the radio report.
- Troy: "I had to listen with my eyes." (Reading the gap between presenting reality and latent reality.)

## Initial Calibrated Question [04:04]
- Troy: "Sir, how do we get here today?"
- He doesn't even look. Just keeps rocking.
- People in Troy's earpiece trying to coach him what to say.
- Troy uses **dynamic silence** instead. Lets the man process.

## The Story Unfolds [05:04]
- "My daughter is a heroin addict. She prostitutes herself. There's nothing I can do for her."
- "She has a boyfriend who's treating her like shit. But he did something unimaginable. He sexually molested my granddaughter."
- "I promised her I would never let that happen again."

## Troy's Label (Identity-Level) [06:09]
- "Sir, I can tell you love your granddaughter with all your heart. You're her superhero. You're her protector. You're her provider. There's nothing you wouldn't do for her. She's the most valuable person in the world to you."
- Old man reveals: "By the way, this guy has a warrant. You dumbasses can't even catch him."
- Troy radioed every officer in the city to find the boyfriend.

## The Crisis Reveal [07:11]
- Troy: "Sir, I know your love is unconditional. But I still don't understand how we got here today."
- Man: "I just came back from the doctor's office. They told me I have stage 4 cancer. About a week to live. I promised my granddaughter I would never let anything happen to her. The only way to keep my word is to take her with me."

## The Pivot — Calibrated Question + Graceful Ask [08:13]
- Troy: "How do we fix it?"
- No response.
- Troy: **"How bad of a position would I put you in to let me take over that responsibility?"**
- First time the man LOOKED at Troy: "Don't say it if you don't mean it."
- Troy: "Sir, I 100% mean it."
- The man set down the gun, walked over, placed granddaughter in Troy's arms: "Now she's your responsibility."

## The Coda [10:15]
- Next morning, Troy returns. Man hugs him: "Officer Smith, I love you. You understood that I was giving you my most valuable possession."
- Daughter (different from heroin one) — her husband in military — coming to adopt the girl.
- Man relieves Troy of responsibility. A week later, the man died.
- Earpiece moment during the original scene: officer whispers "Troy, we caught him." Troy unplugs earpiece: "Say it again." Then tells the old man → "He knew that everything I told him was the truth."

## Real Estate Application [11:15]
- "In your world is no different. When you're talking to these people with emotions thinking about this house... she said it's crumbling, falling apart."
- "You're concerned about that. You're not concerned about HER. Be concerned about HER."
- Label: "Seems like, man, you've had so many great memories in this house."
- She'll tell you. Show you. Open up.
- **"People are 6x more likely to do business with people they like."**
- "Become likable. Don't make it about you. Find out the why behind her behavior."

## CZ adaptation notes
- "Dump your bucket" CZ: pojmenovat jako **"vyklopit vlastní kbelík"** — před každým důležitým callem 10 min audit svého stavu mysli. (Příprava na sales calls.)
- "How do we fix it?" CZ: **"Jak to spravíme?"** / **"Co s tím uděláme?"**
- "How bad a position would I put you in to..." CZ: **"Do jak špatné pozice bych vás dostala, kdybych..."** — graceful ask, no pressure.
- Identity-level label CZ for owner (např. úklidová firma): "Vidím, že vám jde především o to, aby tu kluky/holky drželi pohromadě, aby měli práci. Jste jejich opora." (Identity = protector, provider.)
- 75-80% rule CZ: on sales calls TALK 20%, LISTEN 80%. Small-business owners love being heard — let them.
