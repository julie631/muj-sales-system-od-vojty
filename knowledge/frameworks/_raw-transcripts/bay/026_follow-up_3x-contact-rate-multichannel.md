# How to 3x your contact rates

## The big number — 3×

[00:02] **3× = contact rate increase when using multi-channel vs single-channel.**

We've heard this before — not about the phone, not the email, not social. **Use all three.** But again and again Bay sees reps relying mostly on email and passive forms of communication.

## The barrier — "this channel doesn't work"

[02:40] Clara interviews reps for a "day in the life" before building training. She always asks: "What channels are really working for you?"

Common responses:
- "The phones don't work, so I don't use them."
- "Phones work, but emails aren't a great place to meet our prospects."

There's always this **single-channel belief system** — "this works, this doesn't, so I don't touch the other."

## Case study — enterprise rev intelligence team

Reps in saturated 7-figure deal space (Salesforce, ServiceNow, Adobe competitors):

> "Outbound isn't the approach. We build pipeline through relationships. The only way to break in if I don't have a relationship is to use my executives to break into their execs."

**Their belief:** cold outreach doesn't work, only warm intros do.

The catch: they were **only contacting prospects with warm relationships**. Not even outbounding to the rest of their TAM.

## The fix — AI prompt for omni-channel one-shot

For complex enterprise reps with 7-figure deals, **make it easy**.

Built one AI prompt that, per account:
- Generated account research (10K, 10Q, transcripts, earning reports, common connections)
- Produced first-touch outbound email
- Bump email
- LinkedIn message
- **LinkedIn voice notes script**
- Cold call talk track
- Voicemail script

"It literally gave them everything they needed to create an omni-channel approach within minutes."

**Result:** an enterprise rep resistant to email tried it once. Got a response: *"Let me just text you. This is the channel I like to communicate in. Strong offer in the email — what more can you tell me?"*

## Meet buyers in their preferred channel

[08:11] *"I love voice notes. I hate getting them on LinkedIn because they're not transcribed."*

Different buyers have different preferences. The whole point:
1. Show you've done research
2. Show you're trying to add value / solve a real challenge
3. Let them communicate back in **their** preferred channel

## Why leave voicemails — the data

[08:59] **Voicemails open at ~90%** because they're transcribed. People hate the red icon, but they read the transcript.

**Bay's mom analogy:** "She leaves me a voicemail every time. Do I listen? No. Do I read the transcript and send a voice note back? Yes."

**Voicemail structure:**
- Point them back to (or toward) the email you're about to send
- Reference the subject line of that email
- "Oh that sounds interesting — let me go read that email."

**Gong data: voicemails increase email reply rates by up to 3×.**

## High-level cadence guidance

**KISS-style 3-week cadence:**
- 15–16 touch points over 3 weeks
- **Make a lot of noise, back off a day or two, noise again**
- Could be: 6 emails + 6 calls + 3 social touches

**Critical:** the buyer needs to know all touch points are **connected**. Each one references the same research/value. If you mention X in the email, mention it in the voicemail too.

## Hot take — different cadences for ATL vs BTL

[11:34] Clara's hot take: don't treat all prospects the same.

**ATL (Above The Line — decision makers):**
- 3-week cadence, 15 touches works
- Tight, targeted, concise, less touch points
- Lots of strategy per touch

**BTL / On-the-line (influencers, intel-givers, path-to-power):**
- 4 to 4.5 weeks cadence
- More touch points: 8 emails + 8 calls + 4 social
- They're busy → more consistency wins
- Eventually it stands out: "OK this is valuable"

ATL stakeholders → super specific, targeted. BTL → persistent breakthrough.

## Pro tebe — aplikace (DŮLEŽITÉ — direct match s 5-step FU)

Typická multichannel cadence malého byznysu: DM hlasovka + 5-step FU (v každém dalším kroku zbývá méně leadů). To je Bay's KISS adaptace pro IG/FB DM.

**Co funguje:**
- Hlasovka ke každému leadu = nehrneš všechno do textu
- 5-step FU s decay (= "noise then back off" Bay's princip)
- Stages v tabulce leadů / CRM = pattern tracking

**Co můžeš vylepšit (Bay's bod):**

**1. Multichannel u single-location klienta:**
Pokud jedeš primárně přes IG DM, Bay's 3× contact rate insight = přidat:
- **Email** (pokud ho má) — second touch po hlasovce v IG
- **WhatsApp** (CZ ekvivalent voicemail-driven texting)
- **LinkedIn** (pro premium klienty s majitelem na LI, např. franšízy)

→ Místo 5 touchů 1 kanálem → 5 touchů 3 kanály = víc šance dosáhnout

**2. Voicemail princip pro IG DM:**
Bay's "voicemail points to email subject" → CZ adaptace pro IG hlasovku:
- Hlasovka 1: "Hele Petře, posílám ti za chvíli krátký text, co jsme spolu probrali — mrkni večer, je tam zajímavá věc o {klient z tvých case studies}."
- Text 5 min později: link na case study + subject s výsledkem v číslech ({tvoje case study s čísly})

→ Hlasovka teasuje, text dodává obsah. Stejný princip.

**3. ATL vs BTL u menších firem:**
- **Single-location firma (ATL):** majitel sám = decision maker. Bay's ATL cadence = 3 týdny, méně touchů, strategicky.
- **Firmy s více pobočkami / franšízy:** majitel + provoz. Bay's BTL cadence pro provoz = 4 týdny, víc dotyku.

**4. KISS sequencing CZ template (tvoje verze):**
- Den 1: IG DM hlasovka
- Den 1+1h: text follow-up s case study
- Den 3: text "viděls hlasovku?" + emoji ❤️
- Den 6: voice nota druhá s konkrétní personalizací ("dnes jsem viděla tvoje Insta...")
- Den 10: text s nabídkou času konkrétně
- Den 17: breakup style "už tě nebudu obtěžovat"
- Den 21: "off-ramp" final touch

**5. Multichannel v B2B:**
- LinkedIn message
- IG DM
- Email (pokud je veřejný na webu)
- Telefonát s personalizovanou voicemail

Bay's klíčový stat: **3× contact rate jen tím, že přidáš 2 další kanály**. Např. při 30 active leads → potenciálně 90 contacted opportunities místo 30.
