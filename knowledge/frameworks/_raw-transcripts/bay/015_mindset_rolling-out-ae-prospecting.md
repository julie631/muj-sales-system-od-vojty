# Ideal way on rolling out an AE prospecting motion

[00:00] We did not have an AE prospecting motion, but the business was asking us to create more pipeline and we couldn't rely on inbound and SDRs. We had to create a process around AE **self-sourcing pipeline**.

## The rollout

I just publicly announced it on a team meeting one day:
> "This is coming in one month. This is why we're doing this. The business needs to bring in more revenue. We don't have enough pipeline to hit our targets. You all know the product and the personas really well, and we're going to start asking you to self-source X% of your pipeline."

Important addition: **"That doesn't mean inbound is going to stop or SDR support is going to stop."**

## The framing — business need, not failure

[00:55] *"Just means that in a year from now when you're trying to hit your quota, you can own your destiny a little bit better by controlling the controllables."*

I publicly announced it and **talked about it every single week**.

What was helpful: you give them time to sink it in — "okay, my role is changing because the business has changed. This isn't 'you're not doing your job so now you have to cold call.' This is coming as a business ask and we all have to adapt."

## Pro tebe — onboarding aplikace

I bez týmu **máš klienty** — a klient = "rep", který musí adopt-nout novou praxi (= např. posílat podklady, kontrolovat reporty, schvalovat návrhy).

**Tu Bay's announcement style aplikovat na onboarding call:**

> "Tu novou věc, co s tebou rozjedeme za 14 dní: každý druhý čtvrtek ti pošlu souhrn, co se za ty dva týdny udělalo. Ne že bys to neuměl — ale aby to fungovalo dlouhodobě, potřebuju, abys mi v té chvíli řekl, jestli něco upravit. To je všechno. **Nemyslím tím, že bys to musel hlídat denně** — naopak, smysl je v tom, že se o to starám já. Ale 1× za 14 dní 5 minut tvého času je nezbytných."

**Repeat weekly princip:**
Tato věta musí padnout 3× během onboardingu: po setupu, při 1. update reportu, při 2. update reportu. Bay: "people miss it the first time."

**"Control the controllables" framing pro klienta (ukázka: stavební firma):**
> "Poptávkové portály jsou jako loterie. Někdy ti přijdou 3 poptávky, někdy žádná. Ale tvoji stávající klienti a jejich doporučení — to je tvoje. To jsi schopen ovlivnit. Portály jsou controllable jen napůl."
