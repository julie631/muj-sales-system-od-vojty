# Dialers won't fix your cold calling problem

## The setup — why people try dialers

[00:03] Coming from call center / construction outbound era — auto dialers had reps in cubes with <30 seconds between live conversations. B2B has adopted this in the last 3-4 years.

The biggest bottleneck right now:
- Back when outreach was selling (a few years ago): pickup rate 12-15%
- Pre-COVID: people were in offices, you could call extensions
- **Now: 5% or below**

Two ways to fix:
1. **Overcome with volume** (dial 200 people instead of 20)
2. **Get better per call** (the right answer, mostly)

A dialer just accelerates volume. **The cons are massive.**

## Massive con 1 — Spam flagging

[02:42] Client example: enterprise BDR team selling digital certificates to CISOs/VPs of security. Low connect rate (<5%). Started using dialer.

**3-month timeline:**
- Months 1-3: massive spike in conversations
- Month 3+: connect rate **<1%** — blasted through entire TAM
- T-Mobile, AT&T, Verizon detect fishy/spammy behavior (just like Gmail/Outlook do for email)
- **Dialing more than 15-20 people from a line + hangups before voicemail = spam-flagged**
- Number labeled spam → straight to voicemail for many

**Titan X study:** within 90 days, **connect rate decreases by about half**.

## Massive con 2 — Conversion rate drops 30%+

[05:16] Auto dialer dials 10-15 phone lines at a time. When people pick up simultaneously, it hangs up the others. There's a **2-3 second delay** — you don't hear "hello," just a beep, then you start talking.

**Most people hang up at that point** — they know what's going on.

Plus: rep has no time to prep. Pops up "Clara Johnson, Head of Sales" — no chance to gather thoughts, look at LinkedIn, see "she's in Denver, mountain time, head of sales, couple hundred reps." Rep is in **panic state** hoping someone picks up. Conversation has to be generic.

**Conversion rate suffers dramatically.**

## Combined impact

Take into account:
- People you don't get hold of (lower connect rate)
- Lower conversion when you do connect
- **You're cutting your TAM by 40%**

## The fix — two parts

### Fix 1 — Titan X-style rotation

[07:07] Titan X works on the back end. Give it 1,000 numbers → it tells you "here are the 300 most likely to pick up." Real example: Bay called 30 people in an hour, talked to **9 sales executives**, was mentally fatigued at 45 minutes.

> "I felt mentally fatigued 45 minutes in. I don't want to talk to anyone anymore."

(That's the point — quality conversations are exhausting.)

### Fix 2 — Reverse pitch (rep effectiveness in first 60 seconds)

[07:55] Big flip: instead of talking about certificates (their product), use the **reverse pitch**.

The idea: when I get someone on the phone, I want to demonstrate as quickly as possible that I **understand their world and their challenges**. That's the best way to build credibility and earn the next minute or two.

**Reverse pitch example:**
> "Hey, I talk to a lot of security leaders, and we keep hearing a really big focus right now on automation and PKI modernization specifically. So they're using manual spreadsheet-driven tracking of certificates and it's keeping them from being more strategic with resources — all their folks are just stuck in spreadsheets. How does that compare to your day-to-day?"

Result: **20%+ increase in quarter-over-quarter opportunities created.** A year later, still seeing good results.

## Takeaway

[09:36] Dialers are a no-no. **Technology is not always the solution.** Dig in, diagnose, and from that diagnosis go coach and refine your strategy.

## Pro tebe — aplikace

**Bay's bod o "volume isn't fix" je relevantní i pro tebe.**

**Co dělat správně (quality > volume):**
- Personalizovaná hlasovka / zpráva ke každému leadu = quality (každá je 1-2 min přípravy + osobní)
- Žádný "auto-dialer mode" — každý lead dostane personalizovaný DM / hlasovku
- → Právě tohle drží close rate nahoře, ne objem

**Co můžeš udělat lépe (Bay's "reverse pitch" approach):**

**1. Reverse pitch v hlasovce (ukázka: úklidová firma → kanceláře):**
> "Hele Petře, hodně firem, se kterými mluvím, mění úklidovou firmu každý rok — pokaždé za levnější, a pokaždé řeší stejné stížnosti od lidí v kanceláři. Místo aby řešili, proč to nefunguje, hledají další nejlevnější nabídku. Děje se to i vám?"

→ Demonstruje, že rozumíš jejich world, místo abys vysvětlovala, **co děláš ty**.

**2. Demonstrate understanding before pitching:**

Bay's stejný princip jak v #001 (customer voice) a #017 (buyer's world). **Reverse pitch** je tactical name pro toto v cold callu.

**3. Reverse pitch v jiném oboru (ukázka: fyzioterapeutka → firmy):**
> "Jano, mluvím s HR manažerkami v Praze — vidím opakující se pattern: firma platí lidem benefity na sport, ale půlka kanceláře má bolavá záda od sezení a nikdo to neřeší. Pak se diví, že rostou nemocenské. Stalo se to i u vás?"

**4. Auto-dialer warning (do budoucna):**
Pokud najmeš prvního obchodníka a najdeš se v pokušení "dáme mu auto-dialer, ať to jede" — **NE**. Bay's data: 90 dní → 50% connect rate dolů, 30% conversion dolů, 40% TAM zničený.

**5. Co můžeš vyzkoušet — Titan X-style:**
Pro CZ trh není přímý ekvivalent Titan X (US-specific spam detection na T-Mobile/Verizon). Ale lze experimentovat s:
- Více SIM kartami (různé čísla → menší risk spam flag)
- Volat z nástroje (dialer / CRM), který sleduje, které číslo se "vypálilo"
- **Pokud děláš outreach** primárně přes DM / hlasovky, ne klasické cold cally → tento risk je minimální

**Klíčová věta od Bay:** *"Technology is not always the solution to the problems we have. We got to dig in, diagnose."*
