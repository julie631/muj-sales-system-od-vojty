# The mindset behind the "customer voice" approach

[00:00] **They're giving you the language. That's the coolest part — the language. You don't have to make it up.**

## The visual metaphor

If you can't see me, I'm basically on one side of the screen saying *this is your world* — where you sell stuff, your solution, all of it exists on this side. On the other side is your **buyer's world**.

When you do outbound, **they did not come to you asking for information on your product or service**. You have to meet them in their world first. You have to speak in a language that they understand.

## The mutual language

The mutual language between buyer and seller is **their priorities, their problems**. You bring them over to the solution **later on in the sales process**.

**You do not do that during the outbound process.**

## The quickest way to add context

[00:49] You have to meet them in their world. The quickest way to add context to what you do is to **explain it through their problems**. That's how you add context really, really quickly.
