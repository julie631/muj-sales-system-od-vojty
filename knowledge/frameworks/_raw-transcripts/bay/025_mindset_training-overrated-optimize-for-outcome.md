# HOT TAKE ALERT: Sales training is overrated!

## The fail mode

[00:00] Sales training is overrated. The fail mode: enablement folks / sales leaders think **training is the end destination** — once you've delivered the training, that information will magically seep into reps' brains and everything changes immediately.

**Reality:** the **Ebbinghaus forgetting curve** — people only retain **10%** of training 24 hours later. We've all seen this: do a training, wait a week, **zero result**.

The cause: optimizing for **the training** rather than optimizing for **the outcome.**

## The fix — outcome-first

Flip it on its head:
1. Our number-one goal is to **change this metric / this behavior** which we think will have an impact on this metric
2. **Find THE ONE number** — too often people get lost in 50 different measures and nothing means anything
3. Find the proxy measure that says "is this successful or not"
4. Reverse engineer your way to impacting it

## What "reverse engineering" looks like

[00:55] Some of it might be:
- Training
- Dashboarding
- Going out and finding **exemplars** — what are the golden Gong examples that you can share?
- Constant drumbeat week in, week out **after** the actual training to keep it top of mind

## The 90% that matters — frontline managers

How are you leveraging frontline managers? At the end of the day, all of these initiatives **live or die** on how well they are bought in and how well they're enabled to carry that water.

**If you put 90% of your focus on training versus 90% of your focus on the long-tail activities → you'll have much worse results.**

## Pro tebe — meta-aplikace (sebevzdělávání)

Čteš Bay, Voss, Hormozi videa, knowledge base coache. **Bay's hot take přímo aplikovaný:**

Pokud dnes přečteš všech 30 Bay's videí a zítra si řekneš "tak teď to umím" → 10% retence za 24h. To je Ebbinghaus.

**Co s tím:**

**1. Pick ONE metric (tvoje verze):**
- Měřitelná metrika = **close rate / oslovené leady**
- Sekundární = **resolved-positive rate**
- → Tyhle dvě metriky sleduj měsíčně, ne 15 jiných

**2. Reverse engineer, co může impactnout close rate:**
- Lepší EVO odpovědi na "send me email" (Bay #002)
- Permission-based opener v hlasovkách (Bay's konkrétní phrasing)
- Triple Confirmation eliminace no-show (Bay's framework)
- → Každý měsíc focus na **JEDEN** z těchto, ne všechny najednou

**3. Drumbeat after training (ty sama sobě):**
Po přečtení Bay's videa NESTAČÍ. Musíš:
- Před každým callem 5 min refresh: re-read relevant Bay's md file
- Po každém callu 5 min self-review: "ve které části jsem zfailovala"
- 1× týdně review metrik ve tvé tabulce leadů / CRM

**4. "Frontline manager" = ty sama sobě:**
Bay říká frontline manager carries water. Pokud nemáš manažera, **najdi si sparring partnera** (kolega, společník, kamarádka z jiného oboru):
- 1× týdně 30 min review se sparring partnerem: "tenhle call jsem ztratila ve fázi 4 (prodej), proč?"
- I když sparring partner nedělá sales, jeho otázky jako 'nezaujatého' jsou hodnotné

**5. Aplikace pro klienta (training overrated):**

- 14-day check-in

- Měsíční report = drumbeat
- → To je to, co z tvé služby dělá "managed service", ne "tool"
