# Why prospects ignore your cold emails and cold calls (and how to fix it)

## Push vs Pull (the core distinction)

[00:00] There's two ways to approach outbound and selling. **Push method:** I'm going to take my solution, I'm going to push it onto the buyer. I'm going to try to get them interested in my world — features, functions, all of that. **Pull method:** instead of trying to get them in our world, let's meet them in their world first. Let's talk about what they care about, their priorities, their goals, how they get the job done, things that are painful or stressful for them. And then we lure them over to our world.

The problem: messaging is built **inside-out** versus **outside-in**. We launch a new product, think about its features, then how to talk to the market. Instead, think about the persona first — who's our buyer, what do they care about, how can we align our product to that?

When you don't do this, emails fall flat even if they meet length/guideline requirements. Cold calls fall short — buyer says "this doesn't really resonate" or "send me an email" and you don't get anywhere in the first 45 seconds.

## The data behind Customer Voice

[00:53] All the data supports this approach:
- **Gong's 300M cold call analysis + 30MPC:** problem-based language is nearly 3× more effective than buzzwords
- **Bay's Gong/30MPC email study:** buzzwords (AI platform, ROI) decrease reply rate by up to **57%** versus priority-based language

## Where to gather buyer language

[02:44] AI is only as good as the intel you feed it. Spoon-feed it real buyer language:
- Sales call recordings (find recordings of your ICP talking about their problems)
- Top reps' emails and talk tracks
- Customer success / implementation calls
- Customer interviews, fireside chats, case studies
- Job descriptions, LinkedIn profiles, industry reports
- Conversations with your internal version of that persona (e.g., if selling HR, talk to your CHRO)

## The 4-Component Messaging Matrix

[04:27] AI gets you 80–90% there. You need to know what good looks like to **red-pen** the output.

**1. Priorities** — what's top of mind for this persona? Imagine 12 of them in one room. "What are you most worried about? What's most important to you?" Example (sales leaders): AE self-sourcing — getting AEs to rely less on BDRs and marketing for pipeline.

**2. Current Solutions** — how is the buyer getting the job done right now? Typically people, process, or technology. Example: weekly mandatory prospecting blocks for the whole team.

**3. Problems** — how does the current solution make it harder to accomplish the priority, and what's the impact? Example: "everyone hates these blocks, no real enablement, training, content or coaching — we're taking the entire sales team off the floor for 4–8 hours and getting very few results."

**4. Aspirations** — 6–12 months after they buy your solution, what outcome do they want to feel like they got ROI? *(This is also a great discovery question:)* "Hey, let's zoom out. Let's pretend it's 6, 12 months from now. If you purchased our solution, what would need to happen for you to feel good about the ROI?"

## Cross-industry example (VP Clinical Ops)

[07:54] Same matrix, totally different vertical:
- **Priority:** start studies, quickly enroll participants, get product to market
- **Current solutions:** paper/Excel databases, competing survey tool, outsourcing to a CRO (consulting firm)
- **Problems:** paper = manual work; databases = double data entry; competing tools = expensive, need a software team to maintain; outsourcing = expensive + 3–6 month delay
- **Aspiration:** start studies ahead of schedule, get product to market faster

## AI-augmented workflow

[08:53] Run the matrix manually = ~1 hour per persona. Bay's prompt (copy-paste into ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini) + uploaded resources → minutes. Then 15–20 min red-penning.

Build at least 2 personas; that becomes the foundation for talk tracks and emails.

## Pro tebe — aplikace (salon + B2B)

**Persona: majitelka kosmetického salonu:**
- *Priorities:* plný kalendář, stálé klientky, které chodí pravidelně, spolehlivý tým, večery bez administrativy
- *Current solutions:* Instagram posty, automatické SMS z rezervačního systému, ruční připomínání přes WhatsApp, "doporučení od kamarádek"
- *Problems:* prázdná okna v týdnu, klientky, co si zarezervují a nepřijdou, večery nad zprávami a objednávkami, nemůže sehnat dobrou kolegyni do týmu
- *Aspirations:* kalendář plný na tři týdny dopředu, salon jede i bez ní, může si vzít volno a nic se nezhroutí

**Banned cold outreach phrases (buzzwords):**
- "automatizace", "ROI", "AI", "komplexní řešení", "digitální transformace"

**Customer voice equivalents:**
- "to prázdné okno v úterý odpoledne"
- "klientky, co si zarezervují a pak nepřijdou"
- "večery, kdy po zavíračce ještě hodinu odpovídáš na zprávy"
- "abys mohla jet na dovolenou a salon jel dál"

**B2B persona (jednatel menší stavební firmy):** Priority = stabilní tok zakázek a spolehliví lidi na stavbě; Current = doporučení + poptávkové portály + vlastní kontakty; Problems = výkyvy mezi zakázkami, cenové války na portálech, papírování zabírá večery; Aspirations = zakázky domluvené na půl roku dopředu, vybírat si klienty, ne honit každou poptávku.
