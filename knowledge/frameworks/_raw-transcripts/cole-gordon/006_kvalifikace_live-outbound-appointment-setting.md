# Live Outbound Sales Call — Full Appointment Setter Training

## Opening sequence (cold call, prospect resistant)

[00:00] Prospect: "Who is this? I'm super busy."

Cole's open (does NOT defend, doesn't apologize):
> "No worries. I just wanted to make sure you got the Setter Guide you downloaded from us yesterday. I'm Cole, owner of Closers.io — the company you downloaded the guide from."

Then frames the why:
> "One thing I want to do is reach out personally, understand where you're at, pair you up with the training based on whatever might be most helpful for you. Has anybody on my team reached out yet?"

Then asks for time:
> "Do you have a quick five minutes now? Then I can understand your situation and send you whatever I think will be most helpful."

Key: "quick five minutes" not "do you have time." Pre-frames as bounded.

## Discovery (only 4-5 questions to hit the gap math)

[02:00] Question 1: "Biggest challenge right now? What's not working at the level you feel it should?"
Prospect: "Time. Back-to-back meetings."

[02:32] Question 2: "Back-to-back doing what — sales calls? Fulfillment calls?"
Prospect: "Both, plus checking on clients."

[03:02] Question 3: "What's your revenue?"
Prospect: "$15k/mo."

[03:33] Question 4: "If you got time back, where would you spend it for biggest growth?"
Prospect: "Product development."

[04:03] Question 5: "How do you get clients now?"
Prospect: "Facebook group, post 4×/week."

[04:36] Question 6: "How many sales calls a week?"
Prospect: "3/day, ~60/month."

## The gap reveal (math out loud)

[05:41] Cole drops the bomb:
> "Let me ask you a question, because something's not adding up for me. You're taking ~60 sales calls a month at $1500/mo recurring. Your close rate has to be really low because if I took 60 calls a month, I'd close 20 — probably 30. Let's say 20. That's $30k. You said total revenue's $15k. Why do you think that is?"

Prospect makes excuses (follow-up, offer, retention).

[07:16] Cole calculates more:
> "Even at 20% close rate, that's 12 clients × $1500 = $18k recurring. At 30% close rate, that's 7 new clients = $10.5k additional MRR every month. You should be doing this. Right now you're at $15k total. Do you see the opportunity?"

## The 3-problem diagnostic

[08:16] Cole pivots into pillar mode publicly:
> "Three things I'm seeing. Push your next appointment back 5 min and I'll share. From what I'm seeing, you should be doing $30-50k a month pretty quickly, potentially $100k a month."

Then introduces credibility:
> "Not based on just my opinion — we scaled from 0 to $30M in 2 years, last month did $3.57M cash collected."

**Pen-and-pad command**:
> "Here, I'd write these down. Do you have a pen and pad?"

### Problem 1: Close rate

[09:18] "Even if you do 20 calls/mo, you should close 5-7. At $4500 package, that's $20k — more than you're doing now. Without seeing your metrics, I can tell your close rate is low. WHY THAT MATTERS: you can't delegate to a closer if your sales process isn't validated."

### Problem 2: Show rate

[10:49] "Your 30% show rate is bad. Real estate agents: 50% mediocre, 60-65% good, 70% exceptional. You're below standard. Few adjustments could get you to 50-60% — doubles your sales volume."

### Problem 3: Churn / CSM

[12:20] "Once you validate sales process + delegate + raise show rate, you're at $40-50k/mo. Then we fix churn by building a CSM team so you can deliver one-on-one fulfillment at scale without you being part of it. You agree there's not enough of you to go around?"

## Booking the call (don't pitch price)

[12:50]
> "I know you gotta go. Independent of whether you want to work with us deeper or not, let's set up another call. I can share the frameworks we use, also clients like Solar Exclusive doing $4M/mo and real estate agencies doing $100-500k/mo. Let's set a time and I can expand on these three things. Would that be okay with you?"

Prospect resists: "I tried closers, doesn't work for my offer."

Cole rebuts hard:
> "It doesn't work because you don't have the skill set. If I showed you seven real estate agencies all running your funnel with closers, would you agree it works? I can show you undeniable proof. The reason you're at $15k when you should be at $50k is a LACK OF SKILL SET. I don't say that to be hard on you — to inspire you."

## Price probe deflection

[14:22] Prospect: "What's the price?"
> "Everything we do is customized — answer is it depends. What probably makes most sense at this stage is let me outline on the next call the exact game plan we'd implement with you, then I can tell you the investment based on how much time it'll take working with you. Does that make sense?"

## Meeting confirmation (hard)

[16:25]
> "Cool. I just sent the calendar invite. One last thing — any chance you won't be able to make the call tomorrow? My time is super valuable so I want to make sure anyone I set up time with has an equal investment on both sides. As much as I know we can help you, you have to want to help yourself. Any chance you have to reschedule, or are we 100% good for that time?"

## Closing reframe (prospect apprehensive)

[17:25]
> "I don't mean to be hard on you to be hard on you. I mean to be hard on you because you're doing $15k/mo and getting 60 calls/mo. You could be getting 120 if you got time back, but you can't get time back unless we fix the sales process. You'd agree you're sitting on a really good opportunity, just not utilizing it? That's why I can't let you off the hook with 'I tried it, doesn't work' — when I have a myriad of other real estate agencies doing $400-500k/mo with sales teams. I don't say it to be down on you, to inspire you that there's something here much bigger."

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- US tonalita "I can't tolerate that" v ČR cringe. Změkčit: "To se mi nezdá konzistentní s tím, kde bys mohl být."
- Gap math 1:1 — Češi milují čísla, transparent math = trust.
- "My time is super valuable" → příliš arogantní pro CZ. Lépe: "Abychom oba nestrácel čas, potřebuju vědět, jestli 100% budeš na callu."
- "Pen and pad" command → "Můžeš si u toho dělat poznámky?"
- Closing reframe pro CZ: "Hele, neříkám ti to, abych byl nepříjemný. Říkám ti to proto, že máš na stole velkou příležitost a teď ji nevyužíváš."

## Pro tebe — aplikace (outreach)

Cold calling majitelům malých firem (ukázka: IT servis):

1. **Otevření** — "Krátká pětka teď, abych pochopila, kde s IT stojíte?"
2. **Discovery 4 otázky**:
   - "Kolik lidí u vás pracuje na počítači?"
   - "Kdy jste naposled zkoušeli obnovit data ze zálohy?"
   - "Kolikrát za rok vám něco vypadne (síť, server, e-mail)?"
   - "Když to vypadne, kdo to řeší a za jak dlouho?"
3. **Gap reveal s čísly** — "Tak hele, máte 20 lidí, výpadek vás potká zhruba 4× ročně na půl dne, to je 20 lidí × 4 hodiny × 4 výpadky = 320 ztracených hodin × ~500 Kč za hodinu práce = ~160 000 Kč ročně, co teče bokem. Vidíš tu propast?"
4. **3 problémy** — (1) žádná prevence, (2) neověřené zálohy, (3) hašení výpadků ručně.
5. **Book call** — "Pojďme si na 45min sednout, ukážu ti přesný game plan, jak to systematicky řešit. Cena? To probereme až po game planu — záleží, kolik máte počítačů."

Tonalita: méně "I can't tolerate" — víc "to je škoda toho potenciálu, co tam je."
