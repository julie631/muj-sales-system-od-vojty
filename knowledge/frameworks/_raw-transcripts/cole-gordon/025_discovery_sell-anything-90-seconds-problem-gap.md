# How to Sell Anything in 90 Seconds

## The First Principle

> "Business is about solving problems. When you solve a problem, you create value. And then customers exchange money for that value."

Implications:
- Sales = demonstration you can solve a problem
- No problem = no sale
- Sales conversation = finding the GAP between where prospect IS and where they WANT to be

## Two Problem Types (Gap Types)

1. **Pain** — subpar to par (overweight → healthy weight)
2. **Unfulfilled Desire** — par to excellence (healthy weight → six-pack)

Once you know which gap type, you can ask the rest.

## The 6 Belief-Installation Questions

After finding the gap (Pain), ask:
1. **Doubt**: What's been keeping them from fixing this problem on their own?
2. **Cost**: What's the consequence of not fixing the problem? What if nothing changes?
3. **Desire**: What's the payoff/result if they fix it?
4. **Money**: Do they have the resources AND willingness to fix it?
5. **Support**: Do other stakeholders/decision-makers support them in fixing it?
6. **Trust**: Do they trust your company AND your unique methodology?

> "But you can't answer any of those key questions until you first find the problem or the gap. Start with the problem. Then everything else becomes easier."

## Pro tebe — aplikace

Pre-call mental checklist (90-sec recall):

1. **Najdi GAP** — kde klient je vs kam chce (Hell vs Heaven Island)
2. **Pain nebo Unfulfilled Desire?** — má reálný problém (např. jazyková škola: poloprázdné kurzy, po semestru odchází 40 % studentů) NEBO chce growth (běží OK, chce escalovat)?
3. **6 beliefs**:
   - Doubt: zkoušel sám / přes někoho? Co nefungovalo?
   - Cost: kolik ho roční nečinnost stojí v Kč?
   - Desire: co s tím získá (víc tržby, klid, freedom)?
   - Money: má cashflow na {tvoje cena} + chce to platit?
   - Support: rozhoduje sám nebo s partnerem?
   - Trust: věří specificky TVÉMU řešení (tomu, čím se lišíš od generic alternativy), ne jen kategorii?

Pokud na všech 6 beliefs YES → close. Pokud kdekoliv NO → tam je rut.
