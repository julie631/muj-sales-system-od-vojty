# Advanced Selling Techniques — Roadmap

## Why Philosophy First, Tactics Second

> "If you can get the underlying philosophy and beliefs and mindset, everything else will start to come much more naturally."

If you focus only on scripts, you get lost when situations go off-script. Master philosophy → tactics flow naturally because you understand WHY.

## The Roadmap (16 videos)

### Module 1: Foundations
1. **Sales First Principles** — meta-level philosophy. How to THINK about selling. (Hell Island → Heaven Island, internal vs external pressure, six human needs, moment of decision)
2. **Belief Ladder (7 Beliefs)** — Pain, Doubt, Cost, Desire, Money, Support, Trust. Install via discovery → objectionless close.
3. **Inner Game** — mindset, mental models, beliefs about yourself
4. **Subcommunication & Tonality** — why two closers with same script get different results
5. **Habits of High Performers** — consistency, projections, ruts (covered in video 022)

### Module 2: Sales Process
6. **$50M Sales Process Overview** — one-call vs two-call close, industry adjustments

### Module 3: Pitch & Pre-Pitch
7. **Pitch Codex** — how to take your offer and communicate it on phone (covered videos 004, 019)
8. **Pre-Pitch** — the pitch before the pitch. During TRANSITION, reframe prospect so they believe the thesis BEFORE pitch.

> "The golf tee is the pre-pitch. The golf ball is the pitch. If selling ClickFunnels, pre-pitch sells funnels as best lead-gen method, pitch sells ClickFunnels."

### Module 4: Objections & Close
9. **Live Pitch Breakdowns** — call reviews
10. **Objections Philosophy** — 3 buckets only
11. **Money Objection** (covered 014)
12. **Spouse/Partner** (covered 018)
13. **Uncertainty Objections** ("I need to think") (covered 015)
14. **Advanced Closing Techniques** — loops, deposit closes, follow-up scheduling

### Module 5: Mid-Call & Follow-Up
15. **Advanced Reframing** — when prospects try to take control mid-call
16. **Follow-Up** — Cole's specialty. "I was known in our company as the most Savage follow-up guy."

### Module 6: Outbound
17. **MDR vs SDR**
   - **MDR (Marketing Development Reps)** = warming up inbound leads, hitting opt-ins
   - **SDR (Sales Development Reps)** = cold outbound to lists
18. **MDR Training** + **SDR Training** + call reviews

## The Three Broad Tactical Skills

> "If you get under the actual tactical aspects, there's the Discovery and how you ask questions, there's making sure you communicate the pitch properly, and then there's really how you close at the end."

Most reps:
- Botch the discovery (terrible/robotic questions)
- Or do everything right until the very end and botch the close (can't hold prospect accountable)

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- Funguje 1:1.
- Skvělý mental model: nejdřív philosophy, pak tactics. Obrácený postup nemusíš ošetřovat.
- MDR vs SDR = když děláš cold outbound sama, jsi sama sobě SDR. Až budeš najímat, rozhodni mezi MDR (hot warmup) nebo SDR (cold).
