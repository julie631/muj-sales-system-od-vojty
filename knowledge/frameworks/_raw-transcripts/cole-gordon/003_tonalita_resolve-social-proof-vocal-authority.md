# 3 Dark Psychological Sales Tricks To Sell ANYTHING

## Principle 1: Power Resolve

Level of certainty behind your words.

- NOT hyped-up energy ("Tony Robbins jumping up and down")
- IS quiet unwavering certainty (Michael Jordan flu game — sickest person in room, calmest under pressure)

### How to build it as a closer (not founder)

[01:31] Founders outsell sales teams because they LIVED the problem-solution journey. Their words have weight.

For closers: you don't need to know everything. You need to be **Savage expert on 1-3 core problems** your product solves.

> "Plastic surgeon operating on a billionaire CEO. CEO has higher status in many contexts, but in that specific context with that specific problem, the surgeon holds the power. Same thing happens when you shift into expert frame."

Implication: neprodáváš obecnou kategorii (např. "účetnictví") — prodáváš řešení 3 konkrétních problémů (např. penále za pozdní přiznání, přeplácení na daních, večery nad doklady). Být expertem JEN na ty tři.

## Principle 2: Seeded Social Proof

[03:02] Wrong way: prospect mentions problem → closer immediately launches case study sales pitch.

Right way: **seed during discovery, close loop during pitch**.

### Discovery seed (prospect mentions problem):

> "I totally get it. Have you actually heard of [XY Agency]? We used to work with them — they came to us and said a similar thing: if they could replace their realtor clients with high-end brokers they'd 4x revenue overnight because of higher LTV. Does that sound similar to what you're looking for? Okay great, I'll circle back to that later. Just rang a bell for me. Now tell me more about what you've tried so far."

You drop the **problem** + **stakes**, but leave **outcome open**. Creates curiosity loop.

### Pitch resolution (later in call):

> "Remember that agency XYZ I mentioned earlier? Same exact process we're implementing with you is the one we used for them. They got those higher-value brokers in — because of the higher LTV like we talked about, tripled revenue in 90 days."

### Why it works: Status Delta

[05:03] Status Delta = perceived authority gap.

> "Cop steps in front of you and holds up his hand. You stop. Not because he physically stopped you — because in that context he has much higher status delta and you defer."

When you've helped someone similar (or even MORE successful) than your prospect with the EXACT same problem, prospect defers.

Key: must feel natural, not pitched.

## Principle 3: Vocal Authority

Three components:

### (a) Range of expression

[06:35] Ancient tribal dynamic: leaders had most dynamic, free-flowing range. Lower-status members stifled expression to avoid challenging alpha (survival).

> "We literally evolved to associate dominant expression with leadership and authority. When you restrict your range, you come across as uncertain and untrustworthy."

Fix: **stop monitoring yourself**. Stop overanalyzing every word. Be 100% present, attention on prospect.

### (b) Cadence variance

[07:37] No monotone. Slow down + emphasize certain words. Speed up. **Use silence — sometimes just shut up.** Variance holds attention.

### (c) Inflection (3 types)

- **Upward** (voice up at end, like asking question) = uncertainty, seeking approval. AVOID.
- **Neutral** (level) = stating facts, building rapport.
- **Downward** (voice down at end) = authority, certainty.

### Comparison example

[08:39] Cole demos bad version (monotone, uptalk, hedging):
> "Hey so I really found to work best on these calls is understanding your business you know the mechanics of it how it works and ultimately what's keeping you from feeling where you want to be and once we figure out that I can give you some of my thoughts and if it makes sense we can definitely go into what you have going on over here and how that would work for you if you want to talk about…"

Good version (pauses, downward inflection):
> "Hey so what I found to work best on these calls is FIRST diving into understanding your business. So the mechanics of it. How it works. And ultimately — what do you feel like are the constraints keeping you from being where you want to be. NOW once we get clarity there, I can share some of my thoughts. And if it makes sense, we can definitely go into what we have going on over here. If that's something you want to talk about. But if not, I can point you in the right direction too. So that sound good?"

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- Quiet certainty 1:1 — funguje v ČR ještě líp, Češi jsou anti-cringe. Žádné teatrality.
- Savage expert on 1-3 problems → měj jasně definované svoje 3 pillars (3 konkrétní problémy, které řešíš). Tohle říkat klientovi až k znudění.
- Seeded social proof: tvoje case study je perfektní materiál — během discovery "Mimochodem, jedna klientka v Brně měla podobný problém — neměla ponětí, kolik ji to každý měsíc stojí…" — outcome ({tvoje case study s čísly}) drží na později.
- Vocal authority — Češi mají tendenci k uptalk na konci ("…tak jako jo?"). KILL IT. Downward inflection = "Tohle je řešení. Tečka."

## Pro tebe — aplikace

- Před každým call: připomenout si, že JSI expert na 3 problémy. Není to "trying to sell {tvoje služba}", je to "showing them what their business is missing".
- Discovery: seed {tvoje case study s čísly} bez resolve. Resolve až v pitch.
- Tonalita: nahrát si call → zkontrolovat (a) jsou tam pauzy, (b) jsou tam downward inflections, (c) nepoužíváš uptalk.
