# 11 Funnels That Generated $119M

## The Three Content Categories (applies to ANY platform)

Every post falls into:
- **Direct CTA** — "I'm looking for 5 people who want X. DM me."
- **Indirect (value + CTA)** — 80% value + tied to call-to-action at end. Twitter thread → CTA in last tweet.
- **Pure Value** — just value, maybe PS at end.

CTA produces TWO lead types:
- **Direct lead** — reaching out specifically to work with you.
- **Indirect lead** — reaching out for lead magnet, you pivot to sales.

## DM Combo Scripts (critical)

### Direct DM script (2-3 messages MAX)

1. Confirm intent
2. Ask 2-3 quick qualifying questions
3. Send NAKED calendar link → redirect to application

Never over-qualify in DMs. Most reps drop off 50%+ by interrogating. Sell the BOOKING. Qualify post-book on the app.

> "If they don't book that calendar link, you reach back out: 'Hey, I noticed you didn't book. Will you fill this out before the call so we can hit the ground running?'"

### Indirect DM script (longer)

For lead magnet leads — pivot conversation toward sales call. Longer script because not intent-based.

## Funnel 1: Facebook Organic

Cole got to $200-500K/mo on this alone (combined with Funnel 3 below):
- Friend list of 5,000 from previous sales career (Facebook request before every call)
- Daily/weekly posts: mix of direct, indirect, value
- One direct post in June 2020 produced $169K
- Worked because (a) IM community lives on Facebook, (b) timing un-saturated, (c) high price ($15K+) + 60-70% close rate.

Best for: starting off, IM community offers.

## Funnel 2: IG Boost Post

Post → boost → DMs/followers → script differs by lead type.

Works at all revenue levels. Best B2C, prosumer (e-com, fitness, dating, make-money-online).

Even at HIGH scale: dedicate 1 setter to DM every new follower — generates extra $100-200K/mo from ads you'd otherwise leave on table.

## Funnel 3: Facebook Group Funnel (now mostly dead)

Free resource → opt-in (name, email, phone) → thank-you page redirects to group → 4-second pixelled bridge for ad optimization.

In group: 1 live webinar/week + setter DM every new joiner + email broadcast sequence + setters calling.

Use NOW only if: green untapped B2C audience, existing FB group, or transferring big organic (YouTube/TikTok) audience to community.

## Funnel 4: DM Ads Funnel

1-3 min ad → DMs → direct messenger script (direct preferred) → calendar redirect to app.

Best for B2C and B-to-solopreneur. Whole funnel = the ad. Tests messaging fast.

## Funnel 5: Direct-to-App Funnel (Cole's favorite to scale)

Ad → application → calendar → call. NO DMs.

Best for B2B / SaaS / clear offers. Indirect-leading-to-direct ad structure works best at scale: 30 seconds content + pivot to direct offer.

Setter ratio: 1 setter per 2 closers (hits partial apps + no-shows + 2-grade apps).

> "What's strange — we tested putting our entire 25-min VSL as the ad direct to app. Crushed it. Some offers we ran a 36-min ad to app. Crushed."

## Funnel 6: Call Funnel (his bread and butter)

Ad → opt-in (name, email, phone) → VSL (direct or indirect) → app → call.

**70% of RCA closes came from setters CALLING opt-ins**, not opt-ins booking themselves.

Use Dialer.io for setters (best answer rates Cole tested).

If you remove the opt-in and conversions don't lift >30%, KEEP THE OPT-IN — you'll get way more bookings from setters + email + list build smooths ad volatility.

## KPI Benchmarks (universal formula)

Take your price → divide by 5 = target CPA.
Multiply target CPA × 22.5% close rate = max cost per LIVE call.

Reverse-engineer cost/booked-call from show rate (50% B2C, 60-70% B2B).

**B2C max**: $2K CPA + $300 cost/booked-call.
**B2B max**: $3K CPA + $400 cost/booked-call.

## Channel Desire (Eugene Schwartz)

Two strategies:
1. **Direct demand** — put offer in front of existing desire (sales recruiting → people looking for sales reps).
2. **Channel demand** — take existing demand, redirect to new opportunity.

RCA example: existing demand = "make money online." Cole channeled into "remote closing" by breaking limiting beliefs (you need a business → no, you just need this one skill).

> "If you can put your offer right in front of the desire, that works really well — that's what we do with sales recruiting. But when there's no existing market, channel existing demand."

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- CZ market scale ≠ US. CPAs benchmarks musí být násobeny ~5x níž ($2K CPA US → ~10k Kč CPA CZ).
- DM funnel pro lokální služby: Facebook organic + IG outbound funguje vč. cold DMs s hlasovkou.
- Call funnel až ve chvíli, kdy máš ads spend large enough.
- Channel desire princip — klienti nehledají tvou metodu, ale výsledek. Malé firmy nehledají "externí účetnictví", ale "klid od úřadů a přehled o penězích." Channel "chci mít klid" → "předej to někomu, kdo to pohlídá" → tvoje služba.
