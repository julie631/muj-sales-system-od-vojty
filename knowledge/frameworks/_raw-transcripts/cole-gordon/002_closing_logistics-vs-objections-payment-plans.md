# Close 20% More Deals With This Simple Strategy

## Core distinction: Objections vs Logistics

After tie-downs ("money aside, do you really want this?") you get one of two outcomes:

- **Objection** — prospect doesn't believe it's the right thing or right time. Uncertainty. (Handled in previous video.)
- **Logistic** — prospect 100% wants this, but real obstacle: money, time, spouse, partner, team, board.

> "They're 100% sold like dude, I want to do this — it's just THIS." That's a logistic. Don't sell more. Navigate.

## Money logistic — slow it down

Prospect: "I'm 100% in, just need to move money around. Give me 2 days."

Average closer: "Okay, let's schedule follow-up." → prospect might show, might not.

[02:00] Cole's slow-down probe:
> "When you say you need to move money around, what does that actually look like? Where is that coming from — selling off stocks, taking out of brokerage account? Walk me through that process A to Z. When is that process going to be finished and you're ready to go? Once you're ready to go, you're 100% moving forward on that day?"

Outcome: clarity → almost always you can collect SOMETHING now (1-2K), close the rest in a few days. **1K collected now > 50/50 chance they show next Wednesday.**

[03:00] If they don't fit standard 1/2/3 pay plans:
- Open Wallet probe → see total resources (cash on hand business + personal + credit + 30-day income)
- Custom split: half now + half in 2 weeks; or 1K down + remainder Friday + book onboarding when paid up.

## Time logistic — add-on as exchange

[05:34] Prospect: "Sounds great, want to start January 3rd after holidays."

Wrong: "Okay, talk to you then."

Right sequence:
1. **Isolate timing**: "Timing aside, you're 100% in?" → "Yes."
2. **Double tie-down**: "Just to be clear, if we're having this same exact conversation on Jan 3rd, you're 100% giving me your credit card moving forward?" → "Yes."
3. **Add-on as exchange**:
   > "I don't think the best thing for you is to wait and do nothing. I also don't think the best thing is to hop in right now if you're going to have a bunch of travel and can't fully take advantage. So what I'd be willing to do for you is add on 30 days into your engagement. That way you start now, knock out initial onboarding and items, get result one two and three. Then Jan 3rd we set your official start date — you hit the ground running. **If I'm willing to do that for you, basically adding on 30 days, are you willing to move forward right now?**"

[07:00] Key benefit: framework "eliminates any logical reason not to start today." If they still resist after this, you've smoked out the true (hidden) objection.

## Two common payment-plan mistakes

### Mistake 1: Lack of flexibility (offer-side problem)

[07:35] If your upfront cash collection >40-50%:
- Step 1: raise the price.
- Step 2: when you see resistance, ADD elongated payment plans (4-5 pays) for clients who need flexibility.
- Result: net more revenue. Example: $600 offer with 60% upfront → likely you can go to $900 with 40% upfront and net way more.

Risk: closers get lazy and turn every deal into a 5-pay. Sales manager must QC. Maintain 1-2-3 pays AS BASELINE + add new sales at 4-5 pays.

### Mistake 2: Payment plan ≠ objection handling

[09:06] Prospect: "I need to think about it."
Bad closer: "What if we do 4 payments of XYZ — would you move forward?"
Prospect: "I still need to think about it."

> "This is desperate and pathetic. As a manager, you have to QC this. They can't collapse and give away the farm at the first point of resistance."

Payment plan is a downsell, not a script for handling uncertainty.

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- Distinction objection vs logistic funguje 1:1.
- Slow-down probe ("walk me through that A to Z") v CZ ještě silnější — Češi neradi mluví o penězích, ale když to zarámuješ jako "abych ti mohla udělat férovou nabídku", projde to.
- 4-5 pay splits u CZ services typicky neexistují. Jednorázovou část ceny držet v 1 pay (jednorázově) + monthly subscription. Případně jednorázovou část rozdělit na 2 platby s exchange.
- "If I'm willing to do that, are you willing to move forward right now?" → "Pokud ti vyjdu vstříc s tímhle, jsme dnes domluvení?"

## Pro tebe — aplikace

- Když klient řekne "začneme po sezóně":
  1. "Časově stranou — chcete tohle udělat?" Yes.
  2. "Kdybysme za 2 měsíce měli úplně stejnou konverzaci, řekneme si jdeme do toho?" Yes.
  3. "Mám nápad — co kdybychom rozjeli onboarding teď, ať máš všechno připravené, a oficiální start dáme po sezóně? Tím získáš X dní setupu navíc. **Když ti tohle udělám, jdeš do toho dnes?**"
