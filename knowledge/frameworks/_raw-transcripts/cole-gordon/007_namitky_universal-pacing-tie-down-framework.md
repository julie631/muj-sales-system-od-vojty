# Handle ANY Sales Objection with this Simple Framework

## The universal stem

For any stall objection (think about it, too expensive, talk to spouse, talk to board, talk to dog):

### Step 1: Pace
> "No problem. Just so I can stay organized on my end…"

"No problem" diffuses pressure. "Stay organized" gives you a reason to probe.

### Step 2: Tie-down to thesis

Universal template:
> "[Objection] aside, is there anything else keeping you from being less than 100% certain that this is really what you need and now is the right time?"

Variants by objection:

- **Think about it (uncertainty)**: "Just so I can stay organized — what specifically is keeping you from being less than 100% certain that the four things we covered, in terms of getting your business to $20k/mo, is really what you need?"
- **Money**: "Money and everything aside, is there anything else keeping you from being less than 100% certain that this is what you need and now is the right time?"
- **Spouse/partner**: "Talking with your spouse aside, is there anything else keeping you from being less than 100% certain that this is what you need and now is the right time?"

### Step 3: Two outcomes

After tie-down, prospect either:
1. **Surfaces the real (hidden) objection** → great, now you handle it.
2. **Confirms it's just the stated logistic** → "Oh no, I really want to do it, it's just money/spouse/timing."

### Step 4: DOUBLE TIE-DOWN (require "100%" language)

> "Gotcha. So [logistic] aside — you're 100% in?"

CRITICAL: prospect MUST say "yes" with "100%" framing. This locks the thesis.

## Why we WANT the objection

[02:02]
> "The opposite of what salespeople do is — I really WANT the objection. If they have a hidden objection, it's not like they'll forget about it and accidentally buy. They're just not going to buy. So really, you need to find out what that is ASAP."

Most reps avoid asking because they're scared of hearing "no." Cole inverts: hidden objection = guaranteed lost deal. Surface it = fightable.

## Why pacing matters (no problem)

[02:32]
> "No problem — diffuses pressure. That's a good technique to remember. 'Just so I can stay organized' gives me a reason to probe. Then [objection] aside, is there anything else keeping you from being less than 100% certain this is what you need and now is the right time?"

For uncertainty objections specifically, drop the "aside":
> "No problem. Out of curiosity, what specifically is keeping you from being less than 100% certain that this is what you need and now is the right time?"

## Why thesis FIRST

Two sales in every call:
- Sale 1: The METHOD/THESIS (this approach is the right way)
- Sale 2: The PRODUCT (our company is how you do this approach)

> "Russell Brunson doesn't sell ClickFunnels — he sells that funnels are the most effective fastest best way to get customers online. Once he knocks down that domino, you obviously buy ClickFunnels."

> "If they're 100% bought in on the method — 'I 100% want to do this, it's just money / spouse' — now we're handling LOGISTICS, not OBJECTIONS. If we have to handle uncertainty post-pitch, it's very hard to close at the end of the call."

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- Univerzální stem 1:1.
- "No problem" → "Žádný stres" / "Klid" / "Jasně"
- "[Objection] aside" → "[Námitka] stranou…" funguje skvěle ("Peníze stranou…", "Manželka stranou…")
- "100% in" → "Jdeš do toho na 100%?"
- "Less than 100% certain" → "Co ti chybí, abys byl na 100% jistý, že je to to, co potřebuješ?"

## Pro tebe — aplikace

Typické námitky:

**"Musím si to rozmyslet"**:
> "Klid, nemusíš to teď rozhodovat. Jen abych měla jasno — když si představíš, jakou pomoc ti to může přinést, co ti chybí na 100% jistotě, že je tohle to, co potřebuješ?"

**"Je to drahé"**:
> "Jasně, chápu. Tak peníze stranou — kdyby cena nebyla téma, je tohle to, co ti chybí, abys {výsledek, který klient chce — jeho slovy}?"

**"Musím to probrat se společníkem"**:
> "Jasně. Tak hele, společník stranou — ty osobně do toho jdeš na 100%? Že je to to, co potřebuješ?"

Po YES odpovědi → double tie-down → pak řešit logistiku. Před YES → nemá smysl řešit cenu/spolejníka, prospect není sold na thesis.
