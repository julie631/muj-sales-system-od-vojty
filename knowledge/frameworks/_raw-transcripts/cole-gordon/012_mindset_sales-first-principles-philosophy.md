# Sales First Principles — Sales Philosophy

## Why philosophy, not scripts

> "If you become really reliant on exact words to say or scripts, you get thrown into unplanned situations and you get lost. When you have the right philosophy, all the masters have the right philosophy — you have behavioral flexibility because you can get thrown into any situation and still win."

## Why people buy: Hell Island → Heaven Island

[01:33] Prospects buy because they're trying to move from CURRENT situation to DESIRED situation. Your product = the bridge.

Hormozi Value Equation: highest perceived (dream outcome × likelihood) / (time × effort) = the bridge they buy.

## Surface level vs emotional level

Within both Current and Desired:
- **Surface/logical level** = the situation you can put your finger on
- **Emotional level** = why it actually matters

> "Prospects buy emotionally and justify logically."

## Six Human Needs (Tony Robbins)

[02:35]
1. **Certainty** — most powerful
2. **Variety**
3. **Significance** — most powerful
4. **Growth**
5. **Contribution**
6. **Love and connection**

Always certainty + significance are heaviest hitters.

### Example: Real estate agent on the "business rollercoaster"

Surface current: inconsistent lead flow → inconsistent revenue → inconsistent life.

Emotional current:
- Lack certainty (anxiety of not knowing where next lead comes from)
- Lack significance (not top agent, family doesn't see them as provider)
- Lack love/connection (family stress)
- Lack growth (same income 5 years)
- Lack contribution (better marketers beat better agents)

Desired = inverse + add specific numbers.

> "When I learned this and started weaving these emotional hits into my pitch, I could feel the difference in how features were received on the calls."

## Internal vs External Pressure

[07:11]

- **External pressure** = coercion, fake urgency, fast-action discounts ("normally 15K, today 10K"). Cole calls these "training wheels for newer salespeople." Loses respect of affluent prospects.
- **Internal pressure** = prospect doing it for THEIR OWN reasons.

Cole's advanced selling:
> "Lead the conversation in such a way using your questions that they come to the conclusion that they want to move forward. They're doing it for their reasons, not your reasons — at least in their mind, which usually is true anyway. You're just helping them come to that conclusion through your questions."

### How to build internal pressure: clearly defined Hell + Heaven Islands

The more clearly defined, the more TENSION between them. Tension = internal pressure.

Tony Robbins seminars create motivation through this same clarity. People come home pumped because they HAVE this clarity gap.

## Tactical: Assigning Numbers

[12:17] Always assign exact numbers. Most prospects identify with their best month, not their average.

> "Bad: 'What's your revenue?' → 'Oh, like 20K/mo.'
> Good: 'Last month exactly, what was your revenue?' → '5K.'
> 'Month before exactly?' → '8K.'
>
> 20K was once this year. They're not a 20K-month business owner. They did 5 last month. People deceive themselves more than the salesperson — that's why this matters."

Examples:
- Weight loss: "Weight this morning exactly?" / "Goal weight?"
- Dating: "How many dates last week exactly?"
- Energy: "Scale 1-10 today exactly? Goal 10?"

If no numerical anchor possible, default to scale 1-10.

## Painting the Picture (investigative reporter mode)

[15:50]
> "Imagine yourself painting a picture of the prospect's current and desired situation like an investigative reporter trying to paint it as clearly as possible. What are all the details? What do I see? What do I feel? What do I touch?"

This visual reframes the salesperson into a detective. Better questions emerge naturally.

## Moment of Decision

[15:50]
> "We all have a day or something that happens where we just say 'enough is enough.' That's what finally builds leverage to get us in action."

Re-elicit it:
> "Gotcha. So you said you've wanted to leave your 9-to-5 for years. Take me back to the day where you really drew the line in the sand and decided enough was enough. Walk me through that day — what happened?"

Examples Cole heard:
- Weight loss: daughter hopped on her lap and said "mommy you feel squishy"
- Income: shooter came to school where wife was teaching → "I vowed to retire my wife and never put her in a situation like that"

> "When they explain that experience, they're doing it for themselves to re-elicit all the emotions associated with that experience — which are guess what, the EMOTIONS OF CHANGE. They're telling themselves more than they're telling you."

## Your only two jobs on a sales call

[20:25]
1. **Understand if and how you can help the prospect** — paint Current and Desired situations as clearly as possible
2. **Eliminate ALL objections before the close** — destroy all alternative bridges (DIY, competitors), harpoon objection sharks (money, time, spouse)

### Kindergarten drawing model

- Hell Island → Heaven Island
- Multiple potential bridges (DIY, competitor A, competitor B, YOU)
- Sharks in the water = objections

Master closer: paints both islands sharply + destroys all bridges except yours + harpoons sharks in discovery.

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- Funguje 1:1 v ČR, jen ne tak hyper-energetické Tony Robbins zakroucení.
- "Last month exactly" — v ČR ještě silnější protože Češi mají tendenci nadnášet ("v sezóně dělám…"). Donutit konkrétno.
- "Moment of Decision" — kulturně funguje skvěle, lidi tu mají silné životní příběhy, ale ptát se s respektem ne jako interrogace.
- Internal pressure > external — perfekt pro CZ trh. Češi nesnášejí "akce do zítra do půlnoci" agresivní US sales. Internal logic = trust.
- Hell/Heaven Island visualizace osobně pro tebe při přípravě callu.

## Pro tebe — aplikace

Před každým call workout:

1. **Paint Hell Island** specificky:
   - Surface: kolik má klientů × % co odchází × ARPU × frekvence = roční loss
   - Emotional: stres z nevyužité kapacity, frustrace že lidi v týmu nemají co dělat, pocit že dosavadní investice = vyhozené peníze, neúcta k existující práci
2. **Paint Heaven Island** specificky:
   - Surface: kapacita na 80%+ obsazení, ARPU +20%
   - Emotional: certainty příjmů, significance (jiná úroveň business than competitors), growth (volný čas na expansion)
3. **Moment of Decision otázka**:
   - "Vzpomeň si na den, kdy ti došlo, že takhle to dál nejde. Co se stalo?"
4. **Numbers vždy exact**:
   - "Minulý měsíc přesně, kolik nových klientů jsi měl?" ne "obvykle".
5. **No fast-action discount** — tvoje garance je internal-pressure tool. Ne "do zítra sleva 20%". Místo toho "{tvoje garance}, žádné riziko."
