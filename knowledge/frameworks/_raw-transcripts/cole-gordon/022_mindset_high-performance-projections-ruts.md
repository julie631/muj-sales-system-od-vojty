# Keys to High Performance Sales (Advanced Techniques 06)

## Projections vs Goals

- **Goals** = aspirations (10x your year). Excite us, then let us down.
- **Projections** = bare-minimum standards. Reverse-engineered numbers you hit even if it hits the fan.

> "Projections give us accuracy. They keep everybody accountable. When mastered, this will give you and your salespeople control."

### How to Set Projections (weekly)

Every Friday set next week:
- **Inputs**: lead-gen activities, outbound, follow-up, calls planned.
- **Outputs**: closes, cash.

Cole's old spreadsheet pattern:
1. Decide target closes (e.g., 10).
2. HEDGE: aim for 30% above projection (13) in actual behavior.
3. Reverse-engineer needed inputs:
   - 13 closes ÷ 32% close rate = 40 offers
   - 40 offers ÷ 67% offer rate = ~59 live calls
   - 59 live calls ÷ 76% show rate = 77 scheduled

### The Andy Grove principle

> "Set them high enough that you miss 20-25% of the time. If you're missing all the time, there's an issue. If you're hitting all the time, you're not pushing yourself."

## 3 Reasons You Miss Projections

### Reason 1: Lack of Accuracy
Bad math. Expected 10 closes but only had 14 live calls / 11 offers. 10/11 = unrealistic at any normal rate.

### Reason 2: Insufficient Volume of Behavior
Off-call time NOT spent on outbound/follow-up.
> "If you're not on a call during the sales day, the only two things you can do are reach out to people you haven't talked to (outbound) or people you have (follow-up). That's it."

### Reason 3: Insufficient Efficiency of Behavior
Inputs were good, leverage wasn't. Either close rate or set rate dropped. Causes: skill gaps OR sales rut.

## Sales Rut Definition

> "A rut = 5% close-rate drop sustained over 30 days."

NOT a few bad calls. Regression to the mean.

> "You can be a phenomenal salesperson, take 10 live calls in a row, close zero. Then take next 5 calls, close 4 of 5. That's 28% combined — not bad. But during those 10 bad calls, less experienced reps GET IN THEIR HEAD and throw themselves into an actual rut."

## Qualitative Rut Diagnostic (3 areas)

Because we can't wait 30 days, check QUALITATIVELY in 1-on-1s.

### Area 1: PHYSICAL (usually the issue)

- Sleep — STANDARDIZED time, every day. Cole's biggest jump: 8:30pm bedtime / 5am wake every day → mediocre to top of team.
- Diet — energy + brain fog elimination
- Exercise — energy + mood
- Water
- Meditation — focus + presence on prospect (esp B2C high-emotion calls)
- Morning/night routines

> "Standardized sleep increased my closing ratio probably 50%. Massive. Then morning routine = exercise + water + breath work / meditation. Tons of peer-reviewed research on meditation increasing focus — it's not woo."

Key pitfall: rep starts physical routines, gets great results → stops → results decay (lag) → never connects decline to abandoned routine.

### Area 2: EMOTIONAL

External life events affecting presence:
- Family death
- Injury (Cole snapped a rib doing jiu-jitsu — worst sales month of year)
- Marital issues
- Kids
- Parents
- Relationships

Compartmentalize during call — "let the sales call be its own universe."

### Area 3: MENTAL

Sub-areas:

**Vision/Clarity**:
- Clear goals?
- Clear strong WHY?
- Progressing toward them?
- Pitfalls: vision too big (overwhelm), too small (no gap)

**Standards/Values**:
- Holding yourself accountable?

**Inputs** (information in = output out):
- Good inputs: past wins calls, sales content, industry acumen
- Bad inputs: distractions (Cole's example: obsessing over getting into real estate threw him into rut)
- Bad inputs: ONE upset client message can throw a rep into a full rut despite 90% client success

**Maturity** — comparison culture, social media trap. Constantly chasing the next thing, never satisfied. Healthy mindset = steady.

## The 9-Month Rep Syndrome (Neil Rackham, SPIN Selling)

Performance pattern:
- 0-3 months: ramp up
- 3-7 months: peak
- 9+ months: performance falls

Why: experienced reps STOP doing full discovery. They assume they know prospect's problems (because they've heard thousands).

> "It's not about you understanding the prospect — it's about the prospect saying things OUT LOUD so they say it to themselves and feel understood."

Cole's same observation on his teams. Rut diagnosis #1: Discovery laziness.

## Aside: Hard truth on off-call time

> "Most mediocre salespeople — if they have 5 live calls but 2 don't show, those 2 hours of free time go to lunch, walks, podcasts. NO. You need to be working. Outbound or follow-up. Take a 20-min break to refresh, fine. But mostly: work."

## Pro tebe — aplikace

### Týdenní projection šablona

Cíl: {X} closed/měsíc → {X/4} closed/týden → hedge nahoru (cca +50 %).
Reverse engineer ze svých real funnel dat:
- closed/týden × 1/close rate = offers/week
- offers × 1/offer rate = live calls/week
- live calls × 1/show rate = scheduled calls/week
- scheduled × 1/lead→call rate = opening sent/week

Ilustrace: cíl 2 closed/měsíc → 0.5 closed/týden → hedge na 0.75 closed/týden. Při 25% close rate, 50% offer rate, 60% show rate a 10% lead→call rate: 0.75 closed → 3 offers → 6 live calls → 10 scheduled → 100 opening sent/week.

Když ti vyjde hugely ambiciózní číslo, stanov realistický target (třeba polovinu) — ale pořád nad svým currentem.

### Rut diagnostika

Před každým týdnem sebe-check:
- **Physical**: spala jsi 7+ hodin? Cvičila 3+×/týden? Pila vodu? 5-min ranní rituál?
- **Emotional**: jak to mám s parťákem / týmem? S klienty? Žádný nedořešený stres?
- **Mental**: vize (konkrétní cílový počet klientů) jasná? Daily inputs (sales content, klient calls) > distractions?

### Riziko 9-month syndromu

Po několika měsících se stejným typem klientů přichází kolem 9-month mark hlavní riziko: assume, že už víš, co klienti potřebují → discovery laziness → close rate dropne.

Prevention: po každém callu vyplnit 7-belief checklist (z file 011) → nutí asking-not-assuming.
