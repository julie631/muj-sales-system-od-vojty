# Stop Losing Sales Over Price, Say This Instead (Live Roleplay)

## Kontext

Live roleplay s členem Coleova týmu. Prospect (Aaron) má business a chce na 100k/měsíc. Pitch 10K. Prospect: "It's just a lot of money for this."

## Sequence step-by-step

### 1. One probing question

[00:00] Pitch dropnut: "The investment is just 10K."
- Prospect: "Yeah dude, that's just a lot of money for this."
- Cole: **"In what way?"**

Jen jedna otevřená otázka. Ne výslech. Necháš proudit.

### 2. Money aside — double tie-down

[00:31] Po prvním "objection feel" Cole **odložil rukou peníze stranou** (i fyzicky) a řekl:

> "All of that aside, Aaron, do you really want to do this? Take the money aside for a moment. Do you actually feel like this is what you need to get to 100 grand a month?"

Klíčové:
- **Direct eye contact**, **strong + certain language**.
- Tonalita musí být tak silná, že to prospekta **téměř nutí říct ne**, pokud ne. Většina sales lidí to říká soft — pak prospect řekne "yeah I just need to think about it."
- Pokud prospect váhá → **call it out**: "You seem a little hesitant." (mind reading)

[01:03] Prospect potvrdí: "Yeah, 100%." → **double tie-down** ("Money aside, you're 100% in." → "Yep.").

### 3. Permission frame

[01:36] Set up the conditions:
> "Most of our clients do it upfront. For certain clients like you who really want to do it, it's just finance standing in the way. In some cases, we do break it up. But for me to do that, it's all customized and depends on what you need. So are you open to just having an honest conversation financially, getting everything on the table so we can see if this can actually work for you now? Or at the very least, we can create a game plan for you to work towards in the future. Is that a conversation you're willing to have?"

Klíčové:
- Affirmuješ "you really want to do this" + "money is the ONLY thing in the way"
- Permission ("are you open to…")
- Give them out ("now OR in the future") — odlehčí to tlak

### 4. Financial probe (cash flow + cash on hand + credit)

[02:06] Cole se ptá ve 3 krocích:

1. **"What's your net incoming cash in the next 30 days?"** (after business expenses, drops into savings)
   - Throwaway question — helps structure future payments, but main use: warm-up.
2. **"What's your cash on hand right now?"** ("Both — personal and business." Don't let them split it.)
3. **"Do you have access to credit?"** (only if cash on hand insufficient)

Cole's framing trick: prospect často odpoví **"what they can spend"** (např. 1500) místo **"what they have access to"**. Cole pak řekne: "I'm not asking what you could spend right now. I don't even know if you could do this right now. We may be creating a game plan for the future. I'm asking what you actually have access to."

### 5. Re-isolate + reframe cheaper competitor

[03:39] Prospect: "I talked to a guy that's 5K, would be less out of rainy day fund."
- Cole: **"If the prices were equal, which do you think is going to work better — the 5K or what we're doing?"**
- Prospect: "What you guys are doing."
- Cole: "Then is it really a decision? Would you rather work with somebody who's actually going to get you the result, or the cheapest guy on the block?"

### 6. Spouse/partner step-aside [05:11]

Prospect: "I'd have to talk to one of the partners."
- Cole: "Is there an alternative? You're acting like there's an alternative if you have to tap into the account."
- Probe: "You're the one responsible for marketing and sales? You're 50/50? He knows you're on this call and trusts your judgment on marketing/sales investments?" → Yes, yes, yes.
- Frame the worst case: "What's the worst case scenario with your partner? He's pissed? What are the chances?"
- **Risk reversal**: "If you come in for the 10K right now, set up onboarding tomorrow. If your partner is pissed, we'll talk best next steps. Worst case I give your money back. I don't care."

### 7. Payment split AS EXCHANGE

[20:00] Když musíš break-up dělat:
- Sequence: "I don't think the best thing for you is to come in right now and spend your entire life savings. I also believe the best thing is NOT to do nothing. So what I'd be willing to do for you is break it into two. **If I was willing to do that for you, are you willing to move forward right now?**"
- Klíčové: NIKDY nedávej slevu/split bez exchange. 99% sales lidí to dá zadarmo → prospect řekne "great, let me think about it."

## Tonality & subcommunication

- **Have fun with it**. "Most sales people can't even do that because they're so emotional. They put their selfworth on the line." Detached posture = můžeš pressure harder.
- **Use your hands** — slow, deliberate. Cole's reference: na live eventu prezenter udělal experiment — když lidé pitchovali bez rukou, místnost byla tichá; když mohli použít ruce, hlas přirozeně zesílil a stal se víc persuasive.
- **Mind reading**: pokud vidíš váhání v body language, call it out. "You seem a little hesitant." Builds trust because "they can't put anything past you."
- **Stop selling after you isolate**. Když prospect řekne "yes, I want this", **přestaň prodávat**. Justifikace = "tell of insecurity."

## Klíčový princip: uncertainty first, money second

> "I always handle uncertainty first, which is the whole reason I do those tie-downs is to root out the uncertainty so that I can handle that first and then I always cycle back to money."

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- Sequence funguje 1:1.
- Tonalita: změkčit "ready to go?" → "dává to smysl?", "Are you in?" → "Tak co, jdeme do toho?"
- Cash flow probe je v CZ kontextu OK, ale rámovat ještě obchodněji: "Abych ti mohla udělat férovou nabídku, potřebuju vědět, kde stojíš finančně."
- "Money aside, do you really want this?" → **"Peníze stranou — opravdu chceš tohle udělat?"** funguje skvěle, je to čitelné a profesionální.
- Risk reversal ("worst case I give your money back") — pokud máš garanci ({tvoje garance}) → použít stejně.

## Pro tebe — aplikace

- Při ceně {tvoje cena} — pokud prospect řekne "to je hodně":
  1. "V jakém smyslu?"
  2. "Peníze stranou — opravdu chceš {výsledek, který klient chce — jeho slovy}?"
  3. (po yes) "Tak jasně, dává to smysl. Co tě teda fakt brzdí — peníze, načasování, nebo něco jiného?"
  4. Nikdy nesnižovat cenu bez exchange. Spíš payment split: "Můžeme to rozdělit na dvě platby, ale potřebuju vědět, že jdeš do toho hned."
