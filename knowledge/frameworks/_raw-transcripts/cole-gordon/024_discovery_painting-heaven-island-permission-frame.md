# How to Paint Heaven Island (Goals Section)

## Two discovery syntaxes

- **Problems-first syntax** — for prospect with PAIN (subpar to par). Start with what's wrong.
- **Goals-first syntax** — for prospect with UNFULFILLED DESIRE (par to excellence). Start with what they want.

Choice depends on prospect type.

## The 4-question Heaven Island sequence

### Question 1: Ultimate Goal (most open-ended)

> "Ultimately what's the goal?"

Most open-ended on purpose. Reveals what prospect actually wants — could be money, impact, lifestyle, legacy, anything.

Cole's classic example: selling to real estate agents, expected real-estate goals. One agent answered: "I want to build a non-profit to help kids in Africa." Cole would have NEVER gotten that info if he asked "what's your revenue goal."

### Question 2: Assign a Number

Concrete data point. Income → "$ per month exactly?" Dating → "Dates per week?" Weight → "Target weight?"

If no numerical possible:
> "Give me an example of what life looks like when you blank. Walk me through what that would look like day to day."

### Question 3: Qualify Their Answer

This is the consistency bias trick.

Bad (probe): "Wow John, $20K/month! What's that going to do for you?"
Good (qualify): "Seems like you've thought of that before. If I can ask — why that number?"

> "When I probe, it's more for ME. When I qualify their answer, it's more for THEM. They explain it to themselves. That's the consistency bias at work."

Cialdini's principle: humans drive to APPEAR CONSISTENT with prior statements. Get them to qualify out loud → they're now committed to that goal.

### Question 4: Long-term Vision

> "Overall, what's the long-term vision for your [business / dating life / health]?"

Skip if Question 1 already gave you the long-term vision.

### Question 5: Tie to Non-Monetary Area (4-trigger frame)

CRITICAL — most reps fail here. Bad framing: "John, how would that affect another area of your life?" — probing.

Good framing (4 persuasion triggers stacked):

> "John, can I ask you a personal question? The reason I'm asking this is because what's not only important to me is helping you build a real estate business that's building you wealth, but also one that's empowering you to live whatever lifestyle that you want to live. So when you think about that — what comes up for you? Like what are the non-monetary goals, what are the personal goals that you want your business to allow you to achieve?"

Breakdown:

1. **PERMISSION** — "Can I ask you a personal question?" Compliance trigger 1.
2. **PRESUPPOSITION** — "personal question" not "question" — pre-loads them to give personal answer. Trigger 2.
3. **REASON WHY** (Cialdini) — "The reason I'm asking…" Any reason boosts compliance (Cialdini copier study). Trigger 3.
4. **PREEMINENT FRAME** — "what's important to me is empowering you to live whatever lifestyle you want" — frames the question in THEIR best interest. Trigger 4.

Then ask the question.

> "You see how that's tremendously better than 'John, what's that going to do for you personally?'"

## 4 non-monetary areas to probe

1. Relationships
2. Health and well-being
3. Spirituality / fulfillment
4. Lifestyle / freedom

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- "Ultimately what's the goal?" → "Co je vlastně ten finální cíl?"
- "Seems like you've thought of that before — why that number?" → "Vidím, žes o tom přemýšlel. Proč zrovna tohle číslo?"
- 4-trigger frame:
  > "[Jméno], můžu se tě zeptat na osobní otázku? Protože pro mě je důležitý, abys nejenom zaplnil ten kalendář, ale taky abys měl život, jaký chceš mít. Co tě napadá — co by ti to mohlo přinést mimo peněz?"

## Pro tebe — aplikace

Heaven Island sequence — ukázka pro majitele fyzioterapeutické praxe (převeď na obor svých klientů):

1. **Ultimate Goal**: "Co je tvůj finální cíl s touhle praxí?"
   - Může to být: vlastní síť poboček, freedom finančně, brand, work-life balance — nemusí to být jen "víc pacientů"
2. **Number**: "Kam chceš dostat měsíční obrat?" (např. 300k Kč)
3. **Qualify**: "Vidím, že je to konkrétní číslo. Proč zrovna 300k?"
   - Tady odhalíš osobní reason — splátka úvěru, rodina, expansion, status mezi peers
4. **Long-term vision**: "A za 3 roky kam tohle směřuje?"
5. **Non-monetary tie (4 triggers)**:
   > "Honzo, můžu se zeptat na osobnější otázku? Protože pro mě je důležitý, abychom ti nepomohli jenom naplnit ordinační hodiny, ale aby ti ta praxe přinesla život, jaký chceš. Co ti dnes nejvíc utíká — víc času s rodinou? Konec stresu z prázdných termínů? Možnost dělat byznys, ne provoz?"
