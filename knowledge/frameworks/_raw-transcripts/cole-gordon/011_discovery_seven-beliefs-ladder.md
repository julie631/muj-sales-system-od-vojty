# 7 Beliefs Your Prospect Needs to Buy

The Belief Ladder — Cole's #1 framework. If you install all 7 via discovery questions, prospect closes themselves.

## Belief 1: PAIN

Two types of problems:
- **Pain** — below-average to average (knee pain → no pain)
- **Unfulfilled desire** — average to excellence (healthy → longevity, peak energy)

Both work, but you sell each TYPE differently. For pain prospects, twist the knife. For desire prospects, paint the future.

> "Business is solving problems. Solving problems creates value. People exchange money for value. So sales is just demonstrating you can solve a problem. If there's no problem, there's no sale."

## Belief 2: DOUBT

> "Doubt means the prospect believes the outcome is possible BUT only with your help."

If they lack doubt → DIY objection ("I'll do it myself").

### Doubt exists on a spectrum: the "Buying Pocket"

- **Too far LEFT** = they don't believe it's possible AT ALL → inspire with stories (your story, client stories, statistics)
- **In pocket** = "possible, but only with your help" → sweet spot
- **Too far RIGHT** = "I can do this myself" → open the gap with skilled questions:
  - "How long have you been trying to fix this?"
  - "What's been the hardest part?"
  - "What else have you tried specifically? Why didn't that work? What was missing?"

## Belief 3: COST

> "Cost belief = prospect believes the cost of doing NOTHING is significantly more painful than the cost of doing your solution."

If missing → "Let me think about it" / no urgency.

### Pain Action Threshold Matrix

- Bar 1 (small, left): pain of investing in your solution (time + money)
- Bar 2 (large, right): current + future pain of doing NOTHING

Critical: bar 2 must be 10× bar 1, not slightly more. Must cross the **Action Threshold** for "I want to think about it" → "let's do it now."

## Belief 4: DESIRE

> "Pain, doubt, cost fuel the prospect's energy to change. But they'll only change if they can channel that energy into a specific desired outcome."

Cialdini study: pain-only message vs pain-and-gain message → pain-and-gain far more persuasive.

Don't just twist the knife. Paint the exciting future too.

## Belief 5: MONEY

Two components:
1. **Resources** — physically have funds/credit (diagnose via qualification early)
2. **Willingness** — will spend it on this

Example: attorney with $500k income offered $5k dating coaching. Has resources, lacks willingness.

> "Willingness is actually a smokescreen for a LACK OF COST BELIEF. Distinguish: is it a logistical financial objection (no resources) or a smokescreen for uncertainty (no cost belief)?"

## Belief 6: SUPPORT

Spouse, partner, board, procurement, team.

When this comes up, check: is the person you're talking to 100% sold themselves?

If yes → get on same side of table:
- "What do you think [stakeholder] is going to think?"
- "Do they agree this is a problem?"
- "What would they say about the investment?"
- "Do they know you're on this call? What would they think if they did?"
- "Have you basically already decided you're doing this and you're just letting them know? Or are they a key factor in the decision?"

## Belief 7: TRUST (the most important)

> "Trust = prospect sees your solution as unique, different, and superior to other things they've tried in the past."

Without trust: "I've been burned before" / "How is this different?" / "Sounds similar to what I've tried."

### Two sales in every call

- **Sale on the METHOD** (thesis) — 80% of your effort
- **Sale on the PRODUCT** — 20%

Russell Brunson example: 80% of stage presentations are selling that funnels are the best way to get customers online. Once you believe that, ClickFunnels is the natural byproduct.

For viral video training: "80% should be selling that creating viral videos is the best fastest most effective way to make money online vs alternatives (dropshipping, Amazon FBA, coaching). If they believe THAT, your specific viral-video training is the natural buy."

## How to use this on a call

1. Discovery questions install Pain, Doubt, Cost, Desire.
2. Qualification confirms Money.
3. Discovery probes Support stakeholders.
4. Pitch installs Trust (method sale via pillars).
5. By close → no objections, prospect self-closes.

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- Koncept 100% univerzální, no adaptation needed.
- Pain vs. Desire: most established owners = unfulfilled desire (mají businesss, chtějí ho mít plnější), ne čistá pain. Lidé, co odcházejí z korporátu do vlastního podnikání = obojí (chtějí escape z corp = pain, chtějí success = desire).
- 7 beliefs check-list před každým pitch:
  1. Pain — co tě sere?
  2. Doubt — věří, že to lze? (S mojí pomocí?)
  3. Cost — chápe, kolik ztrácí tím, že nic nedělá?
  4. Desire — vidí, co tím získá?
  5. Money — má na to + chce to platit?
  6. Support — bude do toho jít sám, nebo má partnera?
  7. Trust — věří v MOJI metodu vs alternatívy?

## Pro tebe — aplikace

7 beliefs checklist před každým callem (ukázka: firemní fyzioterapie):

1. **Pain**: "Kolik lidí vám ročně vypadne kvůli zádům? Vidíš to na nemocenských?"
2. **Doubt**: "Zkoušel jsi to řešit sám / přes někoho? Co nefungovalo?"
3. **Cost**: "Když to spočítáme: 5 lidí × 10 dní nemocenské × ~4 000 Kč na den = 200 000 Kč ročně mimo. Jak dlouho takhle ještě chceš jet?"
4. **Desire**: "Představ si: lidi bez bolesti, žádné výpadky v sezóně, tým, který nechce odcházet. Jak by ses cítil?"
5. **Money**: "Při {tvoje cena} se to obvykle vrátí za {za jak dlouho — jen pokud to umíš doložit}. Cashflow ti to vychází?"
6. **Support**: "Rozhoduješ sám, nebo to musíme probírat s někým dalším?"
7. **Trust**: pillars během pitche (prevence > hašení = thesis, naše metoda > jednorázové masáže = product).
