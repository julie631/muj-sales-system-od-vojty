# Sales Was Scary Until I Rewired My Brain

## The trigger story

[01:01] 2018 — Cole's first sales job. Selling lead-gen to real estate agents over 55. Hardened sales-resistant prospects. He was the worst person on the team. Kept only because coachable + worked hard.

Walking through Nashville one night listening to his own call. Prospect said "I'll think about it." Cole heard himself emotionally cave and skip the objection handling he knew he should do.

Realization: **"On every single sales call I was putting my own self-worth on the line. Unconsciously saying — if I close, I'm good. If I don't, I suck."**

The math: even closing 40% (top of team), still rejected 60% of time = "feel like crap 60% of the time" — doesn't make sense.

So he asked: "If I can't control yes/no, what CAN I control? And how do I make every call gradable as win/loss regardless of outcome?"

## The 3 Rules

### Rule 1: Grade the PROCESS, not the outcome

After every call, stop asking "did I close them?" Instead:
- Did I fully flush out discovery?
- Did I surface ALL the objections BEFORE the pitch?

Why: all objections boil down to the 7 core limiting beliefs (Pain, Doubt, Cost, Desire, Money, Support, Trust). When you dismantle them in discovery before pitching, you create the OBJECTIONLESS CLOSE.

> "Great closers don't OVERCOME objections — they PREVENT them."

### Rule 2: Sell the THESIS, not the product

Every sale has 2 parts:
- Thesis — the idea/belief/method of transformation (90% of success)
- Product — actually fulfills the method (10%)

Russell Brunson on stage = 80% selling that funnels are the best way to get customers online → ClickFunnels is the byproduct.

Self-grade question:
> "Did I get to the end of the call with prospect 100% bought in? Like 'man I really want to do it, just need to figure out the finances' or 'really want to do it, just need to consult my partner'? Some deals fall through cracks, but if I leave the call and they're 100% bought in, there's nothing else I could've done."

### Rule 3: Hold them ACCOUNTABLE to who they said they wanted to be

A lot of prospects want success, but when push comes to shove at end of call their actions don't match their words.

> "The best objection handling isn't manipulation — it's accountability."

Stem:
> "Hey, can I ask you something honestly? Earlier on the call you told me this was your number one priority. But now you're saying you want to wait till end of next quarter. So what's really going on?"

You can't control if they buy. You CAN control whether you held them accountable to what they said they wanted.

### Bonus Rule: Enjoy the game

> "The more you can learn to enjoy the gamification and the game of sales itself, the more fun the calls will become. You stop fearing rejection. You start getting more curious and you actually enter flow state."

Research: more fun = easier to enter flow state on challenging tasks.

## The overall reframe

> "Detach from the outcome, reattach to the process. Every call becomes a win you can grade. Doesn't mean you win every call — there'll be times you screw up. But there'll also be times you don't make the sale but look back and say 'dang, that was one of the best calls I ever did.'"

## Pro tebe — aplikace

Tvůj call review framework po každém callu:
1. **Process grade** (5 otázek):
   - Pochopila jsem Hell Island (current state, all 5 emotional layers)?
   - Pochopila jsem Heaven Island (specific desired state, čísla)?
   - Zjistila jsem Moment of Decision?
   - Otestovala jsem všech 7 beliefs?
   - Surfacenula jsem hidden objections PŘED pitchem?
2. **Thesis grade**:
   - Skončil call s prospect 100% bought-in na thesis (tvoje 3 pilíře value)?
3. **Accountability grade**:
   - Když prospect couvnul, držela jsem ho k tomu, co řekl na začátku?

Když všechny 3 grades = ✅, ale prospect nekoupil → "one of the best calls" — můžeš pokračovat detached. Když některý = ❌, tam je improvement.

Tohle přímo pomáhá tvé mentální zátěži — i solidní close rate (třeba 20 %) znamená, že zhruba 80 % hovorů skončí "ne". Takový rejection rate by tě v US emotional mode rozsekl. Process grading drží sanitu.
