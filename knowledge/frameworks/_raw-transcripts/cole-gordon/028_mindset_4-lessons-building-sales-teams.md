# 4 Lessons From Building FOUR 8 Figure Sales Teams

## Lesson 1: Star vs Superstar Hiring

### 3 traits to hire

1. **Specialized Knowledge** (Naval) — knowledge from DOING the thing. Not books. Sales experience in YOUR sales TYPE.
   - Transactional (high-ticket coaching, weight loss, fitness) — 30 days or less
   - Relational (real estate, mortgage brokers)
   - Enterprise (9-month cycles, SaaS, big-ticket)
   - Don't put an enterprise SaaS rep on a transactional weight-loss offer.

2. **Industry Acumen** — speaks market's language naturally.
   - 40-yr-old grizzled BizOp closer on hormonal-women's offer = bombs.
   - Existing top client with some sales experience + train them on sales > pure salesperson without industry context.

3. **Buy-In** — non-negotiable. Mission/Vision/Values alignment.

### Superstar vs Star

- **Superstar** = all 3. Very rare.
- **Star** = buy-in + ONE of (specialized knowledge OR industry acumen).

### The key decision question

> "What can YOU train better internally?"

Cole's case: he's a professional sales trainer. So he hires people with HIGH INDUSTRY ACUMEN + buy-in, trains sales internally. (Best closer ever = no sales experience, came in via business consulting background.)

Client case (Jasmine, real-estate expert, not natural salesperson): she hires SALES VETERANS, trains them up on real-estate industry.

## Lesson 2: Professional Interviewers vs Professional Salespeople

### Group Interview Hack

Process: Application → Video App → **Group Interview** → Screening (Discovery) → Mock (optional) → Scorecard (closing them on the role).

Group Interview specifics:
- 10 candidates in Zoom gallery view
- 1/week × 4 weeks = 40 candidates/mo
- **Comparison bias** = studs vs duds obvious in 2-5 minutes

What to watch for:
- Who takes initiative?
- Original responses vs copying others?
- Timid vs aggressive?

End: DM the 2-3 you want to stay. Roll into 1-on-1s.

### Competency-Based Questions (CBI)

vs Situational ("If I hired you, how would you build pipeline?") — they can prepare/fake.

Competency = "What have you DONE?" Track record. Charlie Munger's CEO hiring principle.

**Examples (use 2-3 per interview MAX)**:

> "Tell me about the last 2 deals you won with an extreme proactive sales effort. Walk me through them end-to-end, step by step. What happened?"

> "Were there times at [previous company] where lead flow wasn't up to par? Give me an example of that month. Specifically what actions did you take to still hit your numbers despite bad lead flow?"

> "What was your worst performing month? How many units did you sell? How does that compare to your best month? So that was 50% of what you're capable of. What specifically did you learn? How did you bounce back in [next month]?"

> "You can't fake the answer to that question. You only need 2-3 to know if they're real or fake."

## Lesson 3: Create a Prodigy

### The Old Way (wrong)

Focus public-meeting time on WORST reps. Constant call reviews of their bad calls. Result:
- Negative meeting energy
- Investing time in highest-churn-risk people
- Top performers tune out (not relevant to them)
- Top performers eventually leave because "I'm not growing anymore" (Cole's #1 reason elite reps leave)

### The New Way

Spend at least 50% of PUBLIC meeting time on BEST reps.

Benefits:
- Top reps still in their growth groove
- Top rep's mistakes are relevant to whole team
- Praise reinforces what's working
- Newer reps MODEL the top rep
- Better meeting energy
- You're training your next sales manager pre-promotion

> "Sales reps learn through MODELING. The easiest way for them to learn is point at the gold standard rep and say 'model this. Review this call. Transcribe it by hand if you have to. Copy it.'"

## Lesson 4: Promote Top Rep Without Losing Production

### Fact 1: Short-term hurts no matter what

If your best candidate for manager IS your best rep producing 30-40% of revenue, production drops short-term. Better long-term because other reps come up.

### Fact 2: Player-Coach DOESN'T WORK in Sales

> "Comp is mostly tied to production → they prioritize production over coaching. They lead by example → if production lags they chase production not coaching. Sales has NO clear boundaries (always more outbound, more follow-up). So they tunnel-vision on the next deal."

### 3 Tactics to Recover ~Half Production

#### Tactic 1: Manager Callbacks on Fringe Deals

**Fringe Deal** = deal that should've closed but didn't. Lowest-hanging fruit on the tree.

How to find:
- End-of-day reports from each rep: 3-4 sentences per call (prospect, quality, what happened, next step)
- Read next morning → identify the ones rep wrote "should come in tomorrow / great call"
- Manager reviews those calls → spots the gap

Then manager picks up the phone:
> "Hey [Prospect], sorry — I know the conversation with [Rep] went like this. I think they missed key components. Do you have 5 minutes? I want to correct the conversation because we can totally help you, but there are a few things missed."

10 min later → $20K close. 5-10 callbacks/week → 4-8 extra deals/month.

#### Tactic 2: Manager as Bait

When rep stuck on follow-up (FU1 → FU2, especially), book FU2 on MANAGER's calendar.

Why it works:
- New face = increased show rate
- "Real company, multiple people" = trust boost
- Even if manager says EXACT SAME THING as rep — prospect closes
- Information loop opened ("new authority figure coming")

#### Tactic 3: Manager Case-Study Cherry-Picks

3-5 calls/week, manager DMs:
> "[Rep] isn't feeling well today. I'll take care of you."

Adds themselves to event. Cherry-picks the BEST calls → easier to close.

Effects:
- 3-12 closes/month from manager → 12 units/mo at $4500 = $54K MRR
- AMAZING training material — review on daily sales meetings (modeling)
- Builds manager's authority with team

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- Když prodáváš a zároveň se sama řídíš, jsi v player-coach trap. Risk: tunnel-vision na další close místo zlepšování processu.
- Tato file = manager strategy guide pro až dorůstá tým.

## Pro tebe — aplikace

Když nemáš žádné sales reps:
- Jsi closer + manager v jedné osobě.
- Player-coach trap je aktivně přítomný — nikdy nemáš čas na call review (manager work).

Recommended:
1. **End-of-day journal**: 3-4 sentences per call → ID Fringe Deals → callback NEXT MORNING.
2. **Modeling sama sebe**: zaznamenat 3 best calls / měsíc → review (training future hire too).
3. **Bench building**: jakmile máš 15+ klientů, group interview week monthly.
4. **První hire = industry acumen primary** (někdo z oboru tvých klientů nebo blízko trhu).

Pro tvého klienta — pokud mu pomáháš building his team to scale jeho firmu (např. jazyková škola nabírá lektory):
- Hire on values (péče o klienta first)
- Group interview pro nábor týmu
- Manager bait pro hard-close VIP klientů
