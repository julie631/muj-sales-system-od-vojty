# Subcommunication & Tonality (Advanced 05)

## The big question

> "Why two different salespeople using the SAME EXACT WORDS get entirely different results?"

Answer = the things BEHIND the words. Subcommunication + tonality.

## Three Filters: Idea Receptiveness

The mind has too much info to process. Three filters dictate which ideas get accepted:

1. **Certainty of the communicator**
2. **Inner alignment of the communicator**
3. **Social proof (other people's thoughts around the idea)**

## Filter 1: CERTAINTY — Three Distinctions

### Distinction A: Sound of Belief

> "When you believe something so deeply, almost like that urge comes up in you — 'man, like this product has worked so well for us, we have thousands of case studies, you already told me you have this problem, you don't know how to fix it, this is going to work but you're getting squirmy when price is on the table — like what's REALLY going on, like how do you not SEE this, obviously this is going to work for you.'"

Cole's mentor: "Your certainty needs to overcome their doubt."

Two reps same skill, one more conviction → conviction wins. Always.

### Distinction B: Resolve vs Pumped-Up Certainty

- **Pumped-Up** = Tony Robbins UPW jumping/screaming. High energy. Better than nothing.
- **Resolve** = certainty AS IF IT'S ALREADY DONE.

Jordan flu game analogy: sick, exhausted, but calmest in room with game on line. Belichick-Brady era — head down, get work done, no drama, supreme confidence.

> "Resolve = you go in LOOSE. You have fun. You're in complete flow state. Not because you've prepared and need to pump up — because you know you're better than them."

Sports parallel: when playing inferior opponent, you don't need pump-up — you have resolve.

If you're an Eeyore now, getting to Pumped-Up is +1. Resolve is +2. Eventually become resolve.

### Distinction C: Depth of Expression

Weight behind words. From thousands of hours, not shallow.

> "Same exact words said by Elon Musk vs guy doing $10K/month from YouTube AdSense — different weight. The audience SENSES depth even if they don't know your background."

For salespeople (not founders): become **SAVAGE EXPERT** in 1-3 core problems your product solves. Don't need to know everything — just be #1 in the world at those specific problems prospects bring you.

> "Plastic surgeon operating on a CEO has higher status IN THAT CONTEXT than the CEO does, even though CEO is much more successful generally."

## Filter 2: INNER ALIGNMENT

Are you walking the walk? Living it?

### Why founders outsell their teams

Lived the journey. Solved the problem themselves first. Words have weight because they've embodied it.

### How salespeople achieve same alignment

1. Be Savage Expert in 1-3 core problems.
2. **MIRROR PRINCIPLE** — be the prospect you want to attract.
   - Your objections become their objections.
   - Sell $10K coaching but never bought $10K coaching yourself = struggle.
   - Sell weight loss but overweight and haven't lost it = struggle.
   - Always say "let me think about it" yourself → won't handle it well from prospects.
3. **SAY-DO RATIO = 1:1**.
   - Core value at Closers.io.
   - When you say something, you do it.
   - Going to bed at X, working out at Y, reading 20 min/day, hitting Z sales/week — DO IT.
   - Builds your energy. Your team senses it. Prospects sense it.

> "If your actions don't align with your words, it's tough to influence prospects to align their actions with their words."

### Inputs = Outputs (worldview improvement)

Personal development = sales development.

> "Sales is just a transference of THINKING. You're not selling them the product — you're selling them a way of thinking that leads to the product."

Recommended books to develop worldview:
- 17.7 Mental Toughness Secrets of the World Class
- Straight Line Leadership
- Relentless (Tim Grover)

> "I'm pretty rusty in sales — haven't done many calls lately. But I'd still slaughter myself 3-4-5 years ago when I was selling full-time. Because of who I've become, my standards, my worldview."

## Filter 3: SOCIAL PROOF (Status Drops)

Don't deliver case studies as forced presentations.

### Seeded Case Studies

Drop case study DURING discovery (just the problem, leave outcome OPEN):
> "Yep totally makes sense. We had a client before — dating coach who started with the same lead-gen problem because they were doing X like you. I'll tell you more about them later — just rang a bell."

CLOSE THE LOOP during pitch:
> "Remember earlier when I was talking about [client]? They had that same problem as you. Here's the part of what we do that allowed them to benefit — and ultimately benefit of that benefit."

### Multiple types of proof

After EVERY claim you want accepted as true:
- Case studies (social proof)
- Personal stories (living proof)
- 3rd party endorsements
- Publicity proof
- Reason-why logic

> "If we're talking about our program's 4 key results, after each result I drop 1-2 proof points to drive the claim home."

## Three Tells of Vocal Authority (Dominance)

### Tell 1: Range of Expression

Prehistoric: low-status tribe members stifled expression to avoid challenging the alpha (survival). We've inherited that stifling.

Most of us go through life with very stifled body language and tonality.

UNSTIFLE. Wider range = more dominance = more receptive prospects.

Monotone version: "This is what it sounds like to be stifled. Limited range. Nothing happens."

Broad range: high pitch when emphasizing + soft to bring in + slow to drive home.

### Tell 2: Cadence Variance

Slow + speed up + silence. Mix all three.

> "Variance holds attention. Sometimes just shutting the hell up is the most powerful move."

### Tell 3: Inflection

- **Upward inflection** (voice up at end like question) = uncertainty, seeking approval. KILL IT.
- **Neutral** = facts.
- **Downward** = authority, certainty.

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- 1:1 univerzální principy.
- "Sound of belief" v CZ projevu jiná tonalita (méně teatrální), ale stejná energy ("co tady nechápeš?" pocit pod tím).
- Resolve > Pumped-Up = perfekt fit pro CZ trh, Češi se cringe-out od US pumped-up.
- Inputs/outputs (osobní rozvoj) = univerzální.

## Pro tebe — aplikace

Tento file = **gold standard tonality reference** při call prep:

1. **Sound of Belief check**: před každým callem — věříš na 100 %, že tvoje služba tomuhle klientovi pomůže? Nebo máš pochybnosti? Pokud doubt → nejdřív vyřešit interně.
2. **Resolve mode**: opři se o klienty, kterým jsi už pomohla, a o své case studies ({tvoje case study s čísly}, sekce Důkazy v `knowledge/moje-nabidka.md`). Žádný pumped-up. Resolve. Quiet confidence.
3. **Savage Expert**: buď expertka na 2–3 konkrétní problémy svého typického klienta (např. úklidová firma pro kanceláře: nespolehliví lidi, výpadky o dovolených, stížnosti nájemníků). Nic víc nemusíš umět. Zakázky mimo svou niche odmítej, drž se jí.
4. **Mirror Principle**: investuješ sama do toho, co prodáváš klientům (používáš to ve vlastní firmě)? Pokud ne → na to navíc.
5. **Say-Do 1:1**: pravidelný morning routine, weekly projection, drilling skill na sales-coach AI — DODRŽOVAT.
6. **Seeded case studies**: case study se DROP'ne v discovery (problem only), CLOSE the loop v pitch.
7. **Vocal authority self-coaching**: nahrávat sebe na callu → poslouchat — (a) jsou tam pauzy? (b) downward inflection? (c) bez "tak jako jo?" uptalk?
