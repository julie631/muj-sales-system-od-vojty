# ALWAYS Do This at the Beginning of Every Sales Call

## The principle

7 beliefs prospects need to buy: Pain → Doubt → Cost → Desire → Money → Support → Trust.

The latter 6 are ALL predicated on pain. Without pain established, none of the others can be installed properly.

**So always: establish pain first.**

## Two types of pain

1. **Problem** = below-average to average
   - "Doctor said if I don't lose weight I won't see my kids past 10"
   - "I can barely pay my bills"
2. **Unfulfilled desire** = average to excellence
   - "I'm 10% body fat but want to be 7% for the figure competition"
   - "Making $15k/mo, want to make $100k/mo"

Both work as motivators — but you sell them differently (twist knife vs paint future).

## The opening question (after rapport + frame)

> "Cool, so what would you say right now is your biggest CHALLENGE in the business? Or like — what's not working at the level you truly feel like it could be that it should?"

### Why this exact phrasing

**"Challenge" not "problem"**: "Problem" makes prospects defensive ("nothing's wrong"). "Challenge" softens it dramatically. Taps into human need to help — they LEAN IN.

**Tonality**: soft, curious, no aggressive interrogation tone.

**Backup softener**: "...what's not working at the level you truly feel like it could be that it should."

### If they dodge

> "Got it. So what do you think the CONSTRAINT is keeping you from doubling your business?"
> "What do you think the constraint is keeping you from scaling to an extra 20-30% growth this year?"

### For new opportunities (no current business)

> "What has you considering potentially starting [X] in the first place?"

Looks for the INITIAL motivation point.

### Alternative angle: outcome + obstacle

> "Best place to start is ultimately what's the goal? [elaborate] Cool — so what do you think is the biggest thing right now keeping you from getting it?"

## 3-step deepening (after they share initial pain)

### Step 1: Isolate

You have the pain on the table. Don't pivot yet.

### Step 2: Understand

Basic probing:
- "Tell me more about that."
- "When you said [their word], what do you mean specifically?"
- "How do you mean exactly?"
- "Can you elaborate?"

> "Just tap into your natural human-being skills here."

### Step 3: Chunk Down (the ninja step)

Take abstract → concrete via NUMBERS or EXAMPLES.

#### Numbers approach

For business:
- "How many leads did you generate last week?"
- "Out of those, how many were qualified?"
- "Last month exactly, what was your revenue?"

For dating: "How many dates did you go on last month?"
For weight loss: "How much do you weigh right now?"

> "If you ask open-ended questions, they'll tell you stories. If you ask for numbers, they'll tell you the truth."

#### Specific example approach (when numbers don't apply)

> "Got it. So you said you've plateaued for 4-5 years at 220lbs. Can you give me a specific example — walk me through exactly what you ate yesterday."

> "Can you walk me back to the last time you were really good on a diet and you fell off? Take me back to that moment — walk me through what happened."

### Why the example matters

When they relive that experience and re-narrate it:
> "What's going to happen is they're going to re-elicit all of the emotions associated with that experience. For somebody inconsistent on diet — emotions of pain, guilt, shame. And those emotions ARE the emotions of change."

This is how you make pain concrete, emotional, motivating without manipulation. They do it to themselves.

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- "Challenge" → "výzva" v ČR funguje, ale ještě silnější je "co tě teď v businessu nejvíc trápí" / "co ti dělá vrásky."
- "Tell me more" → "Pověz mi víc o tom" / "Co tím přesně myslíš?"
- Last-month-exactly numbers v ČR ještě cennější — Češi mají tendenci nadnášet (až 2-3×).
- Walk-me-through example perfekt pro CZ — kulturně tu lidi rádi vyprávějí konkrétní historky.

## Pro tebe — aplikace

Discovery sequence (ukázka: majitel menší stavební firmy):

1. **Opening**:
   > "Co bys řekl, že je teď tvoje největší výzva ve firmě? Nebo co prostě nefunguje na úrovni, kde cítíš, že by to být mohlo?"

2. **Isolate** (poslouchat → probably "zakázky nejdou rovnoměrně" nebo "nevím, na čem reálně vydělávám")

3. **Understand**:
   > "Pověz mi víc — když říkáš 'nevím, na čem vydělávám', co tím přesně myslíš? Jak to vidíš v číslech?"

4. **Chunk down NUMBERS**:
   - "Minulý měsíc, kolik zakázek jste dokončili přesně?"
   - "Z těch zakázek, co jste dělali v lednu, kolik skončilo v plusu?"
   - "Kolik zakázek máte rozjetých celkem?"
   - "U kolika z nich víš, jakou mají marži?"

5. **Chunk down EXAMPLE**:
   > "Vzpomeň si na zakázku, na které jste naposled prodělali — vypadala dobře, pak se to otočilo. Co se podle tebe stalo? Walk me through."

6. **Pak Moment of Decision**:
   > "Kdy ti došlo, že tohle tak nemůže pokračovat?"

→ Vstup do diagnostiky beliefs 2-7 v dalších krocích call.
