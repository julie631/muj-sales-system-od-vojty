# "Let me talk to one of your clients" — and what you say

## Best handler: PREVENT in pitch

Load case studies HEAVILY during pitch — but as PROOF for claims, not as glory:

> "For example, we had a client named Daryl in the very same situation — wanted high-end luxury real estate listings. When we implemented this in his company, he was finally able to get those listings and convert them. You see how I use Daryl's case study as proof for my CLAIM that we help you get high-end listings — that's the key. Not random testimonials, but proof for the specific thing I'm asserting."

> "Objection prevention is always better than objection handling."

## When it still comes up

### Step 1: Pace + isolate

> "No problem. [pause] Talking with one of our clients aside — is there anything else keeping you from being less than 100% certain that this is really what you want to do and you feel like now is the right time?"

Prospect: "Oh no, I just want to speak with them, get their experience, then I'll feel really good."

### Step 2: Double tie-down

> "Gotcha. So let's just suppose you'd already spoken with one of our clients and they pretty much echoed everything I just said on this call to be true. If that were the case — what you're saying is we'd 100% be moving forward, you'd be giving me your credit card and we'd be enrolling you into the program?"

**Anything but YES = smokescreen.** Call it out:
> "Well look, earlier you said there's nothing else keeping you from being less than 100% certain. Now you're saying that even if the person I connect you with echoes everything I just said, you're still not sure you want to move forward. So what's really going on?"

## Preferred close: 7-day refund-guaranteed trial

[05:05] If the objection IS real (not smokescreen) and you can offer it:

> "Look, I definitely don't think the best thing for you is to move forward if you have any doubts. But at the same time, you told me earlier this is the year where you have to do something about this business or go back to corporate. So — what I'm willing to do for you is let you in today at full price, do your onboarding call, I'll do an extra call in a couple days to make sure you're oriented, get your Implementation Pathway. Inside the program you can leverage the community and talk to AS MANY CLIENTS AS YOU WANT. At end of 7 days, if you don't feel totally cool, we'll refund you in full. That way you can make a real decision from the INSIDE, not the outside — which is truly the only informed decision you can make. AND you draw the line in the sand and commit today that the next 5 years won't be like the last 5. **If I'm willing to do that for you, are you willing to move forward right now?**"

Bonus: add to contract for legal weight.

## Backup close: text-only reference + 48h follow-up

If you can't do 7-day trial:

> "Gotcha. So if I connect you with one of our clients right after this call, are you 100% okay with making sure the exchange only happens over chat or text? Reason I ask is I want to really respect our client's time, and I feel like you can get all the info you need just by messaging back and forth. [Yeah totally]. Okay great — so after I connect you with this client right after this call and they give you the info, **we talk in 48 hours** — and they pretty much tell you the same thing I'm telling you. Once we speak again in 48 hours — what do you think the next step is from there?"

Critical: covertly frame the 48h follow-up call as already agreed.

Anything but "moving forward" = smokescreen — call it.

### Pre-empt follow-up showup risk

After scheduling:
> "Look, regardless — even if you decide this isn't for you, or even if you don't feel you've had enough conversation with [client], still show up to the call. I can address any other questions, and even if we don't move forward there, I can create a game plan for you to work towards this in the future."

In that follow-up, come prepared with refund guarantee.

## Why this works: objection-handling is jiu-jitsu

> "You really want to focus on taking the energy they're giving you and not necessarily disagreeing — but agreeing and going with it, and using that energy to move things forward."

Don't fight "I want to talk to your clients." AGREE → "Sure, let me make that as easy as possible (text-only) AND let's pre-frame the follow-up AND let me put my money where my mouth is (refund guarantee inside)."

## Pro tebe — aplikace

Při "chci mluvit s vašima klientama":

1. **Prevention v pitchi** — dvě case studies jako proof ({tvoje case study s čísly}).
2. **Pokud přijde objection**:
   > "Klid, žádný stres. Tak hele — mluvení s našimi klienty stranou — je tam ještě něco, co tě brzdí říct 'jdu do toho'?"
3. **Double tie-down**:
   > "Aha. Takže představ si — už jsi s {klient z tvých case studies} mluvil, řekli ti přesně, co jsem ti tady řekla. V tom případě bys dnes podepsal a poslal mi platbu?"
4. **Anything but yes** → "Vidíš, že to není jen o klientech. Co tě fakt brzdí?"
5. **Real reference need**: "Hele, mám pro tebe lepší věc. {tvoje garance} — pojedeme spolu od dnes, a když výsledky nepřijdou, garance platí. Z vnitřku si s našimi klienty můžeš popovídat kdykoli. **Když ti to garantuju, jdeš do toho dnes?**"
