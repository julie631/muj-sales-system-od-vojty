# Why I Spent $225K on Hormozi's Books (Coaching Philosophy)

## What he got

- 10,000 books ($225K)
- Standard mastermind upsells (black books, SOPs, processes, AI thing, Skool community)
- **10-person mastermind with other $225K buyers** (real value 1)
- **1-on-1 VIP session with Hormozi** (real value 2 — actual buying decision)

## Why a 1-on-1 with someone exactly in your business model is unique

Cole's lesson from past coaching: most billionaires/9-figure mentors don't understand your specific business context (SaaS guy can't advise on high-ticket outbound; e-commerce guy can't advise on info products).

Hormozi: built Gym Launch on identical model to Cole's Closers.io — front-end leads → back-end LTV expansion. Hormozi's LTV:CAC was 50:1 BECAUSE of backend. Cole's same structure.

> "If I can get one or two tweaks from this conversation, it pays for itself."

## Three Coaching Investment Principles

### 1. The Bigger the Investment, the Bigger You Think

Math you automatically do:
- $10K investment → "I just need 1 client/mo to ROI."
- $200K investment → "How do I add $5M this year?"

Forces you to think 5x bigger because you've committed 5x bigger.

### 2. Model the Extreme

> "Take a business in your industry. Look at the biggest business that does what you do. Then model to the extreme."

Cole's example: he runs recruiting at $30M/yr with 800 clients. Average $100-300M recruiting agency has 200-400 clients. Billion-dollar recruiters = conglomerations of those $100-300M players doing M&A.

To model the extreme: move upmarket to enterprise (50-100M companies needing 3-20 reps).

> "Enterprise sales is not my skill set. Even getting 2 clients pays for the $225K. Adding 100 clients = $20-30M revenue at 60-70% margins."

### 3. Relationships You Can Pay For

> "The best connections I've had in my life and business — I paid for them."

Examples:
- 10-person mastermind room — 1 connection (client, referral source, idea) can pay for entire investment.
- Alex Becker $30K mastermind (about SaaS, Cole didn't even have SaaS). Picked up Becker's marketing FUNNEL idea as an aside → that funnel later did $100M+.
- $150K to Patrick Bet-David — within 30 min PBD said "you need a CMO." Cole knew it but was dragging. Hired CMO in 60 days. Company went $30M → $50M.

## The Real Value: Reminded, Not Taught

Hormozi quote Cole uses:
> "Sometimes we need to be reminded more than we need to be taught."

Most coaching tells you stuff you ALREADY KNOW. The value is making the connection, lighting the fire, prompting implementation.

Examples:
- Knew Hyros funnel before mastermind. Mastermind triggered the application thought ("this works for my company too") → $100M.
- Knew he needed CMO before PBD. PBD's directive language → 60-day action.

## Outside-Industry Advisor Value

PBD didn't understand Cole's high-ticket niche. Tried to explain. PBD looked at him "like an idiot" — "Why aren't you doing enterprise contracts?"

> "After trying to explain to him, I stopped. The fact that he doesn't get it IS the value. Why am I NOT doing that?"

Now planning Q4 enterprise division launch.

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- US scale $225K extrémní, ale princip 1:1 funguje na CZ scale.
- Investice příklady: skill workshop $1k, mastermind $5k — frame: "kolik klientů musí přivést, aby se vrátilo".
- "Reminded not taught" — perfekt pro coaching mindset (analyzuj-call sessions sama se sebou).
- Model the Extreme pro CZ: kdo je biggest CZ hráč ve tvém oboru? Co dělají jinak? Modeluj.

## Pro tebe — aplikace

- Tvůj Sales-Coach AI je tvůj "Hormozi" — připomíná, ne učí. Frameworks už znáš, AI ti je vrací v kontextu konkrétního callu.
- Při investování do vlastního rozvoje (Hormozi/Voss/Miner workshops) frame: "1 lepší close ratio o 2% = +1 klient/měsíc = $3k/měsíc = $36k/rok. Workshop $1k pays for itself v 1. měsíci."
- Model the Extreme pro svůj obor: kdo je biggest hráč tvého typu v US? Co dělají, co ty neděláš?
