# 7 Show-Rate Levers (How Sales Team Hit $100M)

## The Real KPI: Offers Per Closer Per Day

Most people think the goal is "increase show rate." Wrong frame.

Real KPI: **OFFERS PER CLOSER PER DAY**. If a closer has 6 open slots, what % result in actual offers made?

Three core departments:
- **Marketing** — KPI = MQLs (Marketing Qualified Leads)
- **Sales** — KPI = units sold / cash collected
- **Sales Ops** — KPI = offers/closer/day. THE missing department for most companies.

Target for transactional sales (cycle <3 weeks): **3 offers/closer/day**.

Show rate is ONE component. Quality + slot optimization (single vs double-booking) are the others.

## Lever 1: Booking Window = Rolling 2 Days

Single biggest lever. Out-of-4-days bookings have significantly lower show rates.

> "Just changing to rolling 2-day booking window took us from 30-40% show rate to 50-60%."

Tool: OnSub (formerly OnceHub) for the rolling-window functionality. Calendly doesn't do this well.

If you have only 1 closer (limited availability), start with 3-day window, edge to 4 if needed. As you scale closers, tighten to 2 days.

## Lever 2: Availability = 6-8 Slots/Closer/Day

More slots = higher booking conversion (more chance of finding convenient time) = higher show rate (booked time is convenient = harder to ditch).

Remote team across time zones = competitive advantage.

Don't trade booking-window for availability. Find sweet spot.

## Lever 3: Application Grading 1-4

Manually (or AI-assisted) grade applications based on:
1. Can we help them (offer/industry fit)?
2. Financially qualified?
3. Decision-maker?
4. Urgency level (from open-text answer)?
5. Detail + coherence of responses (well-filled vs one-word = signals quality)

Grades:
- **1** = Auto-cancel (spam, can't help)
- **2** = Setter triage first (uncertain, money question, not-decision-maker)
- **3** = Closer (low quality)
- **4** = Closer (high quality, more urgency, more revenue)

Monthly: delineate show rate by application grade. You'll find 2-grades show at 50%, 3-grades at 60%, 4-grades at 75%. This data drives the double-booking decision (Lever 6).

## Lever 4: Nurture Specialist (LNS) — THE BIG ONE

Dedicated VA manually texting confirmations day-of for all inbound appointments.

CRITICAL: must send from **company iPhone** = BLUE iMessage. NOT 5-digit number automation. Prospects auto-ignore 5-digit automation.

VA needs: basic English + conversational skills (can text back-and-forth like customer service rep).

Script (multi-send, conversational):
> [Send 1] Hey [name], this is Janelle from Cole Gordon's team.
> [Send 2] Will you do me a favor and reply YES if you're 100% confirmed for your appointment time?
> [Send 3] Looking forward to connecting with you!

If they confirm:
> Awesome — looking forward to connecting. Will you also watch this video before the call? It'll help us hit the ground running. Let me know if you need any assistance. [PRE-CALL VIDEO LINK]

If they don't reply:
> Just bumping this above — text me back if you're confirmed. PS: will you watch this video before the call? [LINK]

Test both: send immediately upon booking vs day-before. Cole: depends on company, test both.

## Lever 5: Automated Email Cadence (full sequence)

- Immediate after booking: "Read before your call — please accept calendar invite + watch this video"
- 2 hours after: PERSONAL intro from main (Cole's) email to the closer ("Wanted to connect you with [Closer]")
- 24 hours before: OnSub auto reminder + LNS text bumps
- 1 hour before: text + email reminder
- 15 min before: text reminder
- 10 min before: BOTH text + email

Last 2 (10-15 min) can be reduced if it feels too much.

## Lever 6: Double-Booking System

After implementing all above, if show rate still <66%:
- Rule 1: **Single-book** any 4-grade (high-quality) application (above 75% show rate)
- Rule 2: **Single-book** anyone CONFIRMED via LNS text (above 66%)
- Rule 3: **Double-book** unconfirmed lower-grade applications

When 2 people show up at same slot:
- Slack/Discord channel for "double shows" — any closer who has a no-show can pick up the second.
- Setters also know top of hour = double-show possibility → triage.
- If only Setter gets them: act like normal 2-call close.

Setters always single-book (their show rate is in their control, should be >70%).

> "It's a far better tradeoff than building team on 40-60% show rate. Smaller team doing more revenue > big team with low utilization."

## Lever 7: Auto-Rebook No-Shows

- Closer marks "no-show" in CRM → automated text + email connection → setter picks up → setter sets follow-up.
- Setter shows rate on no-show rebook = ~70%+.
- DON'T over-chase no-shows. New leads = higher leverage than chasing.

## Setter Show Rate <70% = Setter Issue

If setters showing under 70%, it's almost ALWAYS setter skill, not market.

> "A real person should outperform automation. They're talking to a real person, getting tie-downs, selling them on call value. There's no reason a setter should show worse than ads."

Cause: setter calls feel like telemarketing, no value, no insight, no tie-downs.

Fix: QC review setter calls at same frequency as closer calls. Most managers neglect setters.

Best fix: **closer does setter job for 1-2 days**. Sets the bar, builds scripts, finds problems.

## If Everything Above Fails → Ad Quality

If show rate still <30-40% after all 7 levers, problem is upstream: bad ads, bad offer, bad VSL.

> "Facebook is REALLY good at giving you conversions to keep you spending — but it gives you the bottom-of-the-barrel traffic to optimize. Especially in B2C."

Fix:
1. Write better ads/sales videos
2. Move slightly upmarket — same problem, more sophisticated audience
   - "Make money online" → "Salespeople wanting to break $100k"
   - "Anybody flip sports cards" → "White-collar professionals wanting passive income"
   - "Anyone who wants to write" → "Writers who want to double their income"

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- Scale CZ ≠ US, ale logika 1:1.
- iMessage blue text — v ČR funguje SMS s reálnou identitou (ne 5-cifra automation).
- V malé firmě často děláš sama MARKETING + SALES + SALES OPS + DELIVERY. Žádný dedicated sales ops department.
- CZ market: 6-8 slots/closer/day = pravděpodobně příliš mnoho; 3-4 slots/day realistic.

## Pro tebe — aplikace

Příklad funnelu: cold DMs s hlasovkou → discovery call.

Show-rate optimalizace:
1. **Rolling 2-day booking** — pokud používáš Calendly, zvaž přechod na OnSub/SavvyCal.
2. **Confirmation text z mobilu** (blue iMessage equivalent v ČR = SMS z tvého mobilu, ne přes ESP).
   - "Ahoj [jméno], tady {tvoje jméno}. Potvrdíš mi YES, že platí náš zítřejší call?"
3. **Cadence**:
   - Immediate po booking: confirmation email s pre-call video (story z {tvoje case study s čísly})
   - 24h před: SMS z tvého mobilu
   - 1h před: SMS reminder
4. **Application grading**: pre-call form (velikost firmy, co dnes používá, urgency) → ne všechny call slots stejně cenné.
5. **No-show rebook**: text "Hej, zmeškali jsme se. Kdy se ti hodí?"

Setter show rate principle: pokud budeš najímat setera, kontroluj každý week. Setter show <70% = setter skill.
