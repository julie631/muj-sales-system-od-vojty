# Close 100% of Sales Calls with THIS… (Committing Phase)

## Context: 6 parts of a sales call

1. Introduction (rapport + frame)
2. Information Gathering (discovery)
3. Transition (bridge to pitch)
4. Pitch (pillars)
5. **Committing Phase** ← this video
6. Objection handling

## Committing Phase = 3 substeps

### Step 1: Temp Check

After last pillar:
> "Does that make sense? Cool — what questions do you have about the four things we covered SPECIFICALLY?"

Note word "specifically" — directs attention to YOUR pillars, not "what's the price" or "can I talk to clients."

#### Handling their questions (3 rules)

[05:34] **Rule 1: Remove hidden objection BEFORE answering**

Prospect: "Is this group coaching?"
Bad: "Yes." → if they got burned by group coaching before, you just lost them.
Good: "Yeah, there's parts of our program that are collaborative. I'll give you details in a sec — just before I do, is there a specific reason why you're asking?"

[06:36] Prospect reveals: "I was in a program with 150-person group calls, never got support, total waste of time."

Now pivot: "Got it. Our support is from our community — 24h response time anytime. Group calls 5×/week, max ever 15 people. If you submit your question beforehand, you can be on and off in 5 minutes. Opposed to what you went through — see how this would work better?"

**Rule 2: Answer in fewest words possible** (1 word, 1 sentence — 2 sentences max).

[08:39] **Rule 3: After answering, direct conversation forward**
> "Does that make sense? Cool — what else? What other questions do you have?"

This phrase keeps the call YOURS. Don't sit silent waiting.

#### After all questions answered

> "Cool. How do you feel about those four things we covered specifically? Like, do you feel like that process specifically is what you need to be able to get to [outcome]?"

**Listen for tonality**:
- Assertive: "Oh dude absolutely, this is exactly what we need." → green light.
- Hedging: "Yeah I think so" → orange light, dig deeper.

[10:39] If hedging, scale 1-10 question:
> "I think so — okay, well look. On a scale of 1 to 10, one being 'I hate this guy, want off the phone' and 10 being 'this is exactly what I need' — where do you fall, precisely?"

If 7-8: "What's keeping you from being a 9 or 10?"
If 9: "Anything at all keeping you from being a 10?"

Often they say "just the price." **Redirect**: "Oh, I'm not asking 10/10 on price. I'm asking about the PROCESS — in terms of the four things we covered, are you in alignment that this is what you need to get to [outcome]?"

Critical: get thesis buy-in BEFORE price drop. Post-price-drop uncertainty handling is brutal.

### Step 2: Onboarding Preview (paint the next cliff)

[12:11] Once they're bought in:
> "Cool — you feel really good, no questions, feel it's 100% what you need. What's next — where do you want to go from here?"

Prospect: "How do I move forward? What's the price?"

> "Yeah man — so here's the next steps. You'll process the investment with me, then once we take care of that, what we do is a process called Setting Baseline. We get you on an onboarding call. Before that, you do some homework to prep. Once on the call, we map out your Implementation Pathway — when we'll meet, how often, what we'll do for you, what you'll do right away, what you'll do down the road. You leave that call with full clarity on next steps. All you do is show up and take the next step forward. Does that make sense?"

### Step 3: Investment drop

[13:42]
> "Cool. And the investment is just $9,800."

Or "$24,000." Or "$68,000." Same delivery.

#### Why onboarding BEFORE price

> "When they give you 10 grand, they want to know exactly what happens next. They're making a leap to the next cliff — if you don't paint the picture of the next cliff, they're leaping into the abyss."

Onboarding preview = visualization of next cliff = certainty multiplier = lower resistance at price reveal.

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- Funguje 1:1.
- "What questions do you have about the four things specifically?" → "Co tě napadá k těm čtyřem věcem, co jsme prošli?"
- Scale 1-10 v ČR funguje skvěle, je to klasická coaching technika.
- "What's keeping you from being a 9 or 10?" → "Co by ti chybělo, abys to měl jako 9 nebo 10?"
- Onboarding preview je v ČR ještě cennější — Češi jsou riziko-averzní, vysoká specificita next-steps zvyšuje důvěru.

## Pro tebe — aplikace

Price drop sequence:
1. Pitch završen (4 pillars).
2. "Co tě napadá k těm čtyřem věcem?" → odpovědět krátce, odstranit skryté námitky.
3. "Jak ti to celé zní? Dává ti to smysl jako cesta k tomu, co jsme si na začátku popsali?"
4. (po YES) "Tak skvělý. Co bude dál: dnes podepíšeme smlouvu, poslu ti onboarding email s 3 věcmi, co budeš potřebovat (přístupy, podklady, termín úvodního callu). Za 7 dní máme první výstup. Do 30 dní jede všechno naplno. Dává to smysl?"
5. "Cena je {tvoje cena — jednorázově + měsíčně}."

Risk reversal po price drop ({tvoje garance}) přijde přirozeně po objection, ne preventivně.
