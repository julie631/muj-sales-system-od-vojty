# How to Overcome Every Objection With 1 Simple Question

## The principle: two sales in every call

Every call has two sales:
1. **The thesis/method** — that THIS approach is how you get from A to B
2. **The product** — that OUR company is how you do this approach

> "Russell Brunson doesn't sell ClickFunnels — he sells that funnels are the most effective fastest best way to get customers online. Once he knocks that domino, you obviously buy ClickFunnels."

If thesis isn't sold, no objection handling fixes the product sale.

## The 1 question

After price drop / objection / "let me think":

> "Hey, no problem. Just curious — how do you feel about the process? Investment and everything aside, do you feel like this is ultimately what you need to get to [outcome]?"

### Why each component matters

1. **"No problem"** = diffuses pressure (vs. "what do you need to think about?" which raises defenses)
2. **"Just curious"** = lowers stakes of the question
3. **"Investment and everything aside"** = isolates money (because you just dropped price)
4. **"Do you feel like this is ultimately what you need"** = ties to the thesis (method)

## STRONG language requirement

CRITICAL: this question must be delivered with such certain language it almost ENCOURAGES a no.

Weak (doesn't work):
> "So how do you feel about the process? You feel like this is what you need?"

Strong (works):
> "Investment and everything aside — do you 100% believe this is exactly what you need to get your business to [outcome]?"

Direct eye contact + clear, distinct, slow delivery.

> "Most people throw it out softly so the prospect agrees. Sure, if they agree assertively, great. But if you're soft, they're going to be like 'yeah, it's what I need, just going to think about it.' You need to really kind of throw it in their face."

Goal: squeeze out hidden uncertainty.

## Two outcomes after the 1 question

### Outcome A: Yes → financial logistic

Prospect: "Oh yeah, it's exactly what I need. Just got to figure out how to make it work."

→ Double tie-down: "So what you're saying is finances aside, you're 100% in." → handle financial branch (open wallet, payment split as exchange).

### Outcome B: Hedge / "I think so" → uncertainty

Prospect: "I think so but…"

Cole interjects, calls it out:
> "I think so. Okay — well hey, what's really coming up for you? It seems like you don't think this is 100% what you need to get your business where you want to go. So look, you're not going to offend me, but what's specifically keeping you from thinking that this is going to work?"

### Why "assume they don't think it'll work"

Two benefits:
1. They give SPECIFIC concern → tangible → you can address it.
2. They correct the record → "Oh no, I do think it'll work, it's just [actual concern]" → now you have real objection.

> "What we want to do in objections: intangible to tangible."

## Sub-framework: floundering prospect (no specific concern)

Prospect: "I don't know, it's just a big decision."

### Step 1: Feed them the objection

> "Gotcha. So look, I totally get that. You think it's just like a little bit of nerves then about this decision?"

Most people accept this label. Don't say "fear" — they'll fight you ("I'm a big tough guy"). Say "nerves."

### Step 2: The Nerves Close

> "Yeah, that's it. Okay great — well can I give you a little bit of feedback on that? [Sure]. Okay look, well I'm glad you're nervous. [PAUSE — pattern interrupt]. The nerves are there for a reason. They're GOOD. That means there's some weight to this. There's some meaning to it. I mean, nothing ever great in this world was built by somebody who wasn't a little bit nervous at first. So being nervous is okay, but deep down in the pit of your stomach you should know — despite the little bit of fear, despite the little bit of nerves — that this is the right thing. And when I asked you how you felt about it earlier, you said this is what you need, right? Yeah. Okay — well then let's get you going. You ready to go?"

### Step 3: If still floundering — Identity Close

> "I totally appreciate you being open and honest. And look — having a little bit of nerves in this moment, totally normal. But the real issue isn't the nerves and isn't the fact that it's a big decision. The real question is — who do you want to be right now? And what actions and decisions are you going to make moving forward? Do you want to be the type of person who lets their own fear guide them and therefore never creates the reality you actually want? Or do you want to be the type of person that can feel the fear and do it anyway? The type that senses the discomfort but knows that in the place of discomfort is the only place you can grow. So — which of those two people do you want to be?"

### Why Identity Close works

Cialdini's principle of consistency bias: we want actions consistent with who we believe we are. The reframe stem:
> "The real question isn't [stated objection]. The real question is — who do you want to be?"

Caveat: Identity Close is for FLOUNDERING. NOT for tangible objections (spouse, money, talk to client). For tangible, handle the tangible directly.

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- 1 question stem: "Peníze stranou — myslíš, že je tohle to, co potřebuješ, aby ses dostal k [výsledek]?"
- "Strong language" → "Myslíš, že je tohle stoprocentně to, co potřebuješ?" — důraz na "stoprocentně"
- Nerves close: "Jsem rád, že máš nervy. Když máš nervy, znamená to, že tě to fakt zajímá. Žádný velký krok v životě se nestal bez nervů. A když jsi mi před chvilkou řekl, že je to to, co potřebuješ — tak jdeme do toho." Funguje skvěle, není to americky cringe.
- Identity close: "Hele, pravá otázka není, jestli je to drahé. Pravá otázka je — chceš zůstat v místě, kde jsi, nebo udělat ten krok?"

## Pro tebe — aplikace

Script po price drop + "musím si to rozmyslet":

> "Klid, žádný stres. Jen jedna otázka — peníze stranou, myslíš, že je tohle stoprocentně to, co potřebuješ, abys {výsledek, který chce — jeho slovy} a zastavil {to, co mu dnes utíká — jeho slovy}?"

- Pokud YES + hedging → "Jasně si stůj. Ty kreditky stranou — jsi na 100% v?" → financial logistic.
- Pokud "tak nevím" → "Hmm, vypadáš, že je tam ještě něco. Tak rovně — co tě brzdí, abys řekl 'jo, do toho'?"
- Pokud floundering → Nerves close + případně Identity close ("chceš zůstat tam, kde jsi, nebo si dát ten next step?")
