# Follow My EXACT Pitch Framework (Pitch Codex)

## The 3 parts

### 1. High-Level Promise
What this IS and what this IS NOT, at the highest level of benefit. State end-to-end at top.

### 2. Bridge (3-4 Pillars)
- NOT the deliverables.
- The MILESTONES from current → desired situation.
- Pillars 1-2: **paradigm shifts** (why everything they tried failed + why this is different)
- Pillars 3-4: **future pacing** (what life looks like after pillars 1-2 work)

### 3. Delivery
What they actually get. Shortest part. Most pitch time is on bridge.

> "By the very nature of explaining my solution, I also explain why you failed in the past and why this is going to be different."

## Setup: pen-and-pad command

[04:34] After transition into pitch:
> "Great. Do you have a pen and pad?" → wait for confirmation → "Write out one through four. One, two, three, four." → wait silently until you hear them ready.

Why: (a) confirms buyer state (not walking around, not distracted), (b) note-taking forces internalization, (c) **direct command pattern** — establishes who's leading.

> "Right out one through four — direct command. Not 'and you can write out one through four if you want to.'"

## The High-Level Promise script

> "Cool, well let me preface this by saying everything we do is all customized. Okay? So for you specifically, it's going to be four things. Four things to help you go from [current situation] to [end state with all the dream outcomes]."

Example (real estate coach):
> "Four things to help you go from where you're just starting off in real estate to the point where you have new leads (specifically higher-end listings) coming in every week, you have a process to close them, and the framework to build a team so you can remove yourself from your business and have an asset that gives you cash flow independent of your time."

## Per-pillar formula

Every pillar follows this structure:

```
Look, so when it comes to [desired outcome], here's the problem.
Most people out there [incorrect behavior]
→ which means [consequence 1]
→ which means [consequence 2]
→ ultimately they [end consequence].

So instead what we do is [specific method/proprietary name]
→ which allows you to [benefit 1]
→ which means [benefit 2]
→ ultimately [benefit of benefit].

Does that make sense?
Cool — what are your thoughts on that?
```

Note: ALWAYS double tie-down at end of each pillar (first two especially).

### Example pillar (offer-build pillar)

> "When it comes to dialing in your paid traffic, here's the issue. Most coaches are completely focused on their ads, funnels, CPCs, CPLs. When in reality, it's really the OFFER — especially the economics. So they spend a ton of money on ads, do bunch of targeting tests, bunch of bidding, ultimately just wasting money because they're building their house on a foundation of sand. Does that make sense?
>
> So what we do instead is take you through an Offer Intensive process to build out an offer positioned UNIQUE, DIFFERENT, and SUPERIOR from competitors. When you do that, not only do your ads actually work (because you create a blue ocean), but because you positioned away from commodity, you can command premium pricing — which finally makes your economics work and your ROAS work. So instead of an ad strategy where we lower CPAs, we're creating a strategy where we raise LTVs.
>
> Does that make sense? Cool — what are your thoughts?"

## Proprietary naming for pillars

[18:08] Pillar names must:
- Feel proprietary (not generic "build your funnel")
- NOT sound like BS ("XYZ Blackbox System" = stupid)
- Sound like real things prospect hasn't heard of (like "ketosis" — it exists, but most people haven't encountered it)

Good: "Productized Offer", "Authority Campaign", "Offer Intensive"
Bad: "Phase 1: Discovery"

## Avoid the anvil drop

[11:06] BAD pitch:
> "Get ready, pen and pad. I'm going to go through all this stuff. Hold all questions to the end."

Then they Speedy-Gonzales through, prospect gives up taking notes, forgets questions → "let me think about it" or "send me something."

GOOD pitch: dialogue.
- "Does that make sense?" → small yes
- "What are your thoughts on that?" → actually elicits feedback
- "Do you see how earlier when you said X, this would help you Y?" → triple tie-down

## Multiple forms of proof

[14:07] After each pillar (especially 1-2), drop proof:
> "For example, when we implemented this in my company in December 2021, [result]."

Or:
> "For example, we had a client in your exact same industry experiencing [problem], and once they implemented this, they got [result] — which is basically what you're looking for, right?"

## CZ adaptation poznámka

- Framework je 1:1 přenositelný.
- Pen-and-pad command v ČR slabší — místo direct command vyzvat: "Můžeš si u toho dělat poznámky? Bude to čtyři kroky." Ne tak agresivní.
- Per-pillar formula "Most people / so what we do instead" funguje skvěle česky: "Většina firem řeší X, což vede k Y. My to děláme jinak — místo toho A, což ti přinese B."
- "Does that make sense?" → "Dává to smysl?"
- "What are your thoughts?" → "Jak to vidíš?" / "Jak ti to zní?"

## Pro tebe — aplikace

Pre-baked 4-pillar pitch (ukázka: účetní kancelář):
1. **Setup pillar** — "Většina firem řeší účetnictví jednou za rok, když hoří přiznání. My nejdřív otevřeme tvoje čísla během roku — tam, kde se ještě dá něco ovlivnit."
2. **Daňový pillar** — "Většina majitelů přeplácí na daních a netuší o tom. My to spočítáme přesně podle tvých dokladů."
3. **Future pacing** — "Jednou nastavené, doklady chodí samy a ty to neřešíš."
4. **Future pacing** — "Víš dopředu, kolik zaplatíš, a máš čas na růst firmy místo papírů."

Pillars 1-2 jsou paradigm shifts (proč to dosud nefungovalo + proč nyní bude jinak). Pillars 3-4 jen builduje certainty.
