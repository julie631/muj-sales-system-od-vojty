# Body

## 1. Command silence — speak surgically

[1:04]
> "Respect doesn't go to the one who talks the most. It goes to the one who talks the least and asks the most with the right precision."

Rules:
- No overexplaining
- No nervous laughter
- No "Does that make sense?"

Silence builds tension. Tension commands attention. Attention becomes respect.

## 2. Own the frame with the first question

[1:36] First impression isn't a statement — it's a question that sets the agenda.

> "So, just so I don't go over things you might have already talked about with my team — maybe you could give me your thoughts around kind of what you've talked about so far, and then maybe what you'd like to cover. That way we could focus on you and what you guys might be looking for."

You just flipped the frame. They qualify to you. You control the destination.

## 3. Status-preserving NO

[2:38]
> "I'm not quite sure that would work for me — but I can give you two options that would. Would that help you more?"

- No defensiveness
- No biography of why it wouldn't work
- Just options

> "Boundaries create value. Scarcity creates respect."

**Action:** Say one clean no today. Replace with two acceptable alternatives.

## 4. Gaze, pace, blink control

[3:10]
- Slow blink rate
- Still head
- Measured delivery

Before saying something important:
- Lower volume slightly
- Slow down
- Or — say one word fast, then slow

> "Here's the part you're going to want to pay attention to."

> "Listen, this is important to you."

**Action:** Record a 60-second voice note. Slow down 30–40%. On the strongest sentence, drop volume + lengthen the pause.

## 5. Strategic scarcity of access

[4:10] When someone pressures you for your time:

> "Hey, send me the details and I'll get back to you to see if I'm going to be available on that. I'm not quite sure."

Keep it vague. Don't be constantly available.

> "Scarcity raises perceived value. People disrespect what is easily available."

## 6. Don't react

[4:40] When attacked / snarky comment / public challenge:

Three steps:
1. **Breathe once.**
2. **Pause for a second.**
3. **Label calmly:** "Sounds like you have a concern."
4. **Redirect with question:** "What outcome are you hoping to get from this?"

> "You just converted an attack into information. Attackers lose power when they have to explain themselves. Now they're on the defensive, not you."

**Action:** When challenged, ask one neutralizing question to lower their guard before answering at all.

## 7. Calibrated praise (NEPQ Identity Frame)

[5:12] The most actionable move. **Three components:**

### Component A — Trait-based compliment
> "Hey — you seem like the type of person who thinks fast. I noticed that when we met today, and your boss, they're lucky to have somebody like you that is that forward-thinking and willing to help out."

### Component B — Negative identity contrast
> "Because, you know, I talked to a lot of people in here and you'd be surprised how many of them, they're just here to collect a check. They could care less about going that extra mile and helping out."

→ Nobody wants to be the "just here to collect a check" person.

### Component C — Lock the identity with a born-or-learned question
> "For you — being that willing to help out — is that something you were born with, or something you had to learn?"

Whatever they answer, they've now bought into the identity.

### The cash-in
> "Hey, by the way — would you mind helping me out with something?"

> "More than likely they're going to say, 'Yeah, sure.'"

> "People act in ways that protect the identity you've just elevated."

## Closing principle

[6:45] **Discipline over dominance.**
- Silence instead of scrambling
- Questions instead of overexplaining
- Boundaries instead of apologies
- Certainty over uncertainty
- Calm instead of fireworks

## Key takeaways / CZ kontext

- **Calibrated praise identity frame** for outreach: *"Honzo, podle toho, jak máš ten salon postavený, vidím, že seš typ majitele, co prostě chce, aby to fungovalo na nejvyšší úrovni. Většina majitelů, se kterýma mluvím, prostě jen drží tu loď nad vodou. Pověz mi — je to něco, s čím se člověk narodí, nebo ses to musel naučit?"*
- **"Send me the details"** scarcity move for CZ: *"Pošli mi to mailem a dám ti vědět, jestli to budu schopná projít — teď nevím."* Stops you from sounding desperate.
- **Status-preserving no** is huge for CZ closing: when prospect asks for discount, never apologize, just offer two alternatives — *"Tahle cena bohužel nepůjde, ale můžu ti nabídnout buď A nebo B — co z toho ti dává větší smysl?"*
- **First question controls the frame** — on demo calls always open with: *"Honzo, než začnu, dej mi vědět, co jsi se z toho našeho předchozího hovoru zatím rozhodl probrat — abych se neopakovala a soustředila se na to, co tě fakt zajímá."*
