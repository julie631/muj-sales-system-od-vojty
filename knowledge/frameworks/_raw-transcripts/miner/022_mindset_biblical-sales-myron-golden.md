# Body

## Persuasion vs. Convincing

[0:32]
> "Persuasion and convincing are not just the same — they are actually opposites of each other. Convincing is when I attempt to get you to do something I desire YOU to do for MY reasons. Persuasion is when I help YOU make a decision you already desire to make for YOUR own reasons."

## Biblical sales lens

[0:01]
> "I sell from a biblical perspective. If I want to be great at sales, I have to sell to SERVE the people — not sell so the people can serve me. Love your clients so much you would never sell them anything that would do them harm."

> "I hate the old saying 'oh they're so good they can sell ice to an Eskimo.' I'm going to sell them a coat. I'm going to sell them warmer gloves. You're selling them what they need based on the problems you help them find."

## Mindset + Skillset + Toolset = Assets

[9:24]
> "The ultimate tech stack in selling is mindset + skillset + toolset = assets. It's pretty hard to fail if you have those three down. The biggest problem is people get a toolset (autoresponder, Instagram bot) and think the tool will fix their problem. Or they learn skillset tactics — but they don't have the mindset, so they're saying the right thing with their mouth but their energy is telling the truth on them."

## You are the objection you give

[22:25]
> "You are the objection you give. If you object to paying for training, your prospects will object to paying you. You're in ideological agreement with them."

## The 4 Buyer Identity Tiers (Freeo / Cheapo / Feeo / Preo)

[22:57] **All identity shifts. Reality shifts after identity.**

### 1. Freeo people
Want everything for free.
> "I don't work with freebie people. If you want everything for free, good — go get it from somebody else. I don't have to deal with you. Everybody's happy."

**Salesperson equivalent:** the unwilling-to-invest rep. → "You are the objection you give. If you don't invest in your skills, you can't overcome 'it's too expensive' objections."

### 2. Cheapo people
Want everything cheap.
> "Cheap stuff is more expensive than quality stuff — because you have to keep buying it over and over."

**Salesperson equivalent:** Dabblers. Buy a few sales books, follow YouTube, "eventually I'll figure it out."

### 3. Feeo people
Willing to pay a fee — $1k event, $5k course.
> "They can have some transformation. Some success."

**Salesperson equivalent:** Know-it-allers — low six figures and CAPPED because they stop training.

### 4. Preo people
Willing to pay a PREMIUM.
> "People who are willing to pay a premium are deserving to receive a premium. It's literally a rite of passage."

## The Apple Tree frame

[25:38]
> "How would you like to have a multitude of people lined up outside your business — cash and credit card in hand — clamoring to buy whatever you'd love to sell? But I have to ask you a question first. What does a tree have to do to bear apples? People say it has to grow, have water, sunlight. But the most important thing — it has to BE an apple tree."

> "Everything reproduces after its own kind. **You don't attract the kind of buyer you want. You attract the kind of buyer you ARE.**"

## The Million-Dollar Subtraction frame

[33:20] **Used live to overcome "I don't want to pay that much":**

> "Do you believe you're smart?" — "Yes."
> "Do you believe one smart person can teach another smart person something they know how to do?" — "Yes."
> "If somebody's made millions and millions of dollars, and you're smart and they're smart — could they teach you how to do it?" — "Yes."

> "Okay — write down $1 million at the top of a piece of paper. Then write down how much you made last year, subtracted from the million. Let's say you made $200,000 last year — subtract from $1M → $800,000."

> "So because you don't want to pay me $50,000 to learn how to sell better — you paid LIFE $800,000 for not knowing."

[34:21]
> "But the problem is — it didn't just cost you $800,000. That's just last year. And the year before. And the year before. Would you rather pay LIFE again for not knowing — or would you rather pay me so you can learn and don't have to live in the land of not knowing anymore?"

## Price vs. Cost vs. Worth frame

[35:23]
> "Poor people know how much everything costs. Rich people know how much everything's worth. That's the difference. If you want to be rich, start asking better questions: 'How much is it WORTH?'"

## Don't sell for money — money is the byproduct

[40:01]
> "Money becomes the byproduct of excellence instead of the pursuit."

## Be Do Have framework

[33:40]
> "The platform for success is time, space, and matter.
>
> - The purpose of TIME is BEING.
> - The purpose of SPACE is DOING.
> - The purpose of MATTER is HAVING.
>
> Be → Do → Have. That's the formula."

## "Convincing vs. Persuasion" repeated

[37:29]
> "People do things for their own reasons — not yours. Most salespeople think selling is convincing people to buy something they don't want to buy. That's wrong."

> "Wrong question: 'How do I find people to sell my stuff to?'"

> "Right question: 'There are already millions of people in the world who would love to buy what I'd love to sell. How can I make myself more FINDABLE for them?'"

## "Use the money to love the people"

[36:58]
> "Don't use the money — don't use the people. Use the money and love the people. And take it to its ultimate extreme — use the money to love the people."

## Closing principle for self-improvement

[1:48]
> "If you don't take time to get better, you're going to get worse over time."

## Key takeaways / CZ kontext

- **CZ adaptation: HIGH.** Drop the biblical / Solomon framing — Czech audience is secular. Keep the principles, lose the religious shell.
- **Persuasion vs. convincing** = single highest-value reframe. CZ: *"Přesvědčování je, když se ti snažím něco vnutit. Pomáhat ti udělat rozhodnutí je úplně něco jinýho — pomáhám ti dojít k tomu, co už chceš."*
- **The 4 buyer tiers** work for small-business owner positioning: "freeo" = chce všechno zadarmo, "cheapo" = vždycky chce slevu, "feeo" = ochotný investovat malou částku, "preo" = chce nejlepší, ochotný platit. Cílit na "feeo + preo" segment. Freeo a cheapo nejsou ICP.
- **Million-Dollar Subtraction** = perfect for a premium tier: *"Kolik si vyděláš teď za rok? Vezmi 5 milionů — kolik tě život stál za to, že musíš odmítat zakázky, protože nemáš lidi? A to byl jenom letošek."*
- **"You are the objection you give"** = brutal but critical. Pokud sama nechceš investovat 50 tis. do mentora / coache, tvoji klienti nezaplatí {tvoje cena} za tvou službu.
- **Apple Tree frame** in CZ: *"Aby strom rodil jabka, musí bejt jabloň. Nepřitahuješ klienta, kterýho chceš — přitahuješ klienta, kterým seš."*
- **"How much is it worth?"** in CZ: *"Kolik to stojí?"* → reframe na *"Kolik tě to bude stát, když to neuděláš?"*
