# Body

## Verbal pacing (demonstrated live)

[1:36] Miner catches the rep's verbal pacing:

> "You actually have pretty good verbal pacing. How familiar are you with what I mean by that? Verbal pacing — pacing out when you're talking, like it's it's Juliano. Juliano Messi. You called about the life insurance. *(slow, with pauses)*"

vs. the bad version:
> "Hey, is Mary there? Hey Mary, it's Juliano Maserati. You called about the life insurance. *(monotone robot)*"

**The double-name with pause IS the technique.**

## Lean-in tonality

[2:36]
> "When you lean in like Leonardo DiCaprio did — comes across the desk, you're leaning in — it takes your vocal cords down into that concern tone that you know something they might not know. And that triggers curiosity in the brain."

→ Prospect: *"Wait — what are you talking about? Tell me more."*

## Closed pre-approval vs. reframe

[3:39] Rep's default:
> Prospect: "I'm not really sure. How much does it cost?"
> Rep: "It depends on age and health — we'd have to get you pre-approved real quick, won't take longer than 5 minutes. Just remind me what's your date of birth?"

Miner's reframe:
> "Oh yeah, we'll go through that for sure. I mean, it's really going to depend on like your age, your health, kind of the coverage that you might have compared to what you would be — and once I understand all those details, I can go through all the different options. Would that help you more?"

→ Same destination, but reframes the prospect's cost-question into a discovery invitation.

## Mirror + smile

[4:40]
> "I learned this one thing that changed the way I was selling — to sell with a mirror in front of you and smile."

> "Yeah, like even your phone, your MacBook camera. Then it's like — you're having a conversation, you need to have that energy in the mirror, then it rubs off on the phone and they're all of a sudden laughing and smiling too. You could just feel it."

## Rejection mindset — projection

[5:42]
> "Anytime somebody hates on you, always know it has nothing to do with you and everything to do with what's internally going on inside of them — their own insecurities, their own hate, their own jealousy. Nobody who's doing anything would ever have the time to hate on somebody's post. You might not like it, but you're not going to comment. So it's their own projection."

## "Just dance" — between-call reset

[3:07]
> "Like when you guys get rejected, I swear, you got to start dancing. Just dance it away. Dancing is universal. Get it out of the system. Call the phone. On to the next. Then you get paid."

> "If you're training every single day, you're going to get far less rejection because your skill level goes up."

## Personal branding (recession-proof career)

[1:05] Rep's logic:
- Life insurance: everyone needs it, recession-proof.

- He sells through DMs: "People reach out: 'Hey, I want insurance. I like your vibe. Sell it to me.'"

## Key takeaways / CZ kontext

- **Verbal pacing with the double-name pause** ("Honzo, ahoj — tady {tvoje jméno}. {Tvoje jméno a příjmení}.") works identically in Czech.
- **Mirror-and-smile** is a free habit you can adopt today for every call. CZ prospects often hear "you don't really care" — smiling literally changes vocal warmth.
- **Lean-in tonality** during Zoom calls is huge: lean into the camera at the moment you want to seed doubt or pull empathy.
- **"Just dance" reset** = a real technique. Between cold calls, physical reset breaks the rejection loop. CZ context: literally 30 seconds of movement / shake-it-out between calls.
- **Personal branding logic** maps directly onto your outreach: produce, don't consume, be omnipresent in your niche.
