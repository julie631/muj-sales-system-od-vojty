# Body

## When rebuttals fail

[0:32]
> "Rebuttals are okay. I would actually rather find out what's behind the objection so I know how to help them overcome it — rather than assuming I know. That's a danger in sales."

## The setup — "I want to think it over"

[1:03] When you've tried everything and can't move them, **shift your tone into concern tone.**

> "Imagine if the prospect feels like you're really concerned for the consequences of what happens if these problems don't get solved. They stay in the status quo. Nothing ever changes."

## The line — full structure

[1:35]
> "Um, can I — can I ask you something, John?"

*(Prospect: "Sure, yeah, go ahead.")*

> "Um, and this is between you and I — and it's, you know, it's it's off the record. I'm not going to go post it somewhere. Um, what's really holding you back from moving forward?"

## Why "can I — can I ask you something" not "let me ask you"

[2:38]
> "If I said 'Let me ask you' and then bulldozed you with a question — that's an aggressive form. I'm asking for permission. If I say 'Can I um can I ask you something John?' — no one is ever going to say 'No, you cannot ask me' because of my tone. My tone acts like I care about you."

## Why verbal pacing matters

[3:09]
> "Did you see how I paced that out? Write this down — that's called verbal pacing. What if I said it like this: 'Can I ask you something? Sure go ahead. Off the record, between you and I, what's really holding you back?' That'll work sometimes, but definitely not most of the time. Definitely not on A-type personalities. How many A-types do you lose sales from?"

## The myth — 93% non-verbal

[4:14]
> "Have you ever read a book that says 93% of communication is your non-verbal? That study has since changed. It's about 30% words. But that leaves 70% of you connecting and making that sale based on NOT the questions you ask, NOT what you say — but HOW you say it. How you ask the questions. What's your tone."

## Key takeaways / CZ kontext

- **The line in CZ:** *"Můžu se vás na něco zeptat, Honzo? *(prospect: 'Jasně, klidně.')* Mezi námi, je to úplně mimo záznam — co vás od toho fakt drží?"*
- **"Mezi námi"** is the Czech "off the record" equivalent and lands even harder in Czech business culture (Czechs are MORE private than Americans).
- **A-type / executive personalities in CZ** = owners who have built their business by being tough (think a self-made construction company owner) — they respond to vulnerability signals more than aggression. This is THE line for closing tough owners.
- **The double permission pause** ("Můžu se… můžu se vás na něco zeptat?") with verbal hesitation triggers Czech politeness reflex — they will say yes.
