# Body

## The Mask

[1:02]
> "Human beings — we all wear a mask. Your prospects, when you first start talking to them, have this mask on. Most salespeople and companies are stuck selling to the damn mask. So when you ask questions, they give you vague, generalized, surface-level answers."

> "What I'm going to show you is how you get that person willingly to want to take off the mask, where you're actually selling to the real human — not the masked person."

## Why we have selling-anxiety (and the mask comes back)

[2:04]
> "If we have a belief system that selling is something bad, manipulating, doing TO them — how well will we ever be at selling? Not very. Because that belief system holds us back. When we get to close, internally we get nervous, anxious. That comes out in body language and tone. And then the prospect across from you feels that — and the mask comes back on."

## Behavioral patterns

[3:37]
> "Have you ever talked to a salesperson where you had a massive problem, you knew if you bought it would solve your problems — yet you still didn't do it?"

[3:37]
> "That's called a behavioral pattern. So if WE have patterns that prevent us from doing something we know we should do — by default our PROSPECTS also have similar patterns."

> "Once we recognize these patterns and learn how to label them — it helps us overcome them. Otherwise we just go through life hoping something we say will magically cause them to want to buy."

## Brain wasn't designed to thrive — only to survive

[4:39]
> "The brain wasn't designed for us to thrive — only to keep us safe. To survive. So anytime your prospect has an opportunity to move forward, the mind sees it as a threat — something new, something unknown. The brain freezes up and the mask comes back on."

## Non-committal language

[5:09] **Write this down. Non-committal languaging.**

> "Recognize patterns by the words they say to you, the tone, the body language. Once you learn that, it becomes really easy to know where to go in a conversation."

[16:30]
> "If I ask a really good question that I really needed an answer to, and the prospect just doesn't answer it or waffles — and I go to the next question without finding out what happened — I just bought into their story."

> "If I keep them in non-committal languaging throughout the entire conversation — what's the likelihood they'll commit at the end? Unlikely."

## Pushing things down the road

[6:42]
> "Have you ever had a prospect say 'one day I'm going to do it'? 'It's better timing next month.' You call next month — 'oh, summer's coming up, better in the fall.' You call in the fall — 'family in town now.' Why does this happen? Because the mind feels safer by pushing things down the road."

## Sigmund Freud — "People do who they believe they are"

[13:25]
> "Two most important people that influenced my way of thinking in sales were Sigmund Freud and Carl Jung. Freud said: **people do who they believe they are.** We make decisions based on the identity of who we believe we are."

## The Alcoholics-Anonymous identity trap

[14:57]
> "AA success rate is ~5–10%. Why? Because you're sitting in a group every day taught to say 'I'm an alcoholic. I'm an alcoholic. I've been sober for 30 years. I'm an alcoholic.'"

> "What identity do you believe you are? An alcoholic. And what decisions do alcoholics typically make? Bad decisions."

> "If I believe I'm an alcoholic, it's going to be hard for me not to make decisions that alcoholics make."

## What you're concerned ABOUT (not concerned AT)

[17:31]
> "They have to feel like you care for them — like you're concerned about the consequences if they don't change. But there's a fine line — you can't come across like you're judging them."

> **"I'm not concerned that you don't have the money. I'm concerned that you're not being resourceful to find the funding so you can get what you want."**

## "Did they plan that out?"

[11:50]
> "Did the prospect plan that 'I always talk to my spouse' / 'I need 6 quotes' objection out before you started talking to them? No. So there's something going on FROM THE MOMENT you say a word TO THE MOMENT you try to close that's preventing them from moving forward. If I deconstruct all that and rebuild — selling becomes way easier."

## Key takeaways / CZ kontext

- **The Mask concept** = perfect CZ mental model. *"Klient si nasadí masku, jakmile cítí, že mu chceš něco prodat. Tvůj job — masku sundat."*
- **"I'm not concerned about money — concerned about resourcefulness"** = single most powerful reframe when prospect says "nemám peníze." CZ: *"Mě netrápí, že nemáš teď peníze. Mě trápí, že nehledáš způsob, jak je sehnat, abys mohl vyřešit ten problém, co tě fakt štve."*
- **People do who they believe they are** — Freud-Jung core. Reframe your pitch: "Jsi typ, co odkládá rozhodnutí — nebo typ, co dělá, když ví, že to dává smysl?"
- **Non-committal language** in CZ to watch for: "to si rozmyslím", "možná", "asi", "kdyby", "nějak", "uvidím". Tyhle slova = mask je nasazená.
- **Behavioral patterns** — pokud sama máš pattern "raději neinvestovat", tvoji klienti budou mít stejný pattern. Pracovat na svém prvně.
