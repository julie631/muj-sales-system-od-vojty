# Body

## Cause 1 — Predictable questions

[0:31]
> "These are predictable questions triggering them to shut down emotionally — fight-or-flight. Other salespeople have asked similar questions before, so their brain pattern-matches."

**Trash questions:**
- "What are some of your challenges?"
- "What do you feel like you need?"
- "What problems are you experiencing?"

**Reframe (pattern interrupt):**

> "Help me understand — you've been with vendor X for 5 years. They're fairly decent. What's caused you to feel like you might want to look at someone else?" *(verbal pacing)*

[1:32]
> "If I said it monotone — 'so help me understand you've been with vendor X 5 years, what's caused you to want to look at someone else?' — 'I don't know, we're just always looking around.' Same words. It's HOW I say it."

Auto example:
> "So help me understand. You've got the Cadillac. I mean, that's a fairly decent car. What's caused you to feel like you might want something different?"

Cyber security example (if "fairly decent" already used):
> "Is your management team 100% in love with the way your fraud stack is set up?"

[2:03]
> "Nobody's 100% in love with anything. There's always something they're not satisfied with."

## Cause 2 — Wrong tonality (the HILA antidote)

[2:34]
> "Don't come across monotone, too excited, or timid/nervous. Come across **detached**. Not that I don't care — but I'm not the one with the problem."

> "**HILA — High Intent, Low Attachment.** Who has the problems? The prospect — not you. Who has the solution? You do. I'm concerned for the consequences if you don't do anything, but I'm not ATTACHED because it's not my problem."

### Off-the-record concern tone

> "Can I — can I ask you something?" *(not the first question — maybe ¾ in)*
> Prospect: "Sure, Jeremy, go ahead."
> "And this is, you know, it's between you and I. You know, it's off the record. What's really holding you back from moving forward?"

## Cause 3 — Listen to what they MEAN, not just what they say

[3:05]
> "You haven't learned yet how to listen to what the prospect MEANS — not just what they SAY."

Mid-close:
> "Do you feel like this could be the answer for you?"
> Prospect: "Yeah… yeah… yeah." *(uncertain)*

Do you address the hesitation or hope it goes away?

> "Hey, you seem a bit hesitant when I asked you that question. *(relaxed, low-pressure tone)*"

> "Am I calm? Am I still certain? Am I confident? **Loud doesn't necessarily mean persuasive.** I'm still assertive — but more like an expert is."

### Boardroom body-language example

[4:07]
> "I'm in person, in a boardroom, 8 decision makers. I go through slide 12 and I see one of them cross her arms, look up. That's a non-verbal signal. Tonality and facial expressions."

> "Do uncertain human beings make buying decisions? Unlikely. I want to get her certain."

> "Hey, Mary — you seem a bit hesitant when I went through that last slide. What's um what's behind that? Just so I understand. *(concerned tone, empathy)*"

[5:08]
> "If I came across aggressive — 'Hey John, you seemed a bit hesitant when I went through that last slide. Like, what's coming up for you man?' — they'd get defensive."

## Understand them without buying into their story

[5:08]
> "Write this down. You understand them WITHOUT buying into their story. If they do not buy, I offered them no value — I wasted their time. So I'm not going to buy their story. However, I cannot come across like I'm judging them."

> "What's coming up for you? Just so I understand. *(concerned, empathetic)* Well, it's nothing horrible. *(soft)* Well, you can tell me. It's okay."

## Key takeaways / CZ kontext

- **HILA mindset** = single biggest mental upgrade. *"Vysoký záměr, nízká připoutanost — já chci, aby kupili, ale není to MŮJ problém, je to JEJICH problém. Já mám řešení."*
- **Predictable question reframes** in CZ: *"Jaké jsou tvoje největší výzvy?"* → *"Co tě vede k pocitu, že to nemusí být to nejlepší řešení, co máte?"*
- **"You seem a bit hesitant — what's behind that?"** in CZ: *"Vidím, že trošku váháš — co je za tím? Jen abych chápala."* Disarming Czech equivalent.
- **"Loud doesn't mean persuasive"** — tendence být v hovorech enthusiastic může být kontraproduktivní. Detached posture, expert tone, low volume on key points.
- **Read body language** on Zoom — even on small video, watch the arms-crossing, look-up, eye-drift. Address it.
- **"Understand without buying"** — pokud defaultně přijímáš výmluvy, CZ shift: *"Chápu tě, ale věřím, že to nesouvisí s tím skutečným důvodem. Co je za tím opravdu?"*
